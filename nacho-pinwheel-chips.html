Nacho Pinwheel Chips

Crispy baked tortilla pinwheels filled with seasoned ground beef and baked over shredded cheese.

Source: Josh Elkin from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

Varies

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Taco seasoning
  • Ground beef
  • Flour tortillas
  • Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Shredded white cheese

Instructions

  1. Combine taco seasoning with ground beef.
  2. Spread the mixture over flour tortillas.
  3. Flatten the meat using parchment paper and a tortilla press.
  4. Roll each tortilla tightly into a cylinder.
  5. Slice the rolled tortilla into pinwheel-shaped rounds.
  6. Place shredded cheese on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  7. Place pinwheel slices on top of the cheese piles.
  8. Sprinkle additional cheese over the pinwheels.
  9. Bake until the pinwheels are crispy.

WW Notes

This is likely higher in points because of tortillas, beef, and cheese. For a lighter version, use 96/4 beef or ground turkey, low-carb tortillas, and reduced-fat cheese.
