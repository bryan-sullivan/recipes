Healthy Rice Paper Dumpling Rolls

Pan-crisped rice paper rolls filled with lean ground chicken, shrimp, vegetables, ginger, garlic, cilantro, soy sauce, and sesame oil.

Source: Eating Healthy Today from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

16 to 18 rolls

Ingredients

Filling

  • 1 lb 96% lean ground chicken
  • 8 oz shrimp, peeled, deveined, and diced small
  • 1/2 cup red cabbage, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup carrot, finely chopped
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fresh ginger, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp fresh cilantro, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 egg

Sauce

  • 1/4 cup warm water
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 tbsp carrot, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh cilantro, finely chopped

Dumplings

  • 16 to 18 sheets rice paper
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil, plus more as needed

Instructions

  1. Combine all filling ingredients in a large bowl and mix well.
  2. Mix all sauce ingredients together until the sugar dissolves. Set aside.
  3. Fill a pie dish with warm water and line a cutting board with damp paper towels.
  4. Soak one sheet of rice paper for 10 to 15 seconds, then place it on the board.
  5. Add about 3 tbsp of filling to the bottom third and spread into a rectangle.
  6. Fold the bottom edge up over the filling, roll once more to enclose, fold in the sides, then continue rolling until sealed.
  7. Repeat with remaining rice paper and filling.
  8. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  9. Cook rolls in batches, leaving space between each, for 5 to 7 minutes until golden on the first side.
  10. Flip and cook 2 to 4 minutes more until golden and the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  11. Add more oil as needed.
  12. Serve with sauce spooned on top.

WW Notes

Contains shrimp. Main point drivers are rice paper, sesame oil, neutral oil, sugar, and shrimp/chicken depending on WW settings. For lower points, use cooking spray or less oil and keep the sauce light.
