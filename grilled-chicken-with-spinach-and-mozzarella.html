Grilled Chicken with Spinach and Mozzarella

Serves: 6 · Italian-inspired

Thin grilled chicken cutlets topped with garlicky spinach, roasted red peppers and melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • 24 ounces chicken breast, sliced lengthwise into 6 thin cutlets
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 10 ounces frozen spinach, thawed and thoroughly drained
  • 3 ounces shredded part-skim mozzarella
  • ½ cup roasted red peppers packed in water, sliced
  • Cooking spray or neutral oil

Instructions

Prep

  1. Squeeze as much moisture as possible from the spinach.
  2. Sauté the garlic briefly in olive oil.
  3. Add the spinach, salt and pepper. Cook until hot and relatively dry.
  4. Have the spinach, peppers and cheese ready beside the grill.

Grill

  1. Set the Kamado Joe for direct medium-high heat.
  2. Season the chicken and lightly oil the grate.
  3. Grill the thin cutlets for approximately 2–3 minutes per side, adjusting for thickness, until they reach 165°F.
  4. Top each cutlet with spinach, roasted peppers and ½ ounce mozzarella.
  5. Close the dome for 1–2 minutes, just until the cheese melts.

Notes

Serving ideas

  • Grilled zucchini
  • Arugula with lemon
  • A small serving of whole-wheat pasta with tomato sauce

Nutrition

Approximately 195 calories and 31 grams protein per topped chicken cutlet, before side dishes.
