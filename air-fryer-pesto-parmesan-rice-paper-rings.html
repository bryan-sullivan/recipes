Air Fryer Pesto Parmesan Rice Paper Rings

Air-fried rice paper rings coated with egg, pesto-style paste, grated cheese, sesame seeds, oil spray, and herbs.

Source: Izabella Jakubec from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

4 servings

Ingredients

  • Rice paper
  • Eggs, whisked
  • Pesto or reddish-brown paste
  • Grated Parmesan or similar cheese
  • Sesame seeds
  • Oil spray
  • Chopped herbs
  • White sauce, for dipping

Instructions

  1. Dip rice paper into whisked eggs.
  2. Place egg-coated rice paper on a cutting board.
  3. Spread pesto or reddish-brown paste onto the rice paper.
  4. Grate cheese over the rice paper.
  5. Roll the rice paper into a tight log.
  6. Curl the log into a ring shape, joining the ends.
  7. Place rings in the air fryer basket and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  8. Spray with oil.
  9. Air fry until cooked through and crisp.
  10. Remove from the air fryer and sprinkle with chopped herbs.
  11. Dip in white sauce before eating.
