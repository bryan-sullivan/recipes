Browse and cook straight from the page, or copy a recipe’s link into the WeightWatchers app to import it with points.

Importing into WeightWatchers

  1. Tap Copy import link on any recipe below (or just copy the recipe page’s URL from your browser).
  2. In the WW app, go to Recipes → Create → Import from web.
  3. Paste the link. The name, ingredients, and steps come in automatically; adjust servings if you like, and WW calculates the points.
