Crispy Sushi Tacos With Spicy Tuna

Crispy nori sushi tacos filled with seasoned sushi rice, spicy tuna, cream cheese, cucumber, avocado, and toppings.

Source: Nourishing from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

6 to 7 sushi tacos

Ingredients

Shells

  • 6 to 7 nori sheets
  • Cream cheese
  • Olive oil, for frying

The Rice

  • 1 cup sushi rice, uncooked, about 200 g
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp soy sauce

The Batter

  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • Panko breadcrumbs

The Spicy Tuna

  • 2 cans tuna
  • 1 red onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp mayo
  • 2 tbsp sweet chili sauce
  • 2 tbsp Sriracha
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • 1 tsp chili powder

Toppings

  • Cucumber
  • Avocado
  • Green onions
  • Spicy mayo
  • Teriyaki sauce
  • Fried onions
  • Sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Cook the sushi rice according to package directions.
  2. Mix the cooked sushi rice with rice vinegar, sesame oil, and soy sauce. Let cool.
  3. Cut nori sheets into circles using a bowl as a guide.
  4. Spread about 1 tablespoon of rice onto each nori round.
  5. Whisk water, cornstarch, and flour into a paste.
  6. Brush the paste over the rice side, then press firmly into panko breadcrumbs.
  7. Fry the rounds in a thin layer of hot olive oil for 1 to 2 minutes, until golden.
  8. Gently fold the fried rounds into a taco shape while warm.
  9. Mix tuna, red onion, mayo, sweet chili sauce, Sriracha, soy sauce, lemon juice, and chili powder.
  10. Spread cream cheese inside the shells, then add cucumber, avocado, and spicy tuna.
  11. Top with green onions, spicy mayo, teriyaki sauce, fried onions, and sesame seeds.

WW Notes

The biggest point drivers are the sushi rice, sesame oil, frying oil, mayo, sweet chili sauce, cream cheese, avocado, spicy mayo, teriyaki sauce, fried onions, flour, cornstarch, and panko. For a lighter version, use light mayo, reduced-fat cream cheese, less avocado, less sweet chili, and air fry or griddle the shells with cooking spray instead of frying in oil.
