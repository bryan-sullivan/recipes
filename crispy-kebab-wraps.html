Crispy Kebab Wraps

Thin ground-meat tortilla rolls cut into rounds, skewered, baked, broiled, and finished with lemon juice and herbs.

Source: Ben DeCocker from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

Varies

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Seasoned ground meat
  • Large flour tortillas
  • Fresh lemon juice
  • Chopped fresh herbs

Instructions

  1. Spread a thin, even layer of seasoned ground meat onto a large flour tortilla.
  2. Tightly roll up the tortilla with the meat into a cylinder.
  3. Cut the rolled tortilla into several small, equal-sized rounds.
  4. Place the sliced rolls onto wooden skewers.
  5. Arrange skewers on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  6. Bake the skewers, flip them, bake again, and finish with broiling to achieve a golden-brown texture.
  7. Garnish with chopped fresh herbs and squeeze fresh lemon juice over the top before serving.

WW Notes

Points depend on the meat, tortillas, and portion size. To make it lighter, use lean ground chicken, turkey, or 96/4 beef, and use low-carb tortillas.
