Everything Bagel Protein Biscuits

Protein-style biscuits made with eggs, cottage cheese, mozzarella, Parmesan, everything bagel seasoning, and almond flour or regular flour.

Source: Jasmine Hyare from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

8 biscuits

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 3/4 cup cottage cheese
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp everything bagel seasoning
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan
  • 2 cups almond flour OR 1 3/4 cups regular flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Additional everything bagel seasoning, for topping

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Whisk eggs.
  3. Add cottage cheese, mozzarella, Parmesan, and everything bagel seasoning. Mix well.
  4. In a separate bowl, combine almond flour or regular flour, baking powder, and salt.
  5. Gently mix the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients.
  6. Allow the mixture to sit for 10 to 15 minutes for maximum fluffiness.
  7. Scoop 1/4 cup portions onto the baking sheet and gently form into rounded biscuit shapes.
  8. Top with more everything bagel seasoning.
  9. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes, until lightly browned.

WW Notes

Almond flour is calorie-dense and may be high points. For WW, the regular flour version may import more predictably. Reduced-fat mozzarella and low-fat cottage cheese can lower points.
