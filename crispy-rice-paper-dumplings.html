Crispy Rice Paper Dumplings

Air-fried rice paper dumplings filled with shrimp, green onions, garlic, ginger, and soy sauce.

Source: Peter Martin from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 350 g shrimp
  • 6 sheets rice paper
  • 1 bunch green onions
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 5 g ginger
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce

Instructions

  1. Mix shrimp, green onions, garlic, ginger, salt, pepper, and soy sauce together in a bowl.
  2. Lay the mixture on wet rice paper and top with another sheet.
  3. Cut into 4 pieces and wrap the rice paper around the shrimp filling.
  4. Air fry for 8 minutes at 400°F, until cooked through and crispy.

WW Notes

Contains shrimp. The main point drivers are rice paper and any dipping sauce added later. This should be relatively light if cooked without added oil.
