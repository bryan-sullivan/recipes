Viral Beef Tortilla Kabobs

Grilled tortilla kabobs layered with seasoned beef, cheese, breadcrumbs, peppers, garlic, parsley, BBQ rub, and Japanese barbecue sauce.

Source: Nick Nesgoda from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

9 kabobs

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 2 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 4 oz shredded cheese
  • 1/3 cup bread crumbs
  • 5 cloves minced garlic
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/4 cup parsley
  • 3 tbsp BBQ rub
  • 1/3 cup Bachan's Original Japanese Barbecue Sauce
  • 6 XL grande burrito tortillas
  • Sesame seeds, for garnish
  • Green onions, for garnish

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients except tortillas and garnishes. Mix thoroughly.
  2. Stack 6 tortillas and cut into a large square, removing rounded edges.
  3. Divide beef mixture into 5 servings. Spread each serving on 5 tortillas, covering fully.
  4. Stack beef-covered tortillas and add the last tortilla on top.
  5. Cut the stack into three even sections. Insert three skewers in each section and cut between them.
  6. Grill kabobs over direct heat for 2 to 3 minutes per side.
  7. Move kabobs away from direct heat and cook for 10 more minutes, until they reach an internal temperature of 155 F.
  8. Garnish with sesame seeds, green onions, and a generous drizzle of Bachan's Original Japanese Barbecue Sauce.

WW Notes

This is higher in points as written due to 80/20 beef, large tortillas, cheese, breadcrumbs, and sauce. For a lighter version, use 93/7 or 96/4 beef, smaller tortillas, less cheese, and a lighter sauce drizzle.
