Blackstone Gochujang Pork Tenderloin Bowls

Quick-cooking pork tenderloin caramelized in a savory-sweet gochujang glaze with rice, crunchy cabbage and quick-pickled cucumber.

Ingredients

Pork

  • 1½–1¾ pounds pork tenderloin, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons gochujang
  • 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger

Bowls

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 4 scallions, sliced
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • Sesame seeds, optional

Quick cucumber dressing

  • 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon low-sodium soy sauce
  • ½ teaspoon toasted sesame oil

Tip
Slice the tenderloin across the grain while it is very cold; the firmer meat is easier to cut thinly.

Method

  1. Mix the gochujang, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, sesame oil, garlic and ginger. Toss with the pork and marinate for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Combine the cucumber with its dressing and set aside.
  3. Heat the Blackstone to medium-high or high.
  4. Cook the pork in a thin layer, working in batches if needed. Let the edges caramelize before tossing; thin slices should cook through in 3–5 minutes.
  5. Build each bowl with ½ cup cooked rice, cabbage, carrots, dressed cucumber, pork and scallions. Finish with sesame seeds if desired.

Plan note
Pork tenderloin stays lean and tender when sliced thinly and cooked fast over high heat.

Nutrition

Approximately 475–525 calories and 40–45 grams protein per serving. For fewer calories, use ⅓ cup cooked rice per bowl and add more cabbage.
