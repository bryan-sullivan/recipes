Joetisserie Peruvian-Style Chicken with Green Sauce & Roasted Potatoes

A deeply seasoned whole chicken spinning over charcoal with crisp roasted potatoes and a bright, creamy cilantro-jalapeño sauce.

Ingredients

Peruvian-style chicken

  • 1 whole chicken, 4–5 pounds, giblets removed
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Juice and zest of 1 lime
  • 5 cloves garlic, finely grated
  • 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

Roasted potatoes

  • 1½ pounds baby Yukon Gold potatoes, halved
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Light green sauce

  • ¾ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 packed cup cilantro leaves and tender stems
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded for less heat
  • 1 scallion, roughly chopped
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt

Balance before lighting
Secure the chicken tightly between the forks and rotate the spit by hand before mounting it. Reposition the forks until the bird turns without a heavy side dropping.

Method

  1. Pat the chicken very dry. Mix the olive oil, lime zest and juice, garlic, paprika, cumin, oregano, salt and pepper. Rub the mixture over the chicken and under the breast skin where practical. Refrigerate uncovered for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  2. Remove the chicken from the refrigerator 30 minutes before cooking. Tuck the wing tips, tie the legs securely and mount and balance the bird on the Joetisserie spit.
  3. Bank a moderate charcoal fire toward the back of the Kamado Joe and preheat with the Joetisserie ring installed to 350–375°F. Place a shallow drip pan beneath the chicken, leaving airflow around it.
  4. Toss the potatoes with olive oil, salt and pepper. Spread them in a grill-safe pan or perforated basket and place them on the cooking grate beside the spit once the chicken has cooked for 30 minutes.
  5. Start the motor and cook the chicken for about 75–105 minutes total. Rotate or stir the potatoes every 20 minutes. If the skin darkens too quickly, reduce the airflow or shield the hottest area with foil.
  6. Begin checking early. Remove the chicken when the breast reaches 160–165°F and the thigh reaches 175°F without touching bone. Cook the potatoes until browned and tender; move them over more direct heat for the final few minutes if needed.
  7. Blend the yogurt, cilantro, jalapeño, scallion, garlic, lime juice, olive oil, cumin and salt until smooth. Refrigerate until serving.
  8. Rest the chicken for 15 minutes before carving. Remove the skin for the leanest serving, then plate with the potatoes and green sauce.

Temperature beats the clock
Whole birds vary in shape and the Joetisserie runs differently with each charcoal load. Use breast and thigh temperatures—not elapsed time—as the finish line.

Nutrition

Approximately 525 calories and 48 grams protein per serving. The estimate assumes the chicken skin is not eaten, a 6-ounce portion of cooked meat, one-quarter of the potatoes and one-quarter of the sauce.
