Kamado Indian Yogurt Chicken Kebabs with Basmati & Cucumber-Mint Raita

Lightly smoky yogurt-spice chicken breast kebabs with fluffy rice and cooling raita.

Serves 4 · Kamado Joe Jr. + stovetop · About 40 minutes plus marinating. Ingredient meat weights are raw, and rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, in 1½-inch chunks
  • 1¼ cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt, divided
  • 1 English cucumber, grated and squeezed
  • ¾ cup dry basmati rice
  • 1 lemon
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp Kashmiri chili powder (optional)
  • 2 tbsp fresh mint, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. At least 1 hour ahead, marinate chicken in ¾ cup yogurt, half the lemon juice, garlic, ginger, garam masala, cumin, coriander, chili, oil and salt. Refrigerate; discard used marinade.
  2. Heat Kamado Joe Jr. to 400–425°F; wait for clean, nearly invisible smoke. Thread chicken on skewers with small gaps.
  3. Grill skewers about 10–15 minutes, turning every 3–4 minutes, until all pieces reach 165°F.
  4. Meanwhile cook basmati rice according to package directions. Mix remaining ½ cup yogurt with grated drained cucumber, chopped mint and remaining lemon juice; season to taste.
  5. Serve skewers immediately with rice and raita.

Start marinating before class or overnight. If charcoal produces heavy white smoke, use oven broiler instead.
