Weekly prep

  • Shop for four servings at each of seven dinners. Optional naan or extra pasta/rice is additional; check the pantry before buying sauces and spices. No slaw this week.
  • Check sausage, BBQ sauce, pickles, ketchup and mayonnaise for mustard; use simple individual burger condiments. No turkey burger substitution or salmon.
  • If shopping Tuesday September 29, refrigerate Wednesday’s chicken and Thursday’s pork. Freeze Saturday, Sunday and Tuesday chicken and Monday beef promptly in meal-size packages. Refrigerated raw poultry and ground beef should be cooked or frozen within 1–2 days.
  • Move Saturday chicken to the refrigerator Friday morning; Sunday thighs Saturday morning; Monday beef Sunday morning; Tuesday chicken Sunday night or Monday morning. Allow 24–48 hours for larger or tightly packed portions; thaw in the refrigerator.
  • Thursday evening: chop Friday’s peppers and onion. Store cut potatoes in cold water in the refrigerator; drain and dry before roasting. Keep raw meat separate from vegetables.
  • Monday evening: marinate the thawed Tuesday chicken in the refrigerator for up to 12 hours, or marinate Tuesday morning. Make the sauce earlier Tuesday if useful.
  • Refrigerate cooked leftovers promptly in shallow containers; use within 3–4 days and reheat to 165°F. Keep soup pasta separate when saving portions.
