Weekly prep

  • Shop for four servings at each of seven dinners. Optional naan or extra pasta/rice is additional; check the pantry before buying sauces and spices. No slaw this week.
  • Check sausage, BBQ sauce, pickles, ketchup and mayonnaise for mustard; use simple individual burger condiments. No turkey burger substitution or salmon.
  • If shopping Sunday September 27, refrigerate Monday’s chicken and Tuesday’s pork. Freeze Thursday, Friday and Sunday chicken and Saturday beef promptly in meal-size packages. Refrigerated raw poultry and ground beef should be cooked or frozen within 1–2 days.
  • Move Thursday chicken to the refrigerator Wednesday morning; Friday thighs Thursday morning; Saturday beef Friday morning; Sunday chicken Friday night or Saturday morning. Allow 24–48 hours for larger or tightly packed portions; thaw in the refrigerator.
  • Tuesday evening: chop Wednesday’s peppers and onion. Store cut potatoes in cold water in the refrigerator; drain and dry before roasting. Keep raw meat separate from vegetables.
  • Saturday evening: marinate the thawed Sunday chicken in the refrigerator for up to 12 hours, or marinate Sunday morning. Make the sauce earlier Sunday if useful.
  • Refrigerate cooked leftovers promptly in shallow containers; use within 3–4 days and reheat to 165°F. Keep soup pasta separate when saving portions.
