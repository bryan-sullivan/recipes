Sweet and Salty London Broil

Ingredients

Beef

  • 2½–3 pounds London broil
  • 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar or honey
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 1 teaspoon sambal or chili-garlic sauce, optional
  • Black pepper

Cucumber-herb salad

  • 2 English cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 1 shallot, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro
  • ¼ cup torn mint
  • 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon honey or sugar
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt

Serve with

  • Jasmine rice
  • Lime wedges
  • Sliced scallions

Method

  1. Combine the marinade and refrigerate the beef in it for 4–12 hours.
  2. Set the Classic II for direct cooking at 450–500°F.
  3. Remove excess marinade and pat the beef dry.
  4. Grill, turning for even browning, until the center reaches at least 145°F.
  5. Rest 10 minutes, then slice very thinly across the grain.
  6. Toss the cucumber salad immediately before serving.
  7. Refrigerate enough cooked steak for Steak Green Bean and Potato Salad.
