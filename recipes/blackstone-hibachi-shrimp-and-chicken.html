Blackstone Hibachi Shrimp and Chicken

Two protein options cooked separately.

Ingredients

Proteins

  • 12 ounces peeled and deveined shrimp
  • 1¼ pounds chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 2 teaspoons neutral high-heat oil
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Hibachi sauce

  • ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch, optional

Vegetables and rice

  • 2 zucchini, cut into half-moons
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 yellow onion, cut into chunks
  • 1 pound sliced mushrooms
  • 1 pound broccoli florets, lightly steamed
  • 1½ pounds cauliflower rice
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 3 scallions, sliced

Mustard-free yogurt sauce

  • ½ cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons light mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon ketchup
  • 1 teaspoon rice vinegar
  • Sriracha and water to taste

Method

  1. Whisk the hibachi sauce and mix the yogurt sauce separately.
  2. Keep raw chicken and shrimp on separate trays with separate utensils.
  3. Cook the chicken first and take it to 165°F. Move it to a clean warming area.
  4. Brown the vegetables; add a little hibachi sauce near the end.
  5. Cook the cauliflower rice until dry, then add peas, carrots, scallions, soy sauce and sesame oil.
  6. Cook shrimp last in a clean zone, about 1–2 minutes per side, until opaque and 145°F.
  7. Sauce the proteins separately.

Warning
For a shellfish allergy rather than a preference, use separate tools and cooking surfaces. Different zones on one griddle may not prevent cross-contact.
