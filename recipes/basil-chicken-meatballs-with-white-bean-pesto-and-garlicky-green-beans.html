Basil Chicken Meatballs with White Bean Pesto & Garlicky Green Beans

Tender basil chicken meatballs over a creamy white-bean pesto, with blistered roasted-garlic green beans on the side.

Ingredients

Chicken meatballs

  • 1½ pounds ground chicken
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ cup panko
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil
  • 2 cloves roasted garlic, mashed
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon Italian seasoning

White bean basil pesto

  • 1 can cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 packed cup fresh basil
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 cloves roasted garlic
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ¼–½ cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste

Green beans

  • 1 pound green beans, trimmed
  • 2 cloves roasted garlic
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Kosher salt, black pepper and red pepper flakes, to taste

Tip
Mix the chicken just until combined. Overworking it makes the meatballs dense.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Gently combine all meatball ingredients and form about 20 meatballs. Arrange in a single layer.
  3. Bake for 15–18 minutes, until browned and the centers reach 165°F.
  4. Meanwhile, blend the cannellini beans, basil, Parmesan, lemon juice, roasted garlic and olive oil. Add broth gradually until smooth and thick; season to taste and warm gently in a saucepan.
  5. Sauté or roast the green beans until blistered and tender-crisp. Toss with roasted garlic, salt, pepper and red pepper flakes.
  6. Spoon the warm white bean pesto onto plates, top with meatballs and serve the garlicky green beans alongside.

Plan note
Fresh basil is most perishable, so this meal intentionally uses it early in the week.

Nutrition

Approximately 475–525 calories and 45–50 grams protein per serving. Actual values vary with the ground chicken, Parmesan and amount of oil used.
