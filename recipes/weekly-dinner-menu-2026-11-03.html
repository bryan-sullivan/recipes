Prep strategy

  • Tuesday: crisp the chicken thighs while prepping orzo; use second oven for green beans. Verify chicken reaches 165°F.
  • Wednesday: use pre-cut chicken and peppers for a true easy one-pot meal. Stir light sour cream in off heat.
  • Thursday: buy frozen cod if shopping several days ahead; thaw in refrigerator Wednesday. Give potato wedges a head start.
  • Friday: clean Kamado smoke first; thin pork chops reach 145°F quickly, then rest at least 3 minutes.
  • Saturday: chop chimichurri and dice sweet potatoes ahead; roast Brussels sprouts while heating Blackstone.
