Wegmans Grocery List — November 16–21, 2026

Three double-size recipes, including broccoli, rice, fresh pico and raita. Weekly menu · Shared household list

Quantities below cover eight servings per recipe, not just four. Check pantry before buying spices. Black beans, extra broccoli oil, and some curry finishing ingredients are optional.

Produce

  • ☐ broccoli florets — 2 lb total (Mon+Tue, 1 lb per evening)
  • ☐ yellow onions — 3 medium total (1 lasagna + 2 curry)
  • ☐ red onions — 2 medium (Wed/Thu taco bowls and pico)
  • ☐ garlic — 2 large bulbs, at least 12 cloves (6 lasagna + 6 curry)
  • ☐ Roma tomatoes — 6 total (2 pico + 4 curry)
  • ☐ poblano peppers — 2 (Wed/Thu taco bowls)
  • ☐ cilantro — 1 generous bunch, about 1 cup chopped (pico, curry, raita)
  • ☐ fresh basil — 1 small bunch, at least ½ cup chopped (lasagna)
  • ☐ fresh parsley — 1 small bunch, at least ¼ cup chopped (lasagna)
  • ☐ fresh ginger — 1 piece, enough for 2 tbsp freshly grated (curry)
  • ☐ limes — 2 (fresh pico, tacos)
  • ☐ large cucumbers — 2 (Friday+Saturday raita)
  • ☐ lemon — 1, optional (curry finish)

Meat

  • ☐ 90/10 ground beef — 1½ lb (Mon+Tue lasagna)
  • ☐ boneless skinless chicken breast — 5½ lb total (2½ lb taco bowls; 3 lb curry)

Dairy

  • ☐ part-skim cottage cheese — 1½ cups (buy 16-oz tub; lasagna)
  • ☐ grated Parmesan — ½ cup (lasagna)
  • ☐ part-skim shredded mozzarella — 1½ cups (about 6 oz; lasagna)
  • ☐ reduced-fat shredded cheddar-jack — ½ cup (about 2 oz; taco bowls)
  • ☐ plain nonfat Greek yogurt — 2 cups total (½ cup taco bowls optional + ½ cup curry + 1 cup raita)

Canned and jarred

  • ☐ crushed tomatoes — 5 cups (buy two 28-oz cans; some extra; lasagna)
  • ☐ low-sodium chicken bone broth — 5 cups (buy two 32-fl-oz cartons; extra; lasagna)
  • ☐ corn kernels — 2 cups (frozen bag or canned, drained; taco bowls)
  • ☐ black beans — 2 x 15-oz cans, optional for Thursday variation

Bread, pasta and rice

  • ☐ dry short pasta — 16 oz (campanelle/bowties/broken lasagna, Mon+Tue)
  • ☐ ready-to-heat brown rice — 6 cups cooked total (about three microwave pouches; check yield, or 2 cups dry)
  • ☐ dry basmati rice — 2 cups (about 6 cups cooked, Fri+Sat)

Pantry check

  • ☐ olive or neutral oil — ~5 tbsp total + 2 tsp optional broccoli (lasagna, bowls, curry)
  • ☐ kosher salt — ~4 tsp plus to taste (across three dishes)
  • ☐ black pepper — to taste
  • ☐ garlic powder — 2 tsp (taco seasoning)
  • ☐ ground cumin — 2½ tsp (taco seasoning 2 + raita ½)
  • ☐ chili powder — 1 tsp (taco seasoning)
  • ☐ paprika — 1 tsp taco seasoning + 1 tsp optional curry substitute
  • ☐ dried oregano — 1 tsp (taco seasoning)
  • ☐ ground coriander — 2 tsp (curry)
  • ☐ garam masala — 2 tsp (curry)
  • ☐ turmeric — ½ tsp (curry)
  • ☐ mild Kashmiri chili powder — 1 tsp curry, optional if using sweet paprika
  • ☐ bay leaves — 2 optional (curry)
  • ☐ cinnamon stick — 1 optional, about 2 inches (curry)
  • ☐ green cardamom pods — 4 optional (curry)
  • ☐ whole cloves — 4 optional (curry)
