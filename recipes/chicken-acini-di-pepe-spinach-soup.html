Chicken Soup with Acini di Pepe & Spinach + Crusty Bread

Your rating
Not rated

Back to the weekly menu · Obsidian note

About 45 minutes; Sunday also needs marinating time. Meat weights are raw unless specified; pasta and rice amounts are dry.

Ingredients

Soup and bread

  • 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onions — diced
  • 2 medium carrots — small dice
  • 2 stalks celery — small dice
  • 3 cloves garlic — minced
  • 6 cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 leaves bay leaves
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt — or to taste after checking broth
  • 4 oz dry acini di pepe
  • 5 oz baby spinach
  • 1 whole lemon — wedges
  • 4 oz crusty bread — four 1 oz slices

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large soup pot over medium heat. Cook onion, carrot and celery for 6–8 minutes, stirring; add garlic for the final 30 seconds.
  2. Add broth, chicken, bay leaves, thyme and pepper. Bring to a gentle simmer, partly cover and cook 15–25 minutes, until the thickest part of the chicken reaches 165°F. Remove chicken to a clean plate.
  3. Discard bay leaves. Add acini di pepe to the simmering broth and cook for the package time, usually 8–10 minutes. Stir frequently so it does not stick.
  4. Shred chicken and return it to the pot with spinach. Simmer 1–2 minutes until spinach wilts and the chicken is hot. Taste before adding salt; finish with a squeeze of lemon.
  5. Divide the soup equally among four bowls (roughly 2–2½ cups each, depending on evaporation), with one slice of bread each. Add hot water if you prefer a thinner broth.

For planned leftovers, cook pasta separately and add to each bowl so it does not absorb all the broth. The grocery quantity is unchanged.
