Crispy Baked Cod Fish & Chips with Oven Potato Wedges and Light Tartar Sauce

A family-friendly baked fish-and-chips dinner with golden panko cod, crisp oven fries and tangy mustard-free tartar.

Serves 4 · Double oven · About 45 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note · Shopping list

Ingredients

  • 2 lb skinless cod fillets, cut into generous pieces
  • 2 lb russet or Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 cup plain panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp light mayonnaise
  • ¼ cup finely chopped dill pickles
  • 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 lemon, divided (juice and wedges)
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, plus to taste
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Heat ovens to 450°F (or use two racks in one oven). Toss potato wedges with 1½ tbsp olive oil, half the paprika, half the garlic powder, salt and pepper; spread on a metal sheet pan. Roast 30–35 minutes, turning once.
  2. Meanwhile pat cod very dry. Set out flour in one dish, beaten eggs plus 1 tsp lemon juice in another, and panko mixed with remaining paprika and garlic powder in a third. No mustard.
  3. Dredge each cod piece lightly in flour, egg, then panko; lay on an oiled wire rack set over a baking sheet and mist/drizzle with remaining ½ tbsp oil.
  4. After potatoes have cooked about 18 minutes, bake cod at 450°F for 12–16 minutes until golden and fish reaches 145°F. Avoid overcooking.
  5. Mix Greek yogurt, light mayo, chopped pickles, fresh dill and 1 tbsp lemon juice. Serve fish, potato wedges, tartar sauce and remaining lemon wedges.

Use two oven racks or double ovens: the potatoes need a head start. This variation deliberately omits Dijon mustard from Skinnytaste's original. Fish should reach 145°F and flake easily.
