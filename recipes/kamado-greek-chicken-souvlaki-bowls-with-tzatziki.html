Kamado Greek Chicken Souvlaki Bowls with Tzatziki

Lemon-oregano chicken grilled hot and fast over charcoal, served with rice, a crunchy tomato-cucumber salad, feta and cool homemade tzatziki.

Ingredients

Chicken souvlaki

  • 1½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1½-inch pieces
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • Metal skewers or soaked bamboo skewers

Tzatziki

  • 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • ½ English cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt

Bowls

  • 2 cups cooked brown or jasmine rice, kept warm
  • 1½ English cucumbers, chopped
  • 1 pint grape tomatoes, halved
  • ½ small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • ½ cup crumbled reduced-fat feta

Marinade window
Marinate for 30 minutes to 4 hours. Longer exposure to lemon juice can make the outside of the chicken soft and chalky.

Method

  1. Combine the chicken, lemon zest and juice, olive oil, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper. Refrigerate for 30 minutes to 4 hours.
  2. For the tzatziki, stir together the yogurt, squeezed cucumber, lemon juice, dill, garlic and salt. Refrigerate until serving.
  3. Toss the chopped cucumber, tomatoes, red onion, parsley and lemon juice. Set aside while the grill heats.
  4. Set up the Kamado Joe for direct cooking at 425–450°F. Clean and lightly oil the grate.
  5. Thread the chicken onto skewers with a little space between pieces. Grill with the dome closed for 10–14 minutes, turning every 3–4 minutes, until browned and the thickest pieces reach 165°F.
  6. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes. Divide the rice among four bowls and add the salad, chicken, feta and tzatziki.

Kamado setup
Use a direct fire and keep the pieces uniform. On the Jr., cook in two batches if crowding would prevent browning.

Nutrition

Approximately 475 calories and 50 grams protein per serving. The estimate uses chicken breast, nonfat yogurt, ½ cup cooked rice and 2 tablespoons reduced-fat feta per person.
