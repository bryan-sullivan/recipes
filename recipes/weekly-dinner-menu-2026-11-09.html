Prep and timing

  • Monday: Pork to 145°F, rest at least 3 minutes. No hot honey or mustard; keep Sriracha optional.
  • Tuesday: Stuffing-mix turkey meatballs with egg noodles and roasted carrots. 165°F for ground turkey.
  • Wednesday: Fully cooked chicken sausage makes the sheet-pan dinner fast; pre-cut vegetables.
  • Thursday: Mild yogurt-cashew korma with chicken to 165°F; roast zucchini while rice cooks.
  • Friday: If frozen, begin 12 lb turkey refrigerator thawing at least Monday. Do an unlit rotisserie fit/balance check Thursday. Hold 325°F, no wood, and verify 165°F in breast, innermost thigh and wing. Green bean Instagram reel was inaccessible, so the linked garlic-Parmesan side is explicitly provisional.
  • Saturday: Prepare and refrigerate fried-rice rice Friday; reserve cooked turkey and heat leftovers to 165°F on Blackstone.
