Kamado BBQ Bacon Cheeseburger Stuffed Peppers

Smoky bell peppers packed with lean beef, vegetables, bacon, mustard-free barbecue sauce and cheddar for cheeseburger flavor without the bun.

Ingredients

Peppers and filling

  • 4 large bell peppers, any color
  • 1¼ pounds 90% lean ground beef
  • 4 slices center-cut bacon, cooked crisp and chopped
  • 1 small yellow onion, finely diced
  • 1 medium zucchini, finely diced
  • 2 cups cauliflower rice, thawed if frozen
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • ¾ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ⅓ cup mustard-free barbecue sauce, plus 2 tablespoons for brushing
  • 1 cup reduced-fat shredded sharp cheddar
  • Sliced scallions or dill pickles for serving, optional

Check the label
Many barbecue sauces contain mustard. Choose a mustard-free bottle and recheck the ingredient label if the brand or flavor changes.

Method

  1. Set up the Kamado Joe for indirect cooking at 375°F. Place a heat deflector below the cooking grate.
  2. Slice the peppers in half lengthwise through the stems. Remove the seeds and membranes. Arrange the halves cut-side up in a lightly oiled grill-safe pan.
  3. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the beef and onion for 5–7 minutes, breaking the beef into crumbles. Add the zucchini, cauliflower rice, garlic, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt and pepper; cook for 4 minutes more. Drain excess fat.
  4. Remove the skillet from the heat. Stir in the chopped bacon, ⅓ cup barbecue sauce and ½ cup cheddar.
  5. Divide the filling among the eight pepper halves. Cover the pan loosely with foil and cook on the Kamado for 25 minutes.
  6. Remove the foil, brush the tops with the remaining 2 tablespoons barbecue sauce and sprinkle with the remaining ½ cup cheddar. Cook uncovered for 12–18 minutes, until the peppers are tender, the filling reaches 165°F and the cheese is melted.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes. Serve two pepper halves per person, topped with scallions or chopped pickles if desired.

Make-ahead option
Cook and cool the filling up to one day ahead. Stuff the raw peppers just before grilling, then allow a few extra minutes for the chilled filling to heat through.

Nutrition

Approximately 455 calories and 40 grams protein per serving. The estimate assumes 90% lean beef, center-cut bacon, reduced-fat cheddar and two stuffed pepper halves per serving.
