Cherry-Balsamic Pork Tenderloin with Roasted Potatoes & Broccoli

Your rating
Not rated

Back to the weekly menu · Obsidian note

About 45 minutes; Sunday also needs marinating time. Meat weights are raw unless specified; pasta and rice amounts are dry.

Ingredients

Pork and pan sauce

  • 1½ lb pork tenderloin — trim silver skin
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
  • ¼ cup reduced-sugar cherry preserves
  • ¼ cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • ½ tsp dried thyme

Roasted sides

  • 1½ lb Yukon Gold or russet potatoes — ¾-inch pieces
  • 1½ lb broccoli florets
  • 3 tsp olive oil — 2 tsp potatoes, 1 tsp broccoli
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with 2 tsp oil and half the side seasonings; roast on a sheet pan 30–35 minutes, turning halfway.
  2. After the potatoes have roasted 10 minutes, add a separate pan of broccoli tossed with 1 tsp oil and remaining side seasonings. Roast 18–22 minutes.
  3. Season pork with garlic powder, salt and pepper. Heat 1 tsp oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat; brown pork on all sides, about 5 minutes total. Transfer skillet to the oven for roughly 12–18 minutes, checking early. Remove pork at 145°F and rest at least 3 minutes, preferably 5–10.
  4. Keep a towel on the hot skillet handle. On the stove over medium-low heat, add broth and balsamic; scrape up browned bits and simmer 3–5 minutes. Stir in preserves and thyme, then simmer 1–2 minutes to a spoonable sauce. Add a splash of water if too thick.
  5. Slice pork and divide with sauce, potatoes and broccoli among four plates.

While dinner cooks, slice Wednesday’s peppers and onion. Keep cut potatoes submerged in cold water in the refrigerator overnight, then drain and dry well.

Nutrition

Approximately 480 calories and 44 g protein per serving: one quarter of the complete dinner, including all listed sides and condiments. Household estimate from typical ingredient values; actual brands and cooked yields vary. Optional extra starches are excluded.
