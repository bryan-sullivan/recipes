Kamado Peruvian Chicken with Light Green Sauce & Charred Vegetables

Cumin-paprika chicken grilled over direct heat with a bright Greek-yogurt cilantro sauce and charred vegetables.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 2½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

Light green sauce

  • 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 packed cup cilantro
  • ¼ cup fresh basil
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded if desired
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt

Vegetables

  • 2 zucchini, cut lengthwise
  • 1 red bell pepper, quartered
  • 1 red onion, cut into thick wedges
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste

Tip
If using breasts and thighs together, place thicker pieces over the slightly cooler part of the direct-grilling zone.

Method

  1. Combine the chicken with lime juice, olive oil, garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, salt and pepper. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Set the Kamado for direct grilling at 425–450°F.
  3. Blend all green-sauce ingredients until smooth. Refrigerate until serving.
  4. Toss the vegetables with olive oil, salt and pepper.
  5. Grill the chicken until charred and cooked to 165°F: usually 5–7 minutes per side for breasts and 5–6 minutes per side for thighs, depending on thickness.
  6. Grill the vegetables alongside the chicken until charred and tender.
  7. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes, slice and serve with the chilled green sauce and charred vegetables.

Plan note
The yogurt-based sauce keeps the Peruvian-inspired flavor bright without a mayonnaise-heavy calorie load.

Nutrition

Approximately 400–475 calories and 45–50 grams protein per serving, depending on whether breast or thigh meat is used and whether the recipe is divided into four or six portions.
