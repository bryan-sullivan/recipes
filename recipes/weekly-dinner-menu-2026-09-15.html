Use pre-cut squash and trimmed sprouts. About 10 minutes of active prep, then let the oven do the work. Saturday uses 90/10 beef for the familiar smashburger flavor.

Weekly strategy and prep

  • Tuesday: make the saved pepper recipe. Check the fridge and pantry before shopping; its ingredients may already be on hand.
  • Tuesday evening: mix Wednesday’s seasonings and prep vegetables; store raw chicken separately.
  • Thursday: marinate the steak 1–4 hours in the refrigerator. Reserve clean marinade before raw meat touches it, then turn it into peanut dressing.
  • Friday: wilt spinach ahead, then roll and roast the pork. The oven is an easy alternative to the Kamado.
  • Saturday: mix slaw and sauce first, then cook beef tacos in batches. Keep jalapeño heat mild and offer extra at the table.
  • If shopping Tuesday, freeze Saturday’s ground beef and thaw it in the refrigerator Friday. Refrigerate raw meat promptly; keep raw poultry and ground beef only 1–2 days before cooking or freezing.
