Built around the week

Weekly strategy

  • Tuesday is truly one-pan: give the potatoes a head start, then add the chicken and green beans in stages.
  • Cook Wednesday’s rice ahead and mix the Korean BBQ sauce before heating the Blackstone.
  • Dice Thursday’s chicken and onion in advance; dinner then takes about 25 minutes in one pot.
  • Make Friday’s tzatziki on Thursday night. Marinate the souvlaki for 30 minutes to 4 hours on Friday.
  • Cook Saturday’s filling a day ahead if useful, but stuff the raw peppers shortly before grilling.
  • Season Sunday’s whole chicken Saturday evening and refrigerate uncovered for better skin and deeper flavor.

Prep timeline

Tuesday night

  • Cook 2 cups rice for Wednesday and cool it promptly in a shallow container.
  • Whisk the Korean BBQ sauce and refrigerate.

Thursday night

  • Make Friday’s tzatziki and chop the salad vegetables.
  • If Saturday will be busy, cook and chill the stuffed-pepper filling.

Saturday night

  • Season, truss and refrigerate Sunday’s chicken uncovered.
  • Blend the green sauce or prep its ingredients for Sunday.
