Prep strategy

  • Monday: roast potatoes alongside the pork.
  • Tuesday: chop peppers and onions before turning on the Blackstone.
  • Wednesday: use pre-trimmed beans and thin-cut chicken breasts.
  • Thursday: start the rice as the oven preheats.
  • Friday: marinate chicken early; grill only once charcoal smoke is clean.
  • Saturday: marinate the lamb ahead and parboil potatoes before heating Kamado.
