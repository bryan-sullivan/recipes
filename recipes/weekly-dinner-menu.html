Weekly Dinner Menu — November 16–21, 2026

Prep and reheating

  • Monday: Use a large Dutch oven or split the double-size lasagna into two skillets, with broccoli on the side.
  • Wednesday: Buy ready-to-heat brown rice, dice chicken and poblanos, and pack fresh pico separately for Thursday. Cooking two small skillet batches is easiest.
  • Friday: Make curry in a large pot, rice in a second pot, and cucumber raita cold; reserve four servings for Saturday.
  • Food safety: Chill all leftovers in shallow containers within two hours and reheat cooked meals and rice to 165°F. Keep yogurt, pico and raita refrigerated.
