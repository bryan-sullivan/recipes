Prep strategy

  • Tuesday: use the double oven for pork and vegetables; rest pork before slicing.

  • Thursday: prep thin vegetable slices while the oven warms; aim for 165°F chicken.
  • Friday: make raita as cauliflower roasts and rice cooks.
