Mediterranean Sheet-Pan Salmon

Ingredients

Vegetables

  • 1 zucchini, cut into thick rounds
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 small red onion, cut into chunks
  • 8 ounces Yukon Gold or white potato, thinly sliced
  • 2 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried parsley
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano

Salmon

  • 4 skinless salmon fillets, 6 ounces each
  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¼ teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¾ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ cup mixed pitted olives
  • Lemon wedges

Frozen vacuum-packed salmon

  1. Remove the frozen fillets from vacuum packaging before thawing, unless the label explicitly instructs otherwise.
  2. Thaw on a covered plate in the refrigerator. Place it on a low shelf to prevent drips onto other food.
  3. If a fillet is still icy near cooking time, seal it in a clean zip-top bag and submerge the bag in cold water for 15–30 minutes.
  4. Pat the fish very dry. A milk soak is optional and unnecessary when the salmon smells clean and mild.
  5. Discard salmon that smells sour, rancid or ammonia-like. A soak cannot make spoiled fish safe.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Toss the vegetables with their oil and seasonings. Roast for 20 minutes.
  3. Coat and season the salmon.
  4. Move the vegetables aside, add the salmon and scatter the olives over the pan.
  5. Roast 12–15 minutes, adjusting for thickness. Partially frozen fillets may need another 3–6 minutes.
  6. Cook the thickest portion to 145°F and serve with lemon.

Nutrition

Approximately 380 calories and 36.5 grams protein per serving.
