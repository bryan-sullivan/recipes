Honey-Ginger Pork Tenderloin with Grilled Plums

A glossy honey-ginger glaze gives lean pork enough intensity to stand up to smoke, charred broccoli and juicy grilled plums.

Ingredients

  • 1½ pounds pork tenderloin, silver skin removed
  • 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1½ tablespoons honey
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 4 firm-ripe plums, halved and pitted
  • 1½ pounds broccoli florets
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 2 scallions, thinly sliced

Method

  1. Whisk soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic and sesame oil. Reserve 2 tablespoons in a clean bowl; coat the pork with the rest for 30 minutes to 4 hours.
  2. Set the Kamado Joe for two-zone cooking at 425°F. Place a cast-iron griddle or grill basket over the direct side for the broccoli.
  3. Toss broccoli with olive oil, salt and pepper. Grill over direct heat until charred and crisp-tender, 8–12 minutes, then hold warm.
  4. Pat excess marinade from the pork. Sear over direct heat, turning to brown all sides, 6–8 minutes total. Move to the indirect side, close the dome and cook until 140–142°F.
  5. Rest the pork at least 5 minutes; carryover heat should bring it to the USDA-safe 145°F.
  6. Grill plums cut-side down over direct heat for 2–4 minutes, then turn briefly. Brush with the reserved clean glaze.
  7. Slice the pork and serve with plums and broccoli. Spoon over any remaining clean glaze and scatter with scallions.

Nutrition estimate
Approximately 440 calories and 37 g protein per serving, based on four servings. Brand choice and portion size will change the total.
