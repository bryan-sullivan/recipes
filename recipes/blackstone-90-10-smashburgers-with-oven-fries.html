Blackstone 90/10 Smashburgers with Oven Fries & Pickles

Your rating
Not rated

Back to the weekly menu · Obsidian note

About 45 minutes; Sunday also needs marinating time. Meat weights are raw unless specified; pasta and rice amounts are dry.

Ingredients

Burgers and toppings

  • 1½ lb 90/10 ground beef — eight loose 3 oz balls, kept cold
  • 4 slices reduced-fat American cheese
  • 4 buns hamburger buns — standard-size, roughly 140 calories each
  • 1 tsp neutral high-heat oil
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ cup dill pickle chips — choose without mustard
  • 1 medium tomatoes — sliced
  • 4 leaves lettuce
  • 4 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp mustard-free light mayonnaise — optional; verify ingredients

Oven fries

  • 1½ lb Yukon Gold or russet potatoes — ¼–⅜-inch sticks
  • 3 tsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Heat oven to 450°F. Rinse potato sticks and dry thoroughly. Toss with their oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Spread on a large rimmed sheet pan with space between pieces; roast 30–35 minutes, turning halfway.
  2. When fries have about 10 minutes left, preheat the Blackstone to medium-high, around 425–450°F at the surface. Keep a cooler area for buns. Set out cheese, toppings and clean serving plates.
  3. Wipe the hot cooking zone with 1 tsp oil. Add cold beef balls in batches; immediately smash each thin using a sturdy spatula or press and a small piece of parchment. Remove parchment and season with the burger salt and pepper.
  4. Cook about 2 minutes until the edges brown. Scrape firmly under each patty to keep the crust, flip and cook another 1–2 minutes. Do not press again. Check thin patties from the side with an instant-read thermometer; ground beef must reach 160°F.
  5. Place one cheese slice on each of four patties, stack with the remaining patties and briefly melt. Toast buns on the cooler zone. Build four double burgers with lettuce, tomato, pickles, 1 tbsp ketchup each and optional mayo divided across the four. Serve with one quarter of the fries.

The base serving is two 3 oz raw beef patties, one cheese slice and one bun. No mustard or Big Mac-style sauce. Check mayonnaise and pickle labels; omit mayo if a suitable version is unavailable.

Nutrition

Approximately 700 calories and 44 g protein per serving: one quarter of the complete dinner, including all listed sides and condiments. Household estimate from typical ingredient values; actual brands and cooked yields vary. Optional extra starches are excluded.
