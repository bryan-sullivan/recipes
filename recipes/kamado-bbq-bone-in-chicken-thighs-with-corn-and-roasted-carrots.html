Kamado BBQ Bone-In Chicken Thighs with Grilled Corn & Roasted Carrots

Your rating
Not rated

Bone-in thighs cooked indirectly with a light barbecue glaze; sweet corn and smoky carrots.

Serves 4 · Kamado Joe Classic II + oven · About 60 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 3½–4 lb total)
  • ½ cup BBQ sauce (prefer reduced sugar)
  • 4 ears corn, husked
  • 1½ lb carrots, peeled and cut into sticks
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4 tsp olive oil, divided
  • 2½ tsp smoked paprika, divided
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt, divided
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Light lump charcoal in the Kamado Joe Classic II. Set up heat deflectors for indirect heat and stabilize at 375–400°F. Wait until smoke is light, thin and smells pleasant—never cook chicken in thick acrid white smoke.
  2. Pat thighs dry. Rub with 2 tsp olive oil, 2 tsp smoked paprika, garlic powder, about 1 tsp kosher salt and pepper. Set skin-side up over indirect heat.
  3. Cook with lid closed about 35–50 minutes, checking the largest thigh. Brush with BBQ sauce only during the last 5–10 minutes to prevent burning. Chicken is safe at 165°F, but dark meat is often most tender at 175–185°F.
  4. While chicken cooks, heat oven to 425°F. Toss carrots with remaining 2 tsp oil, minced garlic, remaining ½ tsp smoked paprika and remaining salt. Roast 20–30 minutes.
  5. Grill corn in the Kamado for the final 10–15 minutes, turning until tender with a few charred spots. Rest chicken 5 minutes before serving with corn and carrots.

If the Kamado is still producing bitter smoke, use the oven at 400°F for the thighs and finish under the broiler. Portion BBQ sauce to keep sugar modest.
