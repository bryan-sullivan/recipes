Grilled Chicken with Spinach and Mozzarella

Ingredients

  • 24 ounces chicken breast, sliced lengthwise into 6 thin cutlets
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 10 ounces frozen spinach, thawed and thoroughly drained
  • 3 ounces shredded part-skim mozzarella
  • ½ cup roasted red peppers packed in water, sliced
  • Cooking spray or neutral oil

Method

  1. Squeeze as much moisture as possible from the spinach.
  2. Briefly sauté the garlic in olive oil. Add spinach, salt and pepper and cook until hot and relatively dry.
  3. Set the Kamado Joe for direct medium-high heat.
  4. Season and grill the thin chicken cutlets for roughly 2–3 minutes per side, adjusting for thickness, until they reach 165°F.
  5. Top with spinach, roasted peppers and mozzarella.
  6. Close the dome for 1–2 minutes, just until the cheese melts.

Nutrition

Approximately 195 calories and 31 grams protein per topped cutlet, before side dishes.
