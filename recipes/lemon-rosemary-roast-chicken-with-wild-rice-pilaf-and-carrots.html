Lemon-Rosemary Roast Chicken with Wild Rice Pilaf, Roasted Carrots & Light Gravy

Traditional lemon-rosemary roast chicken with savory wild rice and maple-free roasted carrots.

Serves 4 · Double oven + electric stovetop · About 105 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note · Shopping list

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, 4½–5 lb, giblets removed
  • 1½ lb carrots, peeled and cut on the bias
  • 1 cup dry wild rice blend
  • 1 yellow onion, quartered
  • 2 lemons, halved (1 for chicken, 1 for finishing)
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed or minced
  • 3 tbsp fresh rosemary, divided
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth, divided
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Pat chicken thoroughly dry. Rub with 2 tsp olive oil, salt, pepper and 2 tbsp rosemary. Put onion, lemon halves and 4 garlic cloves in cavity without tightly packing.
  2. Roast breast-up on a rack in a roasting pan, 70–90 minutes for a 4½–5 lb bird, until breast and thigh both reach at least 165°F; tent lightly and rest 15 minutes before carving.
  3. While chicken roasts, rinse wild rice blend and simmer in 2½ cups broth with 2 minced garlic cloves and ½ tbsp thyme for package time, usually 40–50 minutes. Add water as needed; fluff.
  4. In the second oven at 425°F, toss carrots with remaining olive oil, rosemary and thyme; roast 25–30 minutes, turning once, until browned and tender.
  5. For light gravy, skim fat from pan juices and whisk 2 tbsp flour with ½ cup cold broth in a saucepan. Add ½–1 cup strained defatted drippings (or water), simmer until glossy and thick, seasoning to taste. Carve and serve.

Start the chicken early today. A 4½–5 lb whole bird usually needs 70–90 minutes at 400°F; measure 165°F in the thickest breast and thigh without touching bone. For extra safety, check thigh temperature too.
