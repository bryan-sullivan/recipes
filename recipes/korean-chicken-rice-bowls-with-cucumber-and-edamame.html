Korean Chicken Rice Bowls with Cucumber and Edamame

Sweet-savory grilled chicken over brown rice with crisp cucumber and edamame. The marinade is punchy without relying on mustard.

Ingredients

Chicken and marinade

  • 1½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts, pounded to an even ½-inch thickness
  • ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons unsweetened applesauce
  • 1 tablespoon gochujang
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 2 teaspoons honey
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated

Bowls

  • 2 cups cooked brown rice
  • 1½ cups shelled edamame, thawed
  • 1 large English cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 2 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • Cooking spray or neutral high-heat oil

Method

  1. Whisk soy sauce, applesauce, gochujang, rice vinegar, honey, sesame oil, ginger and garlic. Reserve 3 tablespoons in a clean bowl for serving.
  2. Coat the chicken with the remaining marinade and refrigerate for 30 minutes to 4 hours.
  3. Set the Kamado Joe for direct cooking at 400–425°F. Clean and lightly oil the grate.
  4. Grill the chicken 4–6 minutes per side, moving thinner pieces to a cooler edge as needed. Cook to 165°F in the thickest part, then rest 5 minutes.
  5. Warm the rice and edamame. Divide among four bowls and add cucumber.
  6. Slice the chicken across the grain, place it over the bowls, and spoon on the reserved clean sauce. Finish with scallions and sesame seeds.

Nutrition estimate
Approximately 390 calories and 39 g protein per serving, based on four servings. Brand choice and portion size will change the total.
