Feta-Brined Chicken Bowls with Shirazi Salad

Tangy grilled chicken cutlets, finely chopped cucumber-tomato salad and a full cup of regular basmati rice per person.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1 pound thin-sliced chicken breast cutlets, 4 pieces
  • 4 tablespoons brine from a block of feta
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric
  • ¼ teaspoon dried oregano
  • Zest of ½ lemon

Shirazi salad

  • 4 Persian cucumbers, finely diced
  • 2 large Roma tomatoes, finely diced
  • ½ cup finely diced red onion
  • 3 tablespoons chopped basil, mint and parsley
  • ¾ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil

Rice and garnish

  • 4 cups cooked basmati rice, 1 cup per bowl
  • Crumbled feta, optional
  • Tzatziki, optional

Tip
If the feta has no usable brine, blend 1 ounce feta with 1 cup water and measure 4 tablespoons of that mixture.

Marinate

  1. Whisk the feta brine, olive oil, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, turmeric, oregano and lemon zest.
  2. Coat the chicken thoroughly.
  3. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes and no longer than 24 hours.

Cook

  1. Heat the Joe Jr., Blackstone or a grill pan to medium-high.
  2. Cook the thin cutlets for approximately 3–4 minutes per side, adjusting for thickness, until they reach 165°F.
  3. Rest for at least 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Combine all Shirazi salad ingredients.
  5. Build each bowl with 1 cup cooked rice, one-quarter of the chicken and one-quarter of the salad.

Nutrition

The original recipe with ¾ cup rice provides approximately 411 calories and 30.5 grams protein. Using 1 cup rice adds roughly 50 calories per bowl.

Storage

  • Keep the salad separate from the chicken and rice.
  • The salad is best within 2 days; chicken and rice keep for up to 4 days.
