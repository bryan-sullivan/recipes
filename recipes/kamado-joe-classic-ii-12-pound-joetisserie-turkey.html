Kamado Joe Classic II: 12 lb JoeTisserie Turkey Test with Light Gravy & Garlic Mashed Potatoes

Thanksgiving dress rehearsal: trussed, carefully balanced 12 lb whole turkey on the standard JoeTisserie spit, with a controlled 325°F kamado fire, light gravy and mashed potatoes.

Kamado Joe Classic II + JoeTisserie standard spit + stovetop + oven · About 300 minutes (including approximate rotisserie, prep and rest; charcoal timing varies) · About 10–12 turkey portions; sides for 4–6. Meat weights are raw unless stated otherwise; dry rice/pasta quantities are uncooked.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note · Shopping list

Side recipe: Provisional Garlic-Parmesan Green Beans

Ingredients

  • 1 whole 12 lb turkey, fully thawed, giblets and neck removed
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil for turkey skin
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt, only if turkey is not enhanced/pre-salted (adjust for brand)
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp poultry seasoning, salt-free
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh rosemary
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh sage
  • 1 lemon, cut in half (aromatic only, do not tightly pack cavity)
  • 1 small onion, quartered (optional aromatic, not tightly packed)
  • 2½ lb Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled or scrubbed and quartered
  • ½ cup warmed 1% or 2% milk for potatoes
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter for potatoes
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken or turkey stock for gravy
  • 2½ tbsp all-purpose flour for gravy
  • 1 tbsp fresh chopped parsley, optional
  • 1½ lb fresh green beans for separately linked provisional side (see green bean recipe)

Method

  1. MONDAY–THURSDAY PREP: If frozen, thaw the 12 lb turkey in its wrapper in a refrigerator at 40°F or below for roughly 3 days (allow extra margin). Check cavity for ice before Friday. On Thursday, read the label: if already brined/enhanced/self-basting, do not apply a heavy salt dry brine. Pat dry; season only as appropriate, and refrigerate uncovered on a rack overnight if room permits.
  2. THURSDAY DRY FIT: With charcoal UNLIT, tightly truss the legs and secure wings firmly against the body. Center the standard JoeTisserie spit through the turkey, lock both forks tightly, and test rotation in the Classic II with the dome closed. Confirm nothing rubs or hits. Rebalance and adjust if motor strains or bird swings. The optional JoeTisserie basket kit is rated for only 10 lb; do NOT use that kit for this 12 lb whole bird.
  3. FRIDAY FIRE: Bank a modest quantity of clean lump charcoal toward the rear of the charcoal basket to keep concentrated heat away from dripping fat. Light in one location. Install standard JoeTisserie ring and motor according to the accessory instructions. Bring the ceramic grill steadily to approximately 325°F and hold about 20 minutes; wait until heavy white smoke disappears. Do not add smoking wood.
  4. PREP TURKEY: Coat skin lightly with neutral oil and salt-free poultry seasoning, garlic powder, pepper, chopped rosemary and sage; salt only if the label and any prior seasoning justify it. Keep the cavity loose. Slide the balanced spit into the motor and support, turn on motor and verify smooth complete rotation before closing the lid.
  5. ROAST & MONITOR: Cook at a steady 300–325°F with ongoing rotation. A 12 lb bird may need roughly 2½–4 hours, so budget generously; timing is only a guide. Monitor the dome/ambient temperature and use a rotisserie-compatible wireless breast probe if available. Check the turkey periodically without stopping rotation for long. If grease flames flare, reduce airflow and re-position banked coals safely; do not spray water on the fire.
  6. DONENESS: Stop the motor briefly and use a calibrated instant-read thermometer in several locations; reach at least 165°F in the thickest breast, innermost thigh and innermost wing without touching bone. Restart rotation if not done. If skin browns too quickly before the interior is safe, reduce heat; if it is fully done but not browned, avoid overcooking for color.
  7. REST & CARVE: Remove turkey carefully with heat-resistant gloves, disconnect motor power, set spit on a heat-safe surface, remove forks and twine, and rest 20–30 minutes before carving. Refrigerate leftovers promptly within 2 hours; reserve about 1½ lb cooked turkey for Saturday's fried rice.
  8. SIDES: While turkey rests, boil potatoes 15–20 minutes, drain and mash with warmed milk and butter. Make light gravy by whisking 2½ tbsp flour into ½ cup cold stock, then slowly whisk into 2½ cups simmering stock with a few tablespoons of strained defatted turkey juices; simmer until thick and season to taste. Prepare the separate provisional garlic-Parmesan green beans in the oven. Note actual timing, fuel, dome temperature, target reading and motor performance as Thanksgiving test results.

Critical test: clearance and balance are separate from motor weight rating. Official standard JoeTisserie claims up to 50 lb, but the optional BASKET accessory has a 10 lb cap and is NOT suitable for this 12 lb bird. Do an unlit trial first. Always check 165°F across breast, thigh, wing; don't rely on time.
