Ground Beef & Broccoli Stir-Fry with Jasmine Rice & Extra Broccoli

Your rating
Not rated

Serves 4 · Electric stovetop · About 25 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb 90/10 ground beef
  • 2 lb broccoli florets — 1½ lb in stir-fry; ½ lb extra steamed
  • ½ medium yellow onions
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 0.333 cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1½ tsp toasted sesame oil
  • ¾ cup dry jasmine rice
  • ½ cup water

Method

  1. Start the rice using its package water ratio. Steam all broccoli until crisp-tender; reserve one quarter as the extra side.
  2. Whisk soy sauce, honey, ginger, garlic, cornstarch and water. Brown beef with onion in sesame oil, breaking it up; cook to 160°F.
  3. Stir sauce into beef and simmer until thickened. Fold in the remaining broccoli. Divide beef, rice and reserved broccoli among four plates.

Allow about 25 minutes including rice. Use 90/10 beef; drain excess rendered fat.

Nutrition

Approximately 490 calories and 43 g protein per serving including sides. Household estimates; brands and portions vary. Import to calculate WeightWatchers points.

Source and adaptation

Family adaptation of Skinnytaste’s original recipe, with measured portions and the agreed sides.
