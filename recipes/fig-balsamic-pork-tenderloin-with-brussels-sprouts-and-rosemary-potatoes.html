Fig-Balsamic Pork Tenderloin with Roasted Brussels Sprouts & Rosemary Baby Potatoes

Your rating
Not rated

Lean pork, sweet-tangy fig-balsamic glaze, crisp Brussels sprouts and rosemary potatoes.

Serves 4 · Double oven · About 45 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note

Ingredients

  • 2 lb pork tenderloin (raw; about two small tenderloins)
  • 3 tbsp fig butter or fig spread
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1½ lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4 tsp olive oil, divided
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary (or 1 tbsp chopped fresh)
  • ¾ tsp kosher salt, divided
  • ½ tsp black pepper, divided
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan

Method

  1. Preheat one oven to 425°F for the vegetables and the second to 400°F for the pork. Pat pork dry. Mix fig spread and balsamic; season pork with garlic powder and a portion of the salt and pepper. Brush with glaze.
  2. Toss halved potatoes with 2 tsp olive oil, rosemary and a pinch each of salt and pepper. Roast on a lined sheet 25–35 minutes, turning once.
  3. Toss Brussels sprouts with 2 tsp olive oil, minced garlic and remaining salt and pepper. Roast on another sheet 20–25 minutes until browned; add Parmesan near the end.
  4. Roast pork on a separate sheet at 400°F, brushing with remaining glaze partway through, until thickest center reaches 145°F, usually 20–35 minutes depending on thickness. Rest 5–10 minutes before slicing.
  5. Divide sliced pork, Brussels sprouts and potatoes evenly between four plates.

Tenderloin timing depends on thickness: use a thermometer, not a fixed minute count. Fig preserves can replace fig butter; choose a modest-sugar brand.
