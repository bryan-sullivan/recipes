Easy Fall Sheet-Pan Chicken with Butternut Squash, Brussels Sprouts, Red Onion & Feta

A simple oven dinner with lean chicken, autumn vegetables and a salty feta finish.

Ingredients

Meat weights are raw; vegetable weights are trimmed, edible portions.

Chicken and vegetables

  • 1½ pounds (680 g) boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 pound (454 g) pre-cut butternut squash, in ½-inch cubes
  • 1 pound (454 g) Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved; quarter large sprouts
  • 1 medium red onion (150 g), cut into wedges
  • 1½ tablespoons (22 ml) olive oil, divided
  • 3 ounces (85 g) reduced-fat feta, crumbled

Seasoning

  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon dried oregano
  • ¾ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a large rimmed sheet pan with foil. Use two pans if needed to keep everything in a single uncrowded layer.
  2. Toss squash, sprouts and onion with 1 tablespoon oil and half the seasonings. Spread on the pan and roast for 10 minutes.
  3. Toss chicken with the remaining ½ tablespoon oil and seasonings. Add to the vegetables. Roast 18–22 minutes, until chicken reaches 165°F and squash is tender. Remove any finished chicken while vegetables finish if needed.
  4. Scatter feta over the pan and return to the oven for 2 minutes. Divide chicken and vegetables evenly among four plates.

Busy Wednesday: buy peeled, diced squash and trimmed sprouts. Portion the chicken and mix the seasoning Tuesday night; refrigerate raw chicken separately. About 10 minutes active prep and 35 minutes in the oven.
