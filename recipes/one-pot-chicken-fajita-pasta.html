One-Pot Chicken Fajita Pasta

Easy-Wednesday one-skillet chicken pasta with sweet peppers, lightly creamy fajita-tomato sauce, and avocado.

Serves 4 · Electric stovetop only · About 35 minutes (not including Kamado startup). Meat weights raw; pasta and couscous measured dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note · Shopping list

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into ¾-inch pieces
  • 8 oz dry penne or other short pasta
  • 1 large white or yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 large red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 large yellow bell pepper, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2¼ cups low-sodium chicken broth, plus ¼ cup extra if pasta needs it
  • 1 can (10 oz) mild diced tomatoes and green chiles (Ro-Tel), with juices
  • ½ cup light sour cream
  • 1 small avocado (about 4 oz flesh), diced
  • 1 scallion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 1½ tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp chili powder, optional
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, divided, plus to taste
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Combine cumin, paprika, optional chili powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Season the chicken with about half the mixture.
  2. Heat 1 tsp oil in a deep 12-inch skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown chicken in one layer for 4–6 minutes, stirring occasionally; it will finish cooking later. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Add remaining 1 tsp oil, onion, and sliced peppers. Sauté for 6–8 minutes until softened. Add garlic and remaining seasoning for 30 seconds, then transfer vegetables to the chicken plate.
  4. Add dry pasta, broth, and the full can of tomatoes with its juice to the skillet. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook 12–16 minutes, stirring two or three times, until pasta is al dente. If needed, add a splash of extra broth; do not drain.
  5. Return chicken and vegetables with their juices to the pasta; simmer 2–4 minutes until chicken is 165°F throughout. Take off heat and fold in sour cream to make a light sauce. Divide into four portions; top with avocado, scallion, and cilantro.

Genuine one-pot dinner with no separate side. Use mild Ro-Tel and leave the chili powder out for visiting family who prefer mild food. Sour cream goes in off heat to avoid curdling.

Nutrition

Calorie-conscious, high-protein adaptation. Complete-meal nutrition and WeightWatchers points have not been independently calculated. The original Skinnytaste pasta is 390 calories and 29 g protein per serving; the 1½-lb chicken and 4-serving adaptation changes those values.
