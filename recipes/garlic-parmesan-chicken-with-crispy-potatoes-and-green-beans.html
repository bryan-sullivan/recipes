Garlic-Parmesan Chicken with Crispy Potatoes & Green Beans

An easy sheet-pan dinner with juicy garlic-Parmesan chicken cutlets, crisp-edged potatoes and blistered green beans. Staggering the ingredients lets each component finish at the right time without drying out the chicken.

Ingredients

Potatoes

  • 1½ pounds baby Yukon Gold or red potatoes, quartered
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Chicken

  • 1½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • ½ cup finely grated Parmesan, divided
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil

Green beans and finish

  • 12–16 ounces fresh green beans, trimmed
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ teaspoon olive oil
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Red pepper flakes, optional

Tip
Slice thick chicken breasts horizontally into cutlets or pound them to an even ¾-inch thickness. Even pieces cook on the same schedule and give the Parmesan mixture more surface area to cover.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a large rimmed sheet pan with parchment or foil.
  2. Toss the potatoes with 2 teaspoons olive oil, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Arrange them cut-side down and roast for 15–20 minutes.
  3. While the potatoes roast, cut or pound the chicken to an even ¾-inch thickness.
  4. Reserve 2 tablespoons Parmesan for finishing. Combine the remaining Parmesan with Italian seasoning, onion powder, smoked paprika, garlic, salt and pepper. Rub the chicken with 2 teaspoons olive oil, then press the Parmesan mixture onto both sides.
  5. Flip or stir the potatoes and move them toward the edges of the pan. Add the chicken in a single layer and roast for 10 minutes.
  6. Toss the green beans with garlic, ½ teaspoon olive oil, salt and pepper. Add them to the pan and roast everything for another 8–12 minutes, until the beans are blistered and the thickest part of the chicken reaches 165°F.
  7. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes. Finish the pan with lemon juice, the reserved Parmesan and red pepper flakes, if using.

Timing key
The potato head start and evenly sized chicken cutlets are the keys to getting crisp potatoes, tender beans and juicy chicken from one pan.

Nutrition

Approximately 440 calories and 45 grams protein per serving. The estimate assumes 1½ tablespoons olive oil total and four equal servings; actual values vary by chicken size, Parmesan brand and the amount of oil left on the pan.
