Blackstone Grilled Peaches with Cheesecake Cream

Warm, caramelized peaches topped with cool vanilla cheesecake cream and graham-cracker crumbs.

Nutrition

Approximately 140 calories and 13 grams protein per peach half. Actual values depend on the size of the peaches, protein powder and sweetener used.

Ingredients

Grilled peaches

  • 4 ripe but firm peaches
  • Nonstick cooking spray or 1 teaspoon neutral high-heat oil
  • ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon

Cheesecake cream

  • 1 cups nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • ½ scoop vanilla protein powder
  • 2 tablespoons honey or sugar-free maple syrup
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of kosher salt

Topping

  • 2 full graham-cracker sheets, crushed

Prepare the cheesecake cream

  1. Blend the Greek yogurt, cream cheese, protein powder, honey, vanilla and salt until completely smooth.
  2. Transfer to a covered container and refrigerate while preparing the peaches.

Blackstone method

  1. Cut the peaches in half along the seam, twist them apart and remove the pits.
  2. Heat one Blackstone zone to medium-low. Clean the surface well if it was previously used for savory food.
  3. Lightly coat the hot surface with cooking spray or oil.
  4. Place the peaches cut-side down and cook without moving for 3–4 minutes, until marked and lightly caramelized.
  5. Flip the peaches, sprinkle the cut sides with cinnamon and cook for another 2–3 minutes. They should be warm and slightly tender but still hold their shape.
  6. Place one warm peach half in each serving bowl. Top with cheesecake cream and graham-cracker crumbs just before serving.

Kamado or indoor option

  • Kamado Joe: Grill the peach halves over a clean grate at 375–400°F, beginning cut-side down, for the same approximate cooking time.
  • Indoor skillet: Cook in a lightly greased grill pan or skillet over medium heat.

Make ahead and storage

  • Prepare the cheesecake cream up to 2 days in advance.
  • Grill the peaches shortly before serving; they are best warm.
  • Refrigerate leftover cream for up to 4 days and use it as a fruit dip.
