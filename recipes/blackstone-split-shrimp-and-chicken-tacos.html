Blackstone Split Shrimp and Chicken Tacos

One dinner, two completely separate protein paths. The chicken option is cooked first for the diner who dislikes shrimp; the shrimp never shares a bowl, utensil, griddle zone or platter with it.

Separation plan
Use separate bowls, tongs, spatulas and platters. Cook chicken first on the left zone, then shrimp on the right zone. If this is an allergy rather than a preference, use a separate cooking surface because one griddle cannot prevent cross-contact.

Ingredients

Proteins

  • 12 ounces peeled and deveined medium shrimp, tails removed
  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into thin strips
  • 2 teaspoons neutral high-heat oil, divided
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder, divided
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin, divided
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika, divided
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Tacos and toppings

  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 3 cups finely shredded cabbage
  • 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 limes, zested and juiced
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • ½ cup chopped cilantro
  • ½ cup pico de gallo or diced tomato
  • Hot sauce, optional

Method

  1. Use separate bowls, tongs, spatulas and serving platters for the chicken and shrimp. Season each protein in its own bowl with half the oil and spices.
  2. Heat the left side of the Blackstone to medium-high, about 400°F, for chicken. Keep the right side off until the chicken has been moved to a clean serving platter.
  3. Cook chicken on the left zone for 5–7 minutes, turning often, until browned and 165°F. Remove with the chicken-only spatula.
  4. Turn on and lightly oil the right zone. Cook shrimp there for 1–2 minutes per side until opaque and 145°F. Remove with shrimp-only tools.
  5. Warm tortillas in the center zone, keeping them away from shrimp juices.
  6. Mix yogurt with lime zest and enough lime juice to make a drizzle. Fill tacos with cabbage and the chosen protein; add avocado, cilantro, pico and lime yogurt.
  7. Serve the chicken and shrimp from separate platters with their separate utensils.

Nutrition estimate
Approximately 430 calories and 38–43 g protein per serving, based on four servings. Brand choice and portion size will change the total.
