Blackstone Korean BBQ Beef Rice Bowls

Savory-sweet gochujang beef, crisp vegetables, edamame and rice cooked as a fast, family-friendly Blackstone bowl.

Ingredients

Korean BBQ sauce

  • ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons gochujang
  • 1 tablespoon honey or light brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon finely grated fresh ginger

Beef and vegetables

  • 1¼ pounds 90% lean ground beef
  • 12 ounces shredded cabbage or coleslaw mix
  • 2 medium carrots, shredded
  • 1 cup frozen shelled edamame, thawed
  • 1 teaspoon neutral high-heat oil, plus more only if needed
  • 2 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds, optional

Bowls

  • 2 cups cooked jasmine or brown rice, kept warm
  • 1 English cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • Pinch of kosher salt

Fast setup
Mix the sauce, cook the rice and prep every vegetable before lighting the griddle. The Blackstone portion moves quickly once the beef hits the surface.

Method

  1. Whisk the soy sauce, gochujang, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic and ginger until smooth.
  2. Toss the cucumber with 1 tablespoon rice vinegar and a pinch of salt; set aside. Divide the warm rice among four bowls.
  3. Preheat two Blackstone zones to medium-high and one zone to medium. Add the neutral oil to the medium-high side, then spread the beef into a thin layer. Leave it undisturbed for 2 minutes to brown.
  4. Break the beef into bite-size crumbles and cook for 3–4 minutes more, until no pink remains and it reaches 160°F. Drain excess fat if needed.
  5. Add the cabbage and carrots on the adjacent zone. Cook for 3–4 minutes, tossing often, until crisp-tender. Add the edamame for the final 2 minutes.
  6. Pour the sauce over the beef, toss and cook for 1–2 minutes until glossy. Fold in the vegetables or keep them separate for serving.
  7. Spoon the beef and vegetables over the rice. Top with cucumber, scallions and sesame seeds.

Keep it lean
Use 90% lean beef and measure the sesame oil; the gochujang sauce and browned beef provide plenty of flavor without a heavy finishing sauce.

Nutrition

Approximately 485 calories and 39 grams protein per serving. The estimate uses 90% lean beef, ½ cup cooked rice per person and four equal servings.
