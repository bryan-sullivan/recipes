Chicken Taco Poblano Rice Bowls

Fast chicken-breast taco bowls with poblano, corn, brown rice and fresh pico; an easy Wednesday/Thursday meal prep.

Eight servings, for four people on two nights · Large stovetop skillet (or two), microwave for precooked rice · About 30 minutes on cooking day. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta amounts are measured dry unless stated otherwise.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note · Printable shopping list · Shared list

Ingredients for 8 servings

  • 2½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cubed into ½-inch pieces
  • 4 tsp olive oil
  • 2 medium red onions, diced (reserve 6 tbsp for pico)
  • 2 poblano peppers, seeded and diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, finely diced
  • ½ cup fresh cilantro, minced, divided
  • 2 limes (juice for pico, extra wedges)
  • 2 cups frozen or canned corn kernels, drained if canned
  • 6 cups cooked brown rice, warmed (about 3 microwave-ready rice pouches; verify package yield)
  • ½ cup shredded reduced-fat cheddar-jack cheese
  • ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt for topping, optional
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp kosher salt for seasoning, plus ½ tsp for pico, to taste
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 cup water, divided, as needed for chicken skillet
  • 2 cans (15 oz each) black beans, drained and warmed (optional Thursday variation)

Directions

  1. To make the double-batch taco seasoning, stir together garlic powder, cumin, 2 tsp salt, chili powder, paprika, and oregano. Dice chicken and vegetables ahead if possible.
  2. Make a fresh pico in a separate container: combine reserved 6 tbsp red onion, Roma tomatoes, half the cilantro, juice from 1–2 limes, and about ½ tsp salt. Keep cold. Reserve a separate portion for Thursday so it stays fresh.
  3. Heat 2 tsp oil in each of two large skillets over medium-high (or cook chicken in two batches in one skillet). Add half of chicken to each and cook 3–4 minutes until lightly browned.
  4. Divide remaining onion, both poblano peppers, and seasoning across skillets. Stir-cook 3 minutes. Add half the corn and approximately ½ cup water to each, then simmer 2–5 minutes until the chicken's thickest pieces register 165°F. Avoid overcooking small chicken cubes.
  5. Heat or microwave 6 cups cooked brown rice according to package directions. For each of eight bowls, portion ¾ cup rice and one-eighth of the chicken mixture. Add cheese, fresh pico, optional yogurt and cilantro at serving.
  6. Serve four bowls Wednesday. Chill four additional chicken/rice portions promptly in shallow airtight containers and keep pico, cheese and yogurt cold separately. On Thursday, reheat chicken and rice to 165°F and add cold toppings after heating. Optionally add warmed black beans Thursday for variety.

Make-ahead safety: Chill leftovers in shallow containers within two hours. Refrigerate cooked chicken, rice and beef portions promptly, and reheat leftovers thoroughly to 165°F. Keep fresh pico and cucumber raita cold until serving.

Nutrition and WW

Original Skinnytaste recipe: 475 calories and 40.5 g protein per bowl. Our rice brands, toppings, doubled batch and optional black beans may change values; WW points not independently calculated.
