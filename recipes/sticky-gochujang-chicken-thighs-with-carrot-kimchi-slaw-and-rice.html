Sticky Gochujang Chicken Thighs with Carrot-Kimchi Slaw & Rice

Sweet-spicy glazed chicken with a crisp carrot-kimchi slaw and rice, finished under the broiler or over a hot Kamado fire.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1½ pounds (680 g) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, trimmed
  • 3 tablespoons gochujang
  • 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil

Carrot-kimchi slaw

  • 12 ounces (340 g) plain coleslaw mix
  • 2 medium carrots, shredded
  • 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon gochujang
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
  • 3 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds, optional

Serve with

  • 1 cup (185 g) dry jasmine rice, cooked

Tip
Gochujang brands vary in heat and sweetness. Taste the glaze before it touches raw chicken and adjust with honey or vinegar.

Method

  1. Whisk the gochujang, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, garlic, ginger and sesame oil. Reserve 2 tablespoons in a clean bowl, then coat the chicken with the rest for 20 minutes or refrigerate up to 8 hours.
  2. Cook the rice. Toss the slaw mix and carrots with rice vinegar, gochujang, honey, sesame oil and salt. Fold in the scallions and chill.
  3. For the oven, arrange the chicken on a foil-lined broiler pan about 6 inches from the element. Broil for 6–8 minutes, turn and broil 5–7 minutes more, until browned and 165°F.
  4. For the Kamado, cook directly at 425–450°F for about 5–7 minutes per side, moving pieces as needed to avoid scorching, until 165°F.
  5. Brush the cooked chicken with the reserved clean glaze. Rest 5 minutes, then slice and serve with rice and carrot-kimchi slaw. Sprinkle with sesame seeds if using.

Plan note
The oven method gives you the same sticky edges when outdoor cooking is inconvenient.

Nutrition

Approximately ~520 calories and ~42 g protein per serving. Nutrition varies with brands and serving choices.
