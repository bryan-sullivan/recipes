Blackstone Crispy Lamb with Indian Carrot Salad

Crisp lean lamb on the Blackstone, then pile it under bright lemon–ginger carrot ribbons, fresh herbs and toasted seeds. Yogurt and warm naan turn the salad into a complete high-protein dinner.

Family adaptation
This version reduces the oil, specifies lean lamb, adds measured yogurt and naan portions, and moves the cooking to the Blackstone while keeping the original dish’s spicy lamb and sharp carrot-herb contrast.

Ingredients

Crispy lamb

  • 1¼ pounds lean ground lamb, preferably 90% lean or better
  • 2 teaspoons garam masala
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Carrot salad

  • 1¼ pounds carrots, peeled
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • ½ cup fresh cilantro leaves
  • ½ cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole cumin seeds
  • 1 small red onion, very thinly sliced
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon, plus wedges for serving
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil

To serve

  • 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 4 mini whole-wheat naan, or 2 full-size naan halved

Method

  1. Preheat two Blackstone zones to medium-high, about 400°F, leaving a cooler zone for holding. Toast the cumin seeds on a dry zone for about 30 seconds, then toast the sesame seeds until golden. Remove both immediately.
  2. Spread the lamb over the hot griddle. Let it brown undisturbed for 2 minutes, then chop and turn it with the spatula. Drain excess rendered fat into the grease channel.
  3. Season the lamb with garam masala, salt and pepper. Continue cooking until deeply browned, crisp at the edges and 160°F in the center, about 6–8 minutes more.
  4. Crush the toasted cumin. Whisk it with the lemon zest and juice, red onion, ginger, olive oil, a pinch of salt and several grinds of pepper.
  5. Use a vegetable peeler or mandoline to shave the carrots into long ribbons. Toss with the dressing, cilantro and mint immediately before serving.
  6. Warm the naan on the cooler griddle zone for 1–2 minutes per side. Divide the crispy lamb among four plates, mound the carrot salad over it and finish with sesame seeds. Serve with yogurt, naan and lemon wedges.

Food safety
Cook ground lamb to 160°F. Keep the finished lamb on a clean platter, separate from anything that held the raw meat.

Nutrition estimate
Approximately 540 calories and 36 g protein per serving, including yogurt and naan. Lamb leanness and naan size will change the total.

Source and adaptation

Original family adaptation inspired by Jamie Oliver’s Indian Carrot Salad with Crispy Spiced Lamb. Ingredient quantities, nutrition targets, Blackstone directions and wording are adapted for this family menu.
