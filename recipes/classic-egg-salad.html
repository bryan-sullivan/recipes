Classic Egg Salad

Creamy, crunchy, and familiar—this mustard-free egg salad keeps the classic flavor while using Greek yogurt and light mayonnaise to make it a little lighter.

Nutrition

Approximately 180–190 calories and 13–14 grams of protein per serving, before bread, crackers, or other serving extras.

Ingredients

  • 8 large eggs
  • ¼ cup light mayonnaise
  • ¼ cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 celery rib, finely diced
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped scallions or chives
  • 1 tablespoon dill pickle relish
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • ⅛ teaspoon paprika, plus more for serving

How to hard-boil the eggs

  1. Arrange the eggs in a single layer in a medium saucepan. Add enough cold water to cover the eggs by 1 inch.
  2. Set the pan over medium-high heat and bring the water to a full rolling boil, uncovered.
  3. As soon as the water boils, remove the pan from the heat. Cover and let the eggs stand for 11 to 12 minutes.
  4. While they rest, fill a bowl with ice and cold water. Transfer the eggs immediately to the ice bath and cool for 10 minutes.
  5. Tap each egg all over, then roll it gently on the counter to loosen the shell. Peel under cool running water, starting at the wider end where the air pocket is.

Make the egg salad

  1. Roughly chop the peeled eggs and place them in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the light mayonnaise, Greek yogurt, celery, scallions or chives, relish, lemon juice, salt, pepper, and paprika.
  3. Fold gently until creamy but still chunky. Taste and adjust the salt, pepper, lemon juice, or relish.
  4. Serve immediately, or cover and chill for 30 minutes for the best flavor.

Serving ideas

  • Pile it onto toasted whole-grain bread with lettuce and tomato.
  • Spoon it into lettuce cups or over mixed greens for a lighter lunch.
  • Serve with whole-grain crackers, cucumber slices, or mini sweet peppers.

Notes

  • For the best texture, keep some pieces of egg white and yolk visible instead of mashing everything smooth.
  • If the salad seems dry after chilling, fold in another spoonful of Greek yogurt or light mayonnaise.
  • For extra crunch, add another tablespoon of celery or finely diced dill pickle.

Storage

Store covered in the refrigerator and use within 3 days. Keep chilled until serving.
