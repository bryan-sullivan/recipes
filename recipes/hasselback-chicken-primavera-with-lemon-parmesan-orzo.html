Hasselback Chicken Primavera with Lemon-Parmesan Basil Orzo

Your rating
Not rated

Chicken breasts slashed and filled with vegetables and part-skim mozzarella; bright basil orzo.

Serves 4 · Oven + electric stovetop · About 40 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts (4 breasts, about 8 oz each)
  • 1 medium zucchini, cut into thin half-moons
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, thinly sliced
  • ½ medium red onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 oz part-skim mozzarella, shredded
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan, divided
  • 6 oz dry orzo
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 3 tsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Lay chicken breasts on a board and make 5–6 deep parallel crosswise cuts, stopping about ¼ inch before the bottom so the breasts stay intact.
  2. Place chicken on a lined sheet pan. Season with garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper and 2 tsp olive oil. Tuck zucchini, tomato and onion slices into cuts; top each with mozzarella and 2 tbsp of the Parmesan in total.
  3. Bake 25–35 minutes, depending on thickness, until the deepest part of each breast reaches 165°F. Broil 1–2 minutes for color only after chicken is fully cooked, if desired.
  4. Meanwhile cook 6 oz dry orzo in salted water until tender. Drain. Briefly soften garlic in remaining 1 tsp olive oil; toss with orzo, lemon zest and juice, chopped basil and remaining Parmesan.
  5. Serve one chicken breast with one quarter of the orzo; reserve extra vegetables for the plate.

The orzo serving is 1½ oz dry per person. Select similarly sized breasts, and use an instant-read thermometer.
