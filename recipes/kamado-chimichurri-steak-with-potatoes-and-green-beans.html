Kamado Chimichurri Steak with Potatoes and Green Beans

A generous Sunday steak cook designed to become Leftover Chopped Chimichurri Steak Salad with almost no work Monday night.

Ingredients

Steak

  • 3 pounds sirloin, strip steak or flank steak, trimmed
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • High-heat cooking spray or a small amount of neutral oil

Chimichurri

  • 2 packed cups fresh parsley leaves
  • 6 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
  • ¼ cup red-wine vinegar
  • ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon crushed red-pepper flakes, or less to taste
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, plus more only if needed after tasting

Potatoes and green beans

  • 2 pounds baby or Yukon Gold potatoes, halved
  • 1½ pounds green beans, trimmed
  • 1½ tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Lemon wedges

Make the chimichurri

  1. Pulse the parsley, garlic, vinegar, lemon juice, pepper flakes, salt and pepper in a small food processor until finely chopped.
  2. With the machine running, drizzle in 2 tablespoons olive oil. Keep some texture in the sauce. Taste before adding more; the vinegar and lemon already make this a loose sauce.
  3. Before handling raw beef, refrigerate ½ cup in a clean container for Monday.

Note
This version is intended to be spoonable but herb-forward, not an oily vinaigrette. If it tastes balanced after 2 tablespoons oil, stop. If it is unpleasantly sharp or acidic, stir in additional oil 1 teaspoon at a time. If it tastes good but seems too watery, add more finely chopped parsley rather than more oil. Reserve ½ cup for Monday and serve tonight’s portion at the table rather than pouring it all over the steak.

Prepare the sides

  1. Parboil the potatoes in salted water for 8–10 minutes, until the outside is tender but the centers remain firm. Drain and dry well.
  2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tablespoon oil, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Toss the green beans separately with the remaining oil, salt and pepper.

Kamado setup

  1. Fill and light the charcoal basket. Install a half-moon heat deflector beneath one half of the cooking grate; leave the other half exposed to the coals.
  2. Stabilize the dome temperature around 450–500°F.
  3. Treat the exposed half as the direct zone for browning the vegetables and searing the steak.
  4. Treat the half above the ceramic deflector as the indirect zone for finishing the potatoes, gently bringing thick steak to temperature and temporarily holding food away from the flames.

Cooking order and zone use

  1. Potatoes — direct, then indirect: Put the parboiled potatoes in a grill basket or cast-iron pan. Brown them over the direct zone for 5–7 minutes, tossing once or twice. Slide the basket or pan to the indirect zone, close the dome and cook another 8–12 minutes, until tender.
  2. Green beans — direct: Cook the beans in a basket over the direct zone for 5–8 minutes, tossing frequently, until blistered but still crisp. Remove them from the grill; do not leave them parked over the indirect side or they may soften too much.
  3. Steak — direct, then indirect if needed: With the direct zone cleared, sear the steak there for approximately 2–4 minutes per side, depending on thickness. If it has a good crust but the center is not yet at the target temperature, move it to the indirect side, close the dome and finish gently.

Tip
The indirect side is not truly “cool”—it is still roughly a 450°F roasting environment. Its purpose is to shield food from direct radiant heat and flare-ups.

Grill the steak

  1. Season the steak with salt and pepper 30–45 minutes before grilling.
  2. Follow the direct-to-indirect sequence above, turning as needed for an even crust.
  3. Use an instant-read thermometer rather than relying on time. USDA guidance for whole beef cuts is 145°F followed by at least a 3-minute rest.
  4. Rest the steak for 10 minutes.
  5. Slice only what is needed for Sunday, cutting thinly across the grain.

Serve and reserve

  • Serve about 5–6 ounces steak per person with potatoes, green beans and chimichurri.
  • Refrigerate approximately 1¼ pounds cooked steak for Monday, preferably as a whole unsliced piece.
  • Refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours.

Estimated nutrition

Approximately 500–600 calories and 40–45 grams protein per serving, depending on steak cut, potato portion and chimichurri.

Source
