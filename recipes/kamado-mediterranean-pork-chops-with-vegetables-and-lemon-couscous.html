Kamado Mediterranean Pork Chops with Vegetables & Lemon-Herb Couscous

Lean garlic-oregano pork chops with roasted tomatoes, zucchini, squash, olives and feta, served with lemony couscous.

Serves 4 · Kamado Joe Classic II + oven + stovetop · About 45 minutes (not including Kamado startup). Meat weights raw; pasta and couscous measured dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note · Shopping list

Ingredients

  • 8 thin boneless center-cut pork chops (about 2 lb total, ¼ lb each)
  • 2 medium zucchini (about 12 oz total), sliced into thin half-moons
  • 2 medium yellow summer squash (about 12 oz total), sliced into thin half-moons
  • 1 pint grape tomatoes, halved
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup pitted sliced Kalamata olives
  • ¼ cup crumbled feta cheese
  • ¾ cup dry plain couscous
  • 1 cup low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth, for couscous
  • 1 large lemon (1 tsp zest plus 2 tbsp juice; reserve rest for serving)
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley, for couscous
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp garlic powder or salt-free poultry seasoning
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, plus to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Stabilize the Kamado Joe at 400–425°F for direct grilling and wait until the lump-charcoal smoke is thin and clean. Preheat a conventional oven to 425°F.
  2. Season pork chops with garlic powder, half the oregano, salt, pepper and 1 tsp olive oil. Refrigerate while preparing vegetables, then let chops sit briefly at room temperature as the grill settles.
  3. Spread halved tomatoes and sliced garlic on a lined baking sheet; toss with 1 tsp olive oil and roast 12–15 minutes. Sauté zucchini and summer squash on the stovetop with another 1 tsp olive oil, remaining oregano and a pinch of salt for 5–7 minutes. Combine with tomatoes, lemon zest, 1 tbsp lemon juice, olives and feta.
  4. Bring 1 cup broth to a simmer. Stir in ¾ cup dry couscous, cover and remove from heat; fluff after about 5 minutes. Fold in parsley, 1 tbsp lemon juice and 1 tsp olive oil. Add a tablespoon of water if the grains seem dry.
  5. Grill thin pork chops about 2–4 minutes per side (thickness and grill heat vary); verify each reaches 145°F at the center and rest for at least 3 minutes. Serve two chops per diner with vegetable topping and about ½ cup cooked couscous.

Thin pork chops dry out fast: use a probe or instant-read thermometer rather than cooking exclusively by time. Internal 145°F plus at least a 3-minute rest. The Kamado's clean smoke matters for mild pork.

Nutrition

Calorie-conscious, high-protein adaptation. Complete-meal nutrition and WeightWatchers points have not been independently calculated. The original Skinnytaste pork and vegetables are about 230 calories and 28 g protein per serving at the original 1-lb meat quantity; this 2-lb version adds couscous, so nutrition differs.
