Kamado 90% Beef Kofta Plates with Tzatziki & Tomato-Cucumber Salad

Cumin-coriander beef kofta grilled over direct heat with cool tzatziki and a crisp tomato-cucumber salad.

Ingredients

Kofta

  • 1½ pounds 90% lean ground beef
  • ½ small onion, finely grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ cup chopped parsley
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1½ teaspoons ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

Tzatziki

  • 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • ½ cucumber, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 tablespoon chopped dill or parsley
  • Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste

Salad

  • 1 large cucumber, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil

Optional

  • 4 small pitas, or roasted potatoes

Tip
Wet your hands lightly before shaping the kofta. Chill the formed kofta for 15 minutes if the mixture feels soft.

Method

  1. Combine the beef, onion, garlic, parsley, cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, cinnamon, salt and pepper without overworking the meat.
  2. Form the mixture into 8 oblong kofta.
  3. Set the Kamado for direct grilling at about 425°F.
  4. Mix the tzatziki ingredients and refrigerate. Toss the salad ingredients together.
  5. Grill the kofta for about 3–4 minutes per side, turning carefully, until cooked to your preferred doneness and at least 160°F for ground beef.
  6. Serve two kofta per person with salad and tzatziki. Add half or one pita, or roasted potatoes, if desired.

Plan note
Using 90% lean beef keeps the kofta rich and satisfying while still fitting the week's high-protein plan.

Nutrition

Approximately 450–500 calories and 40–45 grams protein per serving without pita. With one small pita, plan on roughly 550–600 calories.
