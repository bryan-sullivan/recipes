Kamado BBQ Chicken with Corn & Garlic-Parmesan Green Beans

Your rating
Not rated

Back to the weekly menu · Obsidian note

About 65 minutes; Sunday also needs marinating time. Meat weights are raw unless specified; pasta and rice amounts are dry.

Ingredients

BBQ chicken

  • 3 lb bone-in chicken thighs, skin removed — about 8 small thighs; buy 3–3½ lb before trimming
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ cup mustard-free lower-sugar BBQ sauce
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons water

Corn and green beans

  • 4 ears corn on the cob — husks removed
  • 1½ lb green beans — trimmed
  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic — minced
  • ⅛ cup grated Parmesan — 2 tbsp
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Allow 20–30 minutes to light and stabilize the Classic II at 375–400°F. Arrange the Divide & Conquer system with an indirect side and a direct finishing side. Season chicken with paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  2. Place thighs on the indirect side, close the lid and cook about 30–45 minutes, turning once. Check beside the bone with a thermometer; chicken must reach at least 165°F, and thighs are usually more tender around 175–185°F.
  3. Mix BBQ sauce, cider vinegar and water in a clean bowl. Reserve half for serving before brushing chicken. Move cooked thighs over direct heat and brush with the remaining glaze, turning for 2–4 minutes until tacky. Watch closely to avoid burning.
  4. During the final 20 minutes, toss beans with oil, garlic, salt and pepper. Roast at 425°F for 15–20 minutes, then toss with Parmesan. Grill corn over the direct side for 10–15 minutes, turning until lightly charred and tender; give chicken priority if space is tight.
  5. Serve two small thighs, one corn cob and one quarter of the beans per person, plus reserved sauce. Portions vary with thigh size; the estimate assumes roughly 5 oz cooked skinless meat per person.

Classic II is the easiest fit for the full batch. On the Joe Jr., cook chicken in batches and boil corn indoors for 5–7 minutes; keep the beans in the oven. Indoor backup: roast thighs at 400°F for about 35–50 minutes to the same temperature, glazing in the last 5 minutes.

Source and adaptation

Inspired by Skinnytaste’s glazed BBQ chicken. This version uses skinless bone-in thighs and a Kamado setup, replaces the mustard-containing glaze, and includes corn and garlic-Parmesan beans.
