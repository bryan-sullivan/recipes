Thai-Marinated Steak Salad

Fish sauce, lime, ginger and chile give this salad its sharp, savory backbone. The dressing is reserved before the raw steak touches the marinade.

Ingredients

Steak and marinade

  • 1½ pounds flank steak, trimmed
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1½ tablespoons fish sauce
  • 1 tablespoon light brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon sambal oelek or sriracha

Salad

  • 8 cups chopped romaine
  • 1 large English cucumber, halved and sliced
  • 1 pint cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • ½ small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 1 cup fresh cilantro leaves
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Method

  1. Whisk lime juice, soy sauce, fish sauce, brown sugar, ginger, garlic, sesame oil and sambal. Reserve ¼ cup in a clean container for dressing.
  2. Marinate the flank steak in the remaining mixture for 1–4 hours.
  3. Set the Kamado Joe for two-zone cooking at 450°F. Pat the steak dry and grill over direct heat for 4–6 minutes per side, moving to indirect heat if the exterior darkens too quickly.
  4. For medium-rare, pull at 130–135°F. For USDA guidance, cook whole beef steak to 145°F and rest at least 3 minutes.
  5. Rest the steak 10 minutes, then slice thinly across the grain.
  6. Toss romaine, cucumber, tomatoes, carrots, onion, mint and cilantro with the reserved clean dressing. Top with sliced steak and lime wedges.

Nutrition estimate
Approximately 396 calories and 40 g protein per serving, based on four servings. Brand choice and portion size will change the total.
