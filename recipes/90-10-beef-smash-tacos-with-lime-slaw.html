90/10 Beef Smash Tacos with Lime Slaw & Creamy Jalapeño-Cilantro Sauce

Crisp corn tortillas topped with browned 90% lean beef, crunchy lime slaw and a mild, creamy jalapeño sauce.

Ingredients

Meat weights are raw; vegetable weights are trimmed, edible portions.

Tacos

  • 1½ pounds (680 g) 90% lean ground beef
  • 12 small corn tortillas (about 25 g each)
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¾ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon neutral high-heat oil
  • 2 ounces (57 g) reduced-fat shredded cheese, optional

Lime slaw

  • 12 ounces (340 g) shredded cabbage or plain coleslaw mix
  • 2 tablespoons (30 ml) fresh lime juice
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt

Creamy sauce

  • ½ cup (120 g) light sour cream
  • 1 tablespoon (15 g) light mayonnaise, mustard-free
  • 2 tablespoons (30 g) drained pickled jalapeños, or less to taste
  • ½ cup (15 g) cilantro leaves
  • 1 tablespoon (15 ml) fresh lime juice
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 1–2 tablespoons water, as needed

Method

  1. Toss cabbage with 2 tablespoons lime juice and slaw salt. Blend the sauce ingredients until smooth, adding water a teaspoon at a time. Keep both chilled while cooking.
  2. Divide beef into 12 loose portions, about 57 g (2 ounces) each. Press each portion evenly onto a tortilla in a thin layer all the way to the edge. Mix taco seasonings and sprinkle over the beef.
  3. Heat Blackstone to medium-high, about 400–425°F, or heat a heavy skillet over medium-high heat. Brush on the oil. Lay tacos beef-side down and press with a spatula for full contact.
  4. Cook without moving for 3–4 minutes until browned. Flip and cook tortilla-side down for 1–2 minutes until crisp; cook longer if needed until the thin beef layer reaches 160°F, checking from the side with a thin probe.
  5. If using cheese, sprinkle it on after flipping. Work in batches with room between tacos. Top with drained slaw and sauce; serve three tacos per person immediately.

Keep the flavor beef-forward. Start the sauce with 1 tablespoon pickled jalapeños for a milder family batch, then add more to taste. No Ranch or mustard. Optional cheese adds about 40 calories and 4 g protein per serving.

Nutrition

Approximately 510 calories and 38 g protein per serving, for one quarter of the complete recipe including sides and sauce. Family adaptation estimate; brands and portion sizes vary. Optional toppings are excluded.
