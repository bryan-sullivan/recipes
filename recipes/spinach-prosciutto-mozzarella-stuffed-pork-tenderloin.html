Spinach, Prosciutto & Mozzarella Stuffed Pork Tenderloin + Roasted Vegetables

A rolled pork tenderloin with a spinach-cheese center, served with roasted broccoli and carrots.

Ingredients

Meat weights are raw; vegetable weights are trimmed, edible portions.

Stuffed pork

  • 1½ pounds (680 g) pork tenderloin, silver skin removed
  • 5 ounces (142 g) baby spinach
  • 2 ounces (57 g) thinly sliced prosciutto
  • 3 ounces (85 g) part-skim mozzarella, shredded
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

Roasted vegetables

  • 1 pound (454 g) broccoli florets
  • 1 pound (454 g) carrots, cut into thin diagonal slices
  • 1 tablespoon (15 ml) olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges

Method

  1. Heat oven or Kamado with heat deflectors to 400°F. On the Kamado, use a grill-safe roasting pan; the Classic II has room for the pork and vegetables.
  2. Heat 1 teaspoon oil in a skillet. Stir in garlic for 30 seconds, then wilt spinach. Cool and squeeze out excess liquid.
  3. Butterfly the tenderloin lengthwise without cutting through. Open like a book and pound to an even ½-inch thickness. If using two small tenderloins, divide the filling between them.
  4. Arrange prosciutto, spinach and mozzarella over the pork, leaving a 1-inch border. Roll snugly and tie with kitchen twine every 1½ inches. Season the outside with Italian seasoning, salt and pepper.
  5. Toss broccoli and carrots with their oil, salt and pepper and spread on a sheet pan. Place pork in a separate roasting pan. Roast both for about 30–40 minutes, stirring vegetables halfway and checking pork at 25 minutes. Remove vegetables when tender.
  6. For this stuffed roast, check the center of the filling and thickest meat with a thermometer; cook to 165°F. Time varies with the roll thickness. Rest 5–10 minutes, remove twine and slice into rounds.
  7. Divide pork and vegetables among four plates and squeeze lemon over the vegetables.

Friday prep: wilt and chill the spinach earlier in the day. Keep the filling layer thin so the center cooks evenly. Kitchen twine and an instant-read thermometer are useful here.
