Steak, Green Bean and Potato Salad

Mustard-free by design.

Ingredients

  • 1–1½ pounds cooked London broil, thinly sliced
  • 1½ pounds baby or Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 1 pound green beans, trimmed
  • 8–10 ounces arugula or spring mix
  • 1 shallot, thinly sliced

Shallot-herb vinaigrette

  • 3 tablespoons sherry or red-wine vinegar
  • 1 small shallot, minced
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 5 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Shake the vinaigrette ingredients together and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Simmer the potatoes in salted water until tender, 12–15 minutes.
  3. Add the green beans for the final 3–4 minutes, then drain.
  4. Toss the warm vegetables with one-third of the dressing.
  5. Arrange greens, vegetables and steak on a platter.
  6. Add the remaining vinaigrette and black pepper.
