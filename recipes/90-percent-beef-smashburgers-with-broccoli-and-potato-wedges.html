90% Beef Smashburgers with Broccoli and Potato Wedges

Two thin beef patties per person, a mustard-free burger sauce and absolutely no slaw.

Ingredients

Burgers

  • 1½ pounds 90% lean ground beef
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 4 burger buns or sandwich thins
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese, optional
  • 1 large yellow onion, very thinly sliced
  • Dill pickle chips
  • Shredded lettuce, optional
  • High-heat cooking oil

Mustard-free burger sauce

  • ½ cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons light mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon dill-pickle brine
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
  • Black pepper

Potato wedges

  • 1½ pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Lemon-Parmesan broccoli

  • 1½ pounds broccoli florets
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 1 lemon
  • ¼ cup finely grated Parmesan

Prep

  1. Mix the burger sauce and refrigerate.
  2. Divide the beef into eight loose 3-ounce balls. Do not pack them tightly or mix seasoning into the meat.
  3. Heat the oven to 425°F.

Cook the sides

  1. Toss the potato wedges with oil and seasonings. Roast for 30–40 minutes, turning once.
  2. Toss the broccoli with oil, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Add the broccoli to a second pan—or alongside the potatoes when space permits—for the final 15–20 minutes.
  4. Finish the broccoli with lemon juice and Parmesan.

Smash the burgers

  1. Preheat the Blackstone to medium-high and lightly oil the surface.
  2. Brown the sliced onion in one zone and hold it warm.
  3. Place the beef balls on the hot griddle with plenty of space between them.
  4. Cover each with a small square of parchment and press very thin for about 10 seconds.
  5. Remove the parchment, season the exposed side and cook undisturbed until the edges are deeply browned and lacy, approximately 90 seconds to 2 minutes.
  6. Scrape firmly underneath each patty, flip, season and add cheese if using.
  7. Cook ground beef completely to 160°F.
  8. Stack two patties per bun and add onions, pickles, lettuce and sauce.

Estimated nutrition

Approximately 550–700 calories and 40–45 grams protein per serving, depending on bun, cheese, sauce and side portions.
