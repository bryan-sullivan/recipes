Cherry-Balsamic Pork Tenderloin with Clean-Out Vegetables

A scaled four-person version designed to use the week’s remaining vegetables before Friday’s trip.

Ingredients

Pork

  • 1½ pounds pork tenderloin, silver skin removed
  • ⅓ cup balsamic vinegar
  • ⅓ cup lower-sugar cherry preserves
  • 1 teaspoon chopped fresh thyme, or ⅓ teaspoon dried
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • Olive-oil spray

Clean-out vegetable tray

  • 6–8 cups remaining vegetables, such as broccoli, green beans, potatoes, onions, peppers or zucchini
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Garlic powder or dried thyme

Oven or Kamado setup

  • Oven: Heat to 400°F.
  • Kamado: Set for indirect cooking at 400°F with a heat deflector.

Make the sauce

  1. Bring the balsamic vinegar to a boil in a small saucepan.
  2. Reduce the heat and simmer until reduced by roughly half, about 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the cherry preserves and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Remove from the heat and add the thyme.

Cook

  1. Toss the vegetables with oil and seasonings. Begin roasting dense vegetables such as potatoes 15–20 minutes before quick-cooking vegetables.
  2. Combine garlic powder, salt and pepper and rub it over the pork.
  3. Heat an oven-safe skillet over medium-high and coat it with oil spray.
  4. Brown the tenderloin on all sides.
  5. Spoon half the cherry sauce over the pork and transfer it to the oven or indirect Kamado.
  6. Cook until the thickest part reaches 145°F, generally 10–18 minutes after searing depending on thickness.
  7. Rest loosely covered for at least 5 minutes.
  8. Slice and serve with the remaining sauce and roasted vegetables.

Nutrition

Approximately 240–270 calories and 32–36 grams protein per serving before vegetables.

Travel-week note

  • Buy one tenderloin close to 1½ pounds to avoid leftovers.
  • Any unexpected leftovers can be frozen Thursday night rather than left refrigerated during the trip.
