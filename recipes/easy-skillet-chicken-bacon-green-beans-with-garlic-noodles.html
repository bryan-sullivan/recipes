Easy Skillet Chicken with Bacon & Green Beans plus Garlic Egg Noodles

Quick chicken cutlets and green beans finished with bacon, plus lightly buttered garlic noodles.

Serves 4 · Electric stovetop · About 25 minutes. Ingredient meat weights are raw, and rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note

Ingredients

  • 1¾ lb thin boneless skinless chicken breast cutlets
  • 1¼ lb green beans, trimmed
  • 4 slices center-cut bacon, chopped
  • 1 shallot, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 6 oz dry egg noodles
  • ½ cup low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 tsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley (optional)

Method

  1. Cook egg noodles in salted water according to package directions; reserve ¼ cup pasta water and drain.
  2. Meanwhile crisp chopped bacon in a large skillet 4–5 minutes; remove to a plate. Add olive oil as needed.
  3. Season chicken and cook in skillet 3–5 minutes per side until 165°F; remove. Add shallot, green beans and broth; cover and simmer 5–7 minutes.
  4. Stir in half the garlic, return chicken and bacon, and heat together 1 minute.
  5. Toss noodles with butter, remaining garlic, a splash of reserved water and parsley. Serve alongside chicken and beans.

Use pre-trimmed beans and thin-sliced chicken to stay near 25 minutes; stovetop only.
