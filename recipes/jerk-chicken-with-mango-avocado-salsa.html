Jerk Chicken with Mango-Avocado Salsa

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons mild prepared jerk seasoning
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon brown sugar
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt

Salsa

  • 1 large mango, diced
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • ¼ cup diced red onion
  • 1 seeded and diced jalapeño
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 3 tablespoons chopped cilantro
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt

Coconut cauliflower rice

  • 1½ pounds cauliflower rice
  • ⅓ cup light coconut milk
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • Salt and lime juice

Method

  1. Coat the chicken with oil, jerk seasoning, garlic, sugar and salt. Refrigerate 30 minutes to 24 hours.
  2. Combine the salsa shortly before serving.
  3. Grill the chicken over direct medium-high heat, turning periodically, until it reaches 165°F. Rest 5 minutes.
  4. Cook the cauliflower rice until its moisture evaporates. Add coconut milk and scallions; cook until absorbed. Season with salt and lime.

Nutrition

The chicken and salsa provide approximately 327 calories and 35 grams protein per serving, before the cauliflower rice.
