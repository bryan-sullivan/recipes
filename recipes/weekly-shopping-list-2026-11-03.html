Wegmans Grocery List — November 3–7, 2026

Five dinners for four · Weekly menu · Live household list

Shopping ahead: freeze fresh cod immediately if purchasing more than 1–2 days before Thursday; thaw under refrigeration Wednesday. Check pantry and any fish or vegetables you already have before buying.

Produce

  • ☐ green beans — 1¼ lb (Tue)
  • ☐ yellow or white onions — 2 medium/large (Tue ½ onion, Wed 1 large; reserve remaining)
  • ☐ bell peppers — 1 large red and 1 large yellow (Wed)
  • ☐ garlic — 3 bulbs (about 19 cloves across Tue–Sat)
  • ☐ lemons — 4 (Tue beans, Thu fish, Fri pork vegetables/couscous, Sat bowls)
  • ☐ fresh basil — 1 small bunch, at least ¼ cup leaves (Tue)
  • ☐ fresh cilantro — 1 small bunch; 2 tbsp (Wed)
  • ☐ scallions — 1 (Wed)
  • ☐ small avocado — 1 (Wed)
  • ☐ russet or Yukon Gold potatoes — 2 lb for oven wedges (Thu)
  • ☐ fresh dill — 1 small bunch; 1 tbsp chopped (Thu)
  • ☐ zucchini — 2 medium, about 12 oz total (Fri)
  • ☐ yellow summer squash — 2 medium, about 12 oz total (Fri)
  • ☐ grape tomatoes — 1 pint (Fri)
  • ☐ fresh flat-leaf parsley — 1 large bunch, at least 1 cup leaves (Fri/Sat)
  • ☐ sweet potatoes — 1½ lb (Sat)
  • ☐ Brussels sprouts — 1½ lb (Sat)
  • ☐ baby arugula — 4 oz (Sat)

Meat

  • ☐ bone-in skin-on chicken thighs — 4 large thighs, ~2½–3 lb (Tue)
  • ☐ boneless skinless chicken breasts — 1½ lb (Wed)
  • ☐ skinless cod fillets — 2 lb (Thu; frozen is fine)
  • ☐ thin boneless center-cut pork chops — 8 chops, ~2 lb total (Fri)
  • ☐ lean sirloin steak — 1½ lb (Sat)

Dairy

  • ☐ grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano — ¼ cup (Tue)
  • ☐ light sour cream — ½ cup (Wed)
  • ☐ large eggs — 2 (Thu breading)
  • ☐ plain nonfat Greek yogurt — ½ cup (Thu tartar)
  • ☐ light mayonnaise — 2 tbsp (Thu tartar)
  • ☐ crumbled feta — ¼ cup (Fri)

Bread, pasta and rice

  • ☐ dry orzo pasta — 1 cup / about 7 oz (Tue)
  • ☐ dry penne or other short pasta — 8 oz (Wed)
  • ☐ plain panko breadcrumbs — 1 cup (Thu)
  • ☐ plain couscous — ¾ cup dry (Fri)

Canned and jarred

  • ☐ tomato paste — ½ cup, buy 1 x 6-oz can (Tue)
  • ☐ mild diced tomatoes and green chiles — 1 x 10-oz Ro-Tel can (Wed)
  • ☐ pitted Kalamata olives — ¼ cup (Fri)

Sauces and condiments

  • ☐ low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth — 6¼ cups total, buy 2 x 32-fl-oz cartons (Tue 3c, Wed 2¼c, Fri 1c); plus optional splash
  • ☐ dill pickles — ¼ cup finely chopped (Thu tartar)
  • ☐ red wine vinegar — 3 tbsp (Sat chimichurri)

Pantry check

  • ☐ olive oil — about ½–¾ cup across the week; check supply
  • ☐ avocado oil (optional) — 1 tsp (Sat steak; olive oil works)
  • ☐ all-purpose flour — ¼ cup (Thu)
  • ☐ ground cumin — 2 tsp total, includes Wed and Sat
  • ☐ paprika — 1 tsp (Wed)
  • ☐ smoked paprika — 1 tsp (Thu fish)
  • ☐ chili powder (optional for mild family meals) — about 1 tsp (Wed/Sat)
  • ☐ garlic powder — about 2 tsp (Wed/Thu/Fri)
  • ☐ dried oregano — 1 tsp (Fri)
  • ☐ kosher salt and black pepper — to taste

Frozen

  • ☐ frozen green peas — 1 lb (Thu)
