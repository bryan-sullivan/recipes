Split Shrimp & Chicken Fra Diavolo with Broccolini and High-Protein Pasta

One spicy tomato pasta with two separately cooked proteins, so the shrimp and chicken stay distinct while everyone eats the same dinner.

Ingredients

Pasta and sauce

  • 12 ounces (340 g) high-protein pasta
  • 1 large bunch broccolini (about 12 ounces / 340 g), cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil, divided
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • ½–1 teaspoon red pepper flakes, to taste
  • ½ cup dry white wine or low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 can (28 ounces / 794 g) crushed tomatoes
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ cup chopped flat-leaf parsley or basil

Shrimp portion

  • 12 ounces (340 g) large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Black pepper

Chicken portion

  • 1 pound (454 g) boneless, skinless chicken breast or tenderloins, cut into bite-size pieces
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon Italian seasoning

Tip
Use separate tongs and plates for the shrimp and chicken. Divide the finished sauce between the proteins only after both are cooked.

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta until just shy of al dente. Add the broccolini for the final 2 minutes. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain.
  2. Season the shrimp with salt and pepper. Season the chicken separately with salt, pepper and Italian seasoning.
  3. Heat half the oil in one skillet over medium-high. Cook the shrimp for 1–2 minutes per side until opaque and 145°F; transfer to its own bowl. In a separate skillet, or after washing the first skillet and utensils, cook the chicken in the remaining oil for 5–7 minutes until 165°F; transfer to a separate bowl.
  4. In a clean large skillet or Dutch oven, soften the garlic and red pepper flakes for 30 seconds. Add the wine or broth and reduce by half. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Toss the pasta and broccolini with the sauce, adding reserved pasta water as needed. Divide the sauced pasta between serving bowls.
  6. Top Bryan’s portions with shrimp and Desiree’s portions with chicken. Keep the serving utensils separate and finish both with parsley or basil.

Two-protein setup
The sauce and pasta are shared, but the proteins never have to touch. Chicken also works for anyone else who prefers it.

Nutrition

Approximately ~480–520 calories and ~45–52 g protein per serving. Nutrition varies with brands and serving choices.
