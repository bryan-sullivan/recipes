Roasted Garlic Chicken Parmesan Bowls

Chicken Parmesan comfort in a lighter bowl: seasoned chicken, garlicky marinara, vegetables, modest pasta and controlled cheese.

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1½ pounds chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 8 ounces protein pasta or regular pasta
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 6 cloves roasted garlic
  • ¾ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 pound broccoli florets or sliced zucchini
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh basil, for finishing

Tip
For a lighter bowl, use 6 ounces pasta total and add more roasted vegetables.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Season the chicken with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Toss the broccoli or zucchini with 1 teaspoon olive oil and roast until browned and tender.
  4. Roast or sauté the chicken with the remaining teaspoon oil until it reaches 165°F.
  5. Mash the roasted garlic into the marinara and warm gently.
  6. Cook the pasta according to package directions and drain.
  7. Divide the pasta among four bowls. Top with vegetables, chicken, garlicky marinara, mozzarella and Parmesan.
  8. If using oven-safe bowls, broil for 2–3 minutes to melt and brown the cheese. Finish with fresh basil.

Plan note
This delivers the flavor of chicken Parmesan without breading or an oversized pasta portion.

Nutrition

Approximately 525–575 calories and 50–55 grams protein per serving. Protein pasta may raise the protein total; brands and cheese quantities will affect the estimate.
