Grilled Chicken with Mango-Black Bean Salad

Smoky grilled chicken, juicy mango and black beans make a fast, colorful dinner. A smaller serving of rice keeps the plate satisfying while preserving the lighter nutrition target.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1¼ pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts, pounded to an even ½-inch thickness
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Mango-black bean salad and rice

  • 1 large ripe mango, diced
  • 1 can (15 ounces) black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • ½ small red onion, finely diced
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
  • ½ cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 2 limes, zested and juiced
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1½ cups cooked brown rice

Method

  1. Combine mango, black beans, bell pepper, red onion, jalapeño and cilantro. Toss with lime zest, lime juice and honey; season lightly with salt.
  2. Rub chicken with olive oil, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Set the Kamado Joe for direct cooking at 400–425°F. Clean and oil the grate.
  4. Grill chicken 4–6 minutes per side until marked and 165°F in the thickest portion. Rest 5 minutes.
  5. Divide warm brown rice among four plates. Slice the chicken and serve with the mango-black bean salad.

Nutrition estimate
Approximately 360 calories and 33 g protein per serving, based on four servings. Brand choice and portion size will change the total.
