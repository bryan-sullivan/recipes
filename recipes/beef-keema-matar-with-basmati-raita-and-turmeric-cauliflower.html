Beef Keema Matar with Basmati Rice, Cucumber Raita & Turmeric Cauliflower

Your rating
Not rated

90/10 beef simmered in warming Indian spices with green peas, a modest rice portion and cooling raita.

Serves 4 · Electric stovetop + oven · About 45 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb 90/10 ground beef
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup frozen green peas
  • ¾ cup dry basmati rice
  • 1 large cauliflower (about 1½ lb florets)
  • 1 cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • ½ English cucumber, finely chopped
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro, divided
  • 4 tsp olive oil, divided
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1¼ tsp ground cumin, divided
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric, divided
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp Kashmiri chili powder (optional)
  • ½ lemon, juice
  • ½ tsp kosher salt, plus to taste
  • Optional: 2 tbsp chopped fresh mint for raita

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss cauliflower with 2 tsp olive oil, ½ tsp turmeric and a pinch of salt. Roast 22–28 minutes, turning halfway.
  2. Rinse ¾ cup basmati rice. Cook according to package directions (typically with about 1½ cups water), yielding about 2 cups cooked rice for four ½-cup portions.
  3. Heat remaining 2 tsp olive oil in a skillet. Cook onion until golden, 4–5 minutes. Add garlic and ginger for 1 minute. Add beef, breaking it up until browned; drain extra fat if needed.
  4. Stir in garam masala, 1 tsp cumin, remaining ½ tsp turmeric, coriander, chili if using and salt. Add canned tomatoes with their juices; simmer 10–12 minutes until beef is fully cooked (160°F). Fold in peas for last 3–4 minutes.
  5. Mix yogurt, diced cucumber, ¼ tsp cumin, mint if using and a squeeze of lemon. Top keema with cilantro and remaining lemon. Serve with ½ cup cooked rice, roasted cauliflower and raita.

Matar means peas. This is a beef adaptation of Swasthi's minced-meat keema recipe; make chili optional and let spices bloom before adding the tomatoes.
