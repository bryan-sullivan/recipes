Parmesan-Crusted Baked Cod with Lemon Rice & Roasted Asparagus

Crisp parmesan-panko cod with bright lemon rice and roasted asparagus.

Serves 4 · Oven + stovetop · About 35 minutes. Ingredient meat weights are raw, and rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note

Ingredients

  • 2 lb cod fillets (four portions)
  • ⅓ cup grated Parmesan
  • ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1¼ lb asparagus, trimmed
  • ¾ cup dry jasmine or long-grain rice
  • 1½ cups low-sodium chicken broth or water
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil, divided
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Cook rice with broth or water according to package directions.
  2. Pat cod very dry. Mix panko, Parmesan, garlic, lemon zest, Italian seasoning and 2 tsp olive oil. Season fish lightly; press crumbs onto the top.
  3. Toss asparagus with 1 tsp oil, salt and pepper; spread on a baking sheet. Place cod on a separate lined tray; drizzle with remaining oil.
  4. Bake cod 12–16 minutes until it flakes and reaches 145°F; roast asparagus 10–14 minutes, turning once.
  5. Fluff rice with lemon juice and parsley. Plate cod with rice and asparagus.

Buy center-cut, similarly thick cod portions for even cooking. No shrimp or salmon.
