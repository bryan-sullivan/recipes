Blackstone Garlic Steak Bites with Crispy Potatoes & Green Beans

Lean sirloin bites seared hot with Worcestershire, butter and roasted garlic, served with crisp potatoes and blistered green beans.

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • 1½ pounds lean sirloin steak, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1½ pounds baby Yukon Gold potatoes
  • ¾–1 pound green beans, trimmed
  • 4 cloves roasted garlic
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • Chopped fresh parsley, optional

Tip
Par-cooking the potatoes is what makes a weeknight Blackstone cook possible; dry them well before they hit the griddle.

Method

  1. Microwave or parboil the potatoes until barely tender, 6–8 minutes. Halve them or lightly smash them, then pat dry.
  2. Season the steak with salt, pepper, smoked paprika and garlic powder.
  3. Preheat one Blackstone zone to medium and another to medium-high.
  4. Add 1 teaspoon oil to the medium zone. Cook the potatoes cut-side down for 8–12 minutes, turning occasionally, until deeply browned.
  5. Add the remaining teaspoon oil and green beans. Cook until blistered but still crisp, then move the vegetables to the cooler zone.
  6. Spread the steak over the hot zone in one layer. Leave undisturbed for about 90 seconds, flip and cook for another 1–2 minutes.
  7. Add butter, Worcestershire and roasted garlic during the final minute. Toss quickly and serve immediately, garnished with parsley if desired.

Plan note
Do not crowd the steak. Cook in two batches if necessary so it sears rather than steams.

Nutrition

Approximately 500–550 calories and 45–50 grams protein per serving. The estimate assumes lean sirloin and four equal servings.
