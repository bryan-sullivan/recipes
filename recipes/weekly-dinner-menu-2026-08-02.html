Recipes

  • Monday: Leftover Chopped Chimichurri Steak Salad

  • Wednesday: 90 Percent Beef Smashburgers with Broccoli and Potato Wedges

Weekly strategy

  • Sunday makes Monday’s steak and chimichurri ahead of the board meeting.
  • Monday and Tuesday intentionally share cucumber, tomato, onion, lemon and herbs.
  • Tuesday uses regular basmati rice—not cauliflower rice.
  • Wednesday uses 90% lean ground beef and has no slaw.
  • Thursday uses remaining vegetables so the refrigerator is nearly empty before Friday’s trip.

Prep timeline

Saturday or Sunday morning

  • Make the chimichurri.
  • Wash and dry Sunday’s potatoes and green beans.

Sunday after dinner

  • Refrigerate approximately 1¼ pounds cooked steak, preferably unsliced.
  • Reserve ½ cup untouched chimichurri in a separate container.
  • Dice Monday’s cucumbers, tomatoes and onion; keep them separate from the dressing.

Monday night

  • Marinate Tuesday’s chicken in feta brine.
  • Cook basmati rice now or on Tuesday.

Thursday

  • Inventory remaining broccoli, green beans, potatoes, onions, peppers and zucchini.
  • Roast the usable assortment alongside the pork.
