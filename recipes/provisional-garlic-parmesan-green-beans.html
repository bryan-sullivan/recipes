Provisional Garlic-Parmesan Green Beans (Instagram Reel Pending)

A practical oven green-bean side for Friday's turkey. The linked Instagram reel could not be viewed, so this is NOT a transcription of that recipe.

Electric oven · About 25 minutes · 4–6 side servings. Meat weights are raw unless stated otherwise; dry rice/pasta quantities are uncooked.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note · Shopping list

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb fresh green beans, trimmed and thoroughly dried
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • ¼ cup finely grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 lemon, juice and zest
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Method

  1. Heat oven to 425°F while turkey rests. Place dried, trimmed green beans on a sheet pan with olive oil, salt and pepper; toss to coat in a single layer.
  2. Roast 10 minutes; stir once and add minced garlic. Return to oven for 4–7 minutes, until tender-crisp and lightly blistered (do not let garlic burn).
  3. Remove from oven, finish with grated Parmesan and fresh lemon zest and juice; toss while hot.
  4. Serve alongside the rotisserie turkey, mashed potatoes and gravy. If the original Instagram recipe's ingredients become available, replace this clearly labeled provisional side with its actual method.
