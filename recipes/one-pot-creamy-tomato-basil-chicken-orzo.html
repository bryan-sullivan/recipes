One-Pot Creamy Tomato-Basil Chicken Orzo

A low-fuss stovetop dinner with tender chicken, tomato-basil orzo, spinach and a creamy finish from Greek yogurt instead of heavy cream.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¾ teaspoon kosher salt, divided
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil

Creamy tomato-basil orzo

  • 1 small yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 can (14½ ounces) fire-roasted diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 1 cup dry orzo
  • 2½ cups low-sodium chicken broth, plus more if needed
  • 3 cups baby spinach
  • ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt, at room temperature
  • ¼ cup finely grated Parmesan
  • ½ cup packed fresh basil leaves, sliced
  • Red pepper flakes, optional

No-curdle finish
Let the Greek yogurt warm toward room temperature and stir it in after the pan leaves the heat. Boiling yogurt can make the sauce grainy.

Method

  1. Season the chicken with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, ½ teaspoon salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high. Brown the chicken for 5–6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until nearly cooked through. Transfer to a clean plate.
  3. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the onion and cook for 3 minutes. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste; cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the diced tomatoes, orzo, broth and remaining ¼ teaspoon salt. Scrape up the browned bits and bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Cook uncovered for 8 minutes, stirring frequently so the orzo does not stick. Return the chicken and any juices to the pan, then simmer for 3–5 minutes more until the orzo is tender and the chicken reaches 165°F. Add a splash of broth if the pan becomes dry.
  6. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Remove the pan from the heat and let it stand for 2 minutes.
  7. Stir in the Greek yogurt, Parmesan and basil. Taste, adjust the seasoning and add red pepper flakes if desired.

Thursday insurance
Cut the chicken and onion ahead if the evening will be hectic. From there, everything cooks in one pan in about 25 minutes.

Nutrition

Approximately 475 calories and 50 grams protein per serving. The estimate assumes nonfat Greek yogurt, 1 cup dry orzo and four equal servings.
