Blackstone Steak Fajitas with Peppers, Onions & Lime Crema

Fast, colorful steak fajitas with Greek-yogurt lime crema, pico de gallo and warm tortillas.

Serves 4 · Blackstone 36-inch griddle · About 35 minutes. Ingredient meat weights are raw, and rice and pasta weights are dry.

Weekly menu · Obsidian note

Ingredients

  • 1¾ lb flank steak or top sirloin, sliced against grain
  • 3 bell peppers (mixed colors), sliced
  • 2 yellow onions, sliced
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 3 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • ½ red onion, finely diced
  • 1 jalapeño, minced (optional)
  • 1 bunch cilantro, divided
  • 3 limes
  • ¾ cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus to taste

Method

  1. Toss sliced steak with cumin, paprika, chili powder, oregano, half the garlic, half the oil and juice of 1 lime; rest 10–20 minutes.
  2. Mix yogurt, juice of ½ lime, pinch of salt and 1 tbsp chopped cilantro for crema. Combine tomatoes, red onion, jalapeño, 2 tbsp cilantro and juice of ½ lime for pico.
  3. Preheat Blackstone medium-high. Cook peppers and yellow onions with remaining oil 8–10 minutes until charred-tender; shift aside.
  4. Cook steak in a hot single layer 2–4 minutes per side until appropriately cooked (whole beef steaks 145°F with 3-minute rest); avoid overcrowding. Warm tortillas on the cooler side.
  5. Serve steak and peppers in tortillas with pico, lime crema and wedges from remaining lime.

Keep tortillas to 2–3 per serving for a lighter plate. Cut steak against the grain.
