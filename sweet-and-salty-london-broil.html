Sweet and Salty London Broil

Serves: 4 plus leftovers · Vietnamese-inspired

Smoky grilled beef with a salty-sweet, garlicky marinade, crisp cucumber-herb salad and jasmine rice.

Ingredients

Beef

  • 2½–3 pounds London broil
  • 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar or honey
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, grated or minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 1 teaspoon chili-garlic sauce or sambal, optional
  • Black pepper

Cucumber-herb salad

  • 2 English cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 1 shallot, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro
  • ¼ cup torn mint
  • 3 tablespoons rice vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon honey or sugar
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt

To serve

  • 2 cups dry jasmine rice, cooked
  • Lime wedges
  • Sliced scallions, optional

Instructions

Tonight

  1. Check that the beef has remained at 40°F or colder and is within its use-by date.
  2. Combine the marinade ingredients.
  3. Marinate the beef under refrigeration for 4–12 hours.

Cook

  1. Set the Classic II for direct cooking at 450–500°F.
  2. Remove the beef from the marinade and blot away excess surface moisture.
  3. Grill over direct heat, turning for even browning. Use temperature rather than time to judge doneness.
  4. For USDA guidance, cook the center to at least 145°F and rest for at least 3 minutes.
  5. Rest the steak for 10 minutes. Identify the grain and slice very thinly across it.
  6. Toss the cucumber salad immediately before serving.
  7. Reserve about 1½ pounds of cooked steak for Monday - Steak Green Bean and Potato Salad.

Notes

Serving

Serve 5–6 ounces sliced beef with cucumber salad and ½–¾ cup cooked rice.

Storage

Refrigerate the reserved cooked steak promptly in a shallow sealed container. Use within 3–4 days.

Source

Inspired by NYT Cooking's Sweet and Salty Grilled Steak With Cucumber Salad, adapted for London broil and the Kamado Joe.
