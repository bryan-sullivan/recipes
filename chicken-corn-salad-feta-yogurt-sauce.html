Chicken and Corn Salad with Feta and Yogurt Sauce

A high-protein chicken and charred corn salad with feta or cotija and a 0% Greek yogurt lime sauce.

Source: Louis Howard | Online fitness coach from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

4 servings

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 800 g chicken breast, diced

Marinade

  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Salad

  • 2 cups sweetcorn, about 2 drained tins
  • 1 large red onion, finely diced
  • 1 large red bell pepper, finely diced
  • 3 jalapenos, finely diced
  • 1 handful fresh coriander, chopped
  • 50 g cotija cheese or feta, crumbled

Sauce

  • 250 g 0% Greek yogurt
  • Juice of 1/2 lime
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce
  • 1 tsp Tajin seasoning
  • 120 ml water
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Add chicken, lime juice, avocado oil, chilli powder, cumin, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper to a bowl.
  2. Mix well and marinate for at least 15 to 30 minutes.
  3. Drain the sweetcorn and pat dry with kitchen roll.
  4. Heat a large pan over high heat and char the sweetcorn until slightly blackened. Set aside to cool.
  5. Cook the chicken in a large frying pan until fully cooked through and slightly browned.
  6. In a large bowl, combine sweetcorn, red onion, red pepper, jalapenos, coriander, and cheese.
  7. In a separate bowl, add Greek yogurt, lime juice, hot sauce, Tajin, water, salt, and black pepper. Mix well.
  8. Pour the sauce over the salad mixture and stir well.
  9. Add the cooked chicken and mix everything well.
  10. Serve immediately or divide into meal prep containers.

WW Notes

This is one of the most WW-friendly recipes in the batch. To reduce points further, use less avocado oil, reduce cheese slightly, and use nonfat Greek yogurt.
