Crispy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Wraps

Low-carb wraps filled with crispy garlic parmesan chicken, lettuce, tomato, onion, and creamy garlic parmesan sauce.

Source: Jalal from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

10 wraps

Ingredients

Garlic Parmesan Chicken

  • 600 g raw boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into cubes
  • 600 g raw chicken breast, cut into cubes
  • 2.5 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 3 tsp parsley
  • 4 tsp Italian herbs
  • 3 tsp paprika
  • 4 tsp garlic granules or powder
  • 200 g grated Parmesan
  • 6 tsp olive oil
  • Avocado cooking spray, for baking

Garlic Parmesan Creamy Sauce

  • 350 g light evaporated milk
  • 2 tsp Italian herbs
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 80 g grated Parmesan
  • 100 g grated Parmesan
  • 15 g light butter

Wraps and Filling

  • 10 low-carb wraps, such as Mission
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Chopped tomatoes
  • Chopped onions

Instructions

  1. Slice chicken breast and thighs into cubes.
  2. Add seasonings, grated Parmesan cheese, and olive oil. Mix until well coated.
  3. In a pan over low heat, add light butter and seasonings. Toast for about 1 minute.
  4. Add evaporated milk, extra Parmesan, and cream cheese if using. Stir until smooth and creamy.
  5. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper and spread marinated chicken evenly without overcrowding.
  6. Spray lightly with avocado cooking spray.
  7. Bake in a preheated 200°C / 400°F oven for 20 to 22 minutes, until crispy and cooked through.
  8. While the chicken is still hot, add extra Parmesan and parsley. Mix until coated and cheesy.
  9. Assemble wraps with shredded lettuce, onion, tomato, crispy chicken, and creamy sauce.
  10. Store chicken and sauce separately. Reheat chicken in an oven or air fryer for 4 to 5 minutes at 180°C / 370°F.

WW Notes

Biggest point drivers: Parmesan, olive oil, light butter, evaporated milk, and wraps. For lower points, use more chicken breast than thighs, reduce Parmesan, use less sauce per wrap, and load up lettuce/tomato/onion.
