Caramelized Onion Chicken Pasta

Garlic herb chicken thighs served with spaghetti or linguine in a creamy caramelized onion, sundried tomato, parmesan, and light cream cheese sauce.

Source: Jalal from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

6 servings

Ingredients

Garlic Herb Chicken

  • 1200 g raw boneless chicken thighs
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 4 tsp spicy Italian seasoning, such as Schwartz
  • 3 tsp parsley
  • 4 tsp garlic granules or garlic powder
  • 4 tsp paprika
  • 6 tsp or 30 g olive oil

Caramelized Onions

  • 200 g red onion, thinly sliced
  • 200 g white onion, thinly sliced
  • 100 g sundried tomatoes, drained and chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 whole garlic bulb, top sliced off
  • 10 seconds avocado oil spray

Creamy Pasta Sauce

  • 400 to 450 g light evaporated milk or regular milk
  • 120 g light cream cheese
  • 90 to 100 g grated parmesan
  • 20 to 25 g dark soy sauce
  • 1 to 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 350 g spaghetti or linguine, dry weight

Instructions

  1. Slice chicken thighs into cubes. Add seasoning and olive oil, mix well, then set aside.
  2. Slice red and white onion. Add to a large oven dish with chopped drained sundried tomatoes, seasonings, and avocado oil spray. Mix well.
  3. Place the whole garlic bulb in the center, then cover with foil.
  4. Oven bake for 45 minutes at 190 C / 380 F, checking halfway. The onions should be golden and caramelized.
  5. Squeeze in the roasted garlic and mix well. While the dish is still hot, add parsley, soy sauce, parmesan, milk, and cream cheese.
  6. Stir continuously until melted and creamy. Place back in the oven on low temperature if needed to thicken the sauce slightly.
  7. Add cooked pasta and fold gently until coated.
  8. Spread chicken on a lined large sheet pan. Oven bake for 18 minutes at 200 C / 400 F, then broil for 5 extra minutes until golden and crispy.
  9. Serve the garlic herb chicken with the caramelized onion pasta.

WW Notes

Delicious but not light as written. For a lower-point version, use chicken breast or trimmed thighs, reduce olive oil, use light evaporated milk, reduce parmesan, and increase the serving count.
