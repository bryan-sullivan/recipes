Leftover Chopped Chimichurri Steak Salad

Serves: 4 · Argentine-inspired

A board-meeting-night dinner assembled from Sunday's steak and reserved chimichurri. No lettuce means nothing gets soggy while you are out.

Ingredients

  • 1¼ pounds cooked steak from Kamado Chimichurri Steak with Potatoes and Green Beans
  • 1½ cups diced mini or English cucumber
  • 1½ cups halved cherry tomatoes
  • 1 large avocado, diced
  • ¼ cup thinly sliced red onion
  • ½ cup reserved chimichurri
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Optional: microwaveable rice, up to 1 cooked cup per person

Instructions

Sunday prep

  1. Dice the cucumber and onion and halve the tomatoes.
  2. Refrigerate the vegetables separately or together in a sealed container.
  3. Leave the avocado whole until Monday.

Monday assembly

  1. Take the steak and reserved chimichurri from the refrigerator 20–30 minutes before eating, if convenient.
  2. Thinly slice or dice the steak across the grain.
  3. Toss the cucumber, tomatoes, onion and salt with half the chimichurri and lemon juice.
  4. Fold in the steak and remaining chimichurri.
  5. Add the avocado immediately before serving.
  6. Serve alone or over reheated rice.

Notes

Nutrition

  • Salad alone: approximately 395 calories and 35 grams protein per serving.
  • Add approximately 200 calories for 1 cup cooked rice.
