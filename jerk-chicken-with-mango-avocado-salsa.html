Jerk Chicken with Mango-Avocado Salsa

Serves: 4 · Jamaican-inspired

Bold jerk-seasoned chicken balanced by a cool fruit salsa, served with coconut cauliflower rice.

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 1½ pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons mild prepared jerk seasoning
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon brown sugar
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Cooking spray or neutral oil

Mango-avocado salsa

  • 1 large mango, diced
  • 1 medium avocado, diced
  • ¼ cup finely diced red onion
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and diced
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 3 tablespoons chopped cilantro
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt

Coconut cauliflower rice

  • 1½ pounds frozen cauliflower rice
  • ⅓ cup light coconut milk
  • 2 sliced scallions
  • Salt and lime juice to taste

Instructions

Prep

  1. Coat the chicken with oil, jerk seasoning, garlic, brown sugar and salt.
  2. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes or up to 24 hours.
  3. Combine the salsa ingredients shortly before serving.

Cook

  1. Set the Joe Jr. for direct, medium-high cooking.
  2. Grill the chicken, turning every few minutes, until browned and the thickest pieces reach 165°F. Total time will usually be 10–15 minutes.
  3. Rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Cook the cauliflower rice in a large skillet until excess moisture evaporates.
  5. Stir in the coconut milk and scallions. Cook until absorbed; season with salt and lime.

Notes

Nutrition

The Skinnytaste chicken and salsa provide approximately 327 calories and 35 grams protein per serving. The cauliflower rice adds roughly 60–90 calories.
