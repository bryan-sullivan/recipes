Steak, Green Bean and Potato Salad

Serves: 4 · French-inspired

A substantial leftover-steak salad with crisp green beans, creamy potatoes and a bright mustard-free shallot–herb vinaigrette.

Ingredients

Salad

  • 1–1½ pounds cooked London broil, thinly sliced
  • 1½ pounds baby or Yukon Gold potatoes, halved or quartered
  • 1 pound green beans, trimmed
  • 8–10 ounces arugula or spring mix
  • 1 shallot, thinly sliced
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Mustard-free vinaigrette

  • 3 tablespoons sherry vinegar or red-wine vinegar
  • 1 small shallot, finely minced
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 5 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Instructions

Prep

  1. Shake all vinaigrette ingredients together in a jar.
  2. Let the dressing stand for at least 10 minutes so the shallot softens.

Cook and assemble

  1. Cover the potatoes with salted cold water. Bring to a simmer and cook until easily pierced, about 12–15 minutes.
  2. Add the green beans during the final 3–4 minutes. Drain both well.
  3. While warm, toss the potatoes and beans with one-third of the vinaigrette.
  4. Bring the leftover steak just to room temperature, serve it cold, or warm it very briefly so it does not overcook.
  5. Arrange the greens, dressed vegetables, steak and sliced shallot on a platter.
  6. Spoon over the remaining vinaigrette and finish with black pepper.

Notes

Optional additions

  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Chopped cornichons
  • Crumbled feta or goat cheese

Estimated nutrition

Approximately 450–500 calories and 35–40 grams protein per serving, depending on the steak portion and dressing used.
