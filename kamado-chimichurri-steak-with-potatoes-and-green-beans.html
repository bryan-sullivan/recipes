Kamado Chimichurri Steak with Potatoes and Green Beans

Serves: 4 plus Monday leftovers · Argentine-inspired

A generous Sunday steak cook designed to become Leftover Chopped Chimichurri Steak Salad with almost no work Monday night.

Ingredients

Steak

  • 3 pounds sirloin, strip steak or flank steak, trimmed
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • High-heat cooking spray or a small amount of neutral oil

Chimichurri

  • 2 packed cups fresh parsley leaves
  • 6 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
  • ¼ cup red-wine vinegar
  • ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon crushed red-pepper flakes, or less to taste
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil

Potatoes and green beans

  • 2 pounds baby or Yukon Gold potatoes, halved
  • 1½ pounds green beans, trimmed
  • 1½ tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Lemon wedges

Instructions

Make the chimichurri

  1. Pulse the parsley, garlic, vinegar, lemon juice, pepper flakes, salt and pepper in a small food processor until finely chopped.
  2. With the machine running, drizzle in the olive oil. Keep some texture in the sauce.
  3. Before handling raw beef, refrigerate ½ cup in a clean container for Monday.

Prepare the sides

  1. Parboil the potatoes in salted water for 8–10 minutes, until the outside is tender but the centers remain firm. Drain and dry well.
  2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tablespoon oil, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Toss the green beans separately with the remaining oil, salt and pepper.

Kamado setup

  1. Set the Classic II for two-zone cooking around 450–500°F, with a direct side and a cooler side.
  2. Grill the potatoes in a basket or cast-iron pan until browned and tender, approximately 12–18 minutes.
  3. Grill the green beans until blistered but still crisp, approximately 5–8 minutes.

Grill the steak

  1. Season the steak with salt and pepper 30–45 minutes before grilling.
  2. Sear over direct heat, turning as needed for an even crust. Move thicker pieces to the cooler side to finish.
  3. Use an instant-read thermometer rather than relying on time. USDA guidance for whole beef cuts is 145°F followed by at least a 3-minute rest.
  4. Rest the steak for 10 minutes.
  5. Slice only what is needed for Sunday, cutting thinly across the grain.

Notes

Serve and reserve

  • Serve about 5–6 ounces steak per person with potatoes, green beans and chimichurri.
  • Refrigerate approximately 1¼ pounds cooked steak for Monday, preferably as a whole unsliced piece.
  • Refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours.

Estimated nutrition

Approximately 500–600 calories and 40–45 grams protein per serving, depending on steak cut, potato portion and chimichurri.

Source

Chimichurri proportions adapted and doubled from Skinnytaste's Chopped Chimichurri Steak Salad.
