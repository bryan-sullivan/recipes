Crispy Rice Paper Cuban Sandwich Spiral

A Cuban sandwich-inspired rice paper spiral with egg, ham, mustard, pickles, cheese, and seasoning baked until golden.

Source: Josh Gale from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

1 spiral

Ingredients

  • Rice paper sheets
  • Eggs, whisked
  • Ham slices
  • Mustard
  • Diced pickles
  • Shredded cheese
  • Spice blend

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs in a pan or shallow dish.
  2. Dip rice paper sheets into the egg mixture.
  3. Lay the egg-soaked rice paper on a board.
  4. Layer ham slices on the rice paper.
  5. Drizzle mustard over the ham.
  6. Sprinkle diced pickles on top.
  7. Add shredded cheese over the pickles.
  8. Roll the rice paper with the fillings.
  9. Coil the roll into a circular spiral shape.
  10. Place the coil on a lined baking tray.
  11. Brush the coil with egg wash.
  12. Sprinkle spice blend over the coil.
  13. Bake until golden.
  14. Slice the roll in half and serve.

WW Notes

The point drivers are shredded cheese, ham, eggs, and rice paper. For lower points, use lean ham, reduced-fat cheese, and a lighter amount of cheese.
