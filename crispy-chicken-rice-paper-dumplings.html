Crispy Chicken Rice Paper Dumplings

Air-fried rice paper dumplings filled with seasoned chicken mince, herbs, chilli, ginger, garlic, and served with peanut sauce.

Source: Georgia May from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

6 dumplings

Ingredients

Dumplings

  • 250 g chicken mince
  • 2 spring onions, diced
  • 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 10 g ginger, finely chopped
  • Small bunch coriander, finely chopped
  • 1 red chilli, seeds removed and finely chopped
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 6 rice paper rolls
  • Black and white sesame seeds, for coating
  • Oil spray

Peanut Sauce

  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Juice of 1/2 lime
  • 2 tbsp milk of choice, such as coconut milk
  • 1 to 2 tsp sriracha

Instructions

  1. Mix chicken mince with spring onion, coriander, chilli, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, honey, oyster sauce, and sesame oil.
  2. Fill a tray or plate with warm water and soak one rice paper roll for 30 seconds to 1 minute, until malleable.
  3. Lay the rice paper on a damp chopping board. Add a heaped tablespoon of chicken mince onto the roll.
  4. Gently but tightly roll into a parcel.
  5. Continue until all chicken mince is used.
  6. Lightly spray with oil, then coat with sesame seeds.
  7. Air fry for 17 to 20 minutes at 200 C / 400 F until golden and crispy.
  8. For the peanut sauce, add all sauce ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth.

WW Notes

Good protein option, but peanut butter, honey, sesame oil, sesame seeds, and rice paper add points. For a lighter version, use PB2 or powdered peanut butter in the sauce and reduce honey.
