Crispy Air-Fried Chicken Rice Bowls with Yuzu Sesame Soy Dressing

A high-protein rice bowl with crispy air-fried chicken, golden crispy rice, crunchy vegetables, avocado, and Mingle Yuzu, Sesame & Soy Japanese Dressing.

Source: Deglaze recipe link

Servings

4 servings

Ingredients

Chicken

  • 680 g boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 120 g plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 large egg
  • 60 g panko breadcrumbs
  • 30 g cornstarch
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Cooking spray

Rice

  • 600 g cooked jasmine rice or basmati rice
  • 2 tsp sesame oil
  • Cooking spray
  • Pinch of salt

Bowl toppings

  • 200 g cucumber, sliced
  • 100 g radishes, thinly sliced
  • 60 g green onions, sliced
  • 200 g red cabbage, shredded
  • 1 medium avocado, sliced
  • 80 g Mingle Yuzu, Sesame & Soy Japanese Dressing

Instructions

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 400°F.
  2. Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces.
  3. Mix Greek yogurt and egg in one bowl.
  4. In another bowl, combine panko, cornstarch, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
  5. Coat chicken in the yogurt mixture, then toss in the panko mixture.
  6. Spray the air fryer basket with cooking spray.
  7. Air fry the chicken for 10 to 14 minutes, shaking halfway, until crisp and cooked through.
  8. Toss cooked rice with sesame oil and a pinch of salt.
  9. Crisp the rice in an air fryer or on a hot griddle until golden in spots.
  10. Assemble bowls with crispy rice, chicken, cucumber, radishes, green onions, red cabbage, avocado, and dressing.

WW Notes

For a lower-point version, reduce the rice to 450 g cooked rice, use half an avocado, reduce dressing to 60 g, or replace half the rice with cauliflower rice.
