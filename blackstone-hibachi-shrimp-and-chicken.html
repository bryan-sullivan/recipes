Blackstone Hibachi Shrimp and Chicken

Serves: 4 · Japanese steakhouse-inspired

Two separately cooked proteins with garlic-ginger vegetables and cauliflower fried rice. Shrimp is optional per plate.

Ingredients

Proteins

  • 12 ounces peeled and deveined shrimp
  • 1¼ pounds chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 2 teaspoons neutral high-heat oil, divided
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Hibachi sauce

  • ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch, optional

Vegetables

  • 2 zucchini, cut into thick half-moons
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 yellow onion, cut into chunks
  • 1 pound mushrooms, halved or sliced
  • 1 pound broccoli florets, lightly steamed

Cauliflower fried rice

  • 1½ pounds cauliflower rice
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1 egg, optional

Instructions

Prep

  1. Whisk together the hibachi sauce.
  2. Mix the yogurt sauce and refrigerate.
  3. Keep the raw chicken and shrimp on separate trays with separate utensils.

Blackstone plan

  1. Preheat three zones: medium-high for chicken, high for vegetables and medium for fried rice.
  2. Cook the chicken first with a little oil. Brown thoroughly and cook to 165°F.
  3. Move the finished chicken to a clean warming area.
  4. Cook the vegetables until browned but still crisp. Add a little hibachi sauce near the end.
  5. Cook the cauliflower rice until its moisture evaporates. Add peas, carrots, scallions, soy and sesame oil. Scramble in the egg if using.
  6. Cook the shrimp last in its own clean zone, about 1–2 minutes per side, until opaque and 145°F.
  7. Sauce the proteins separately and serve with the yogurt sauce.

Note: If shrimp avoidance is due to an allergy rather than preference, use completely separate tools and cooking surfaces. Cooking on different zones of the same griddle may not prevent cross-contact.

Notes

Estimated nutrition

  • Chicken version: approximately 400–450 calories and 40–45 grams protein.
  • Shrimp version: approximately 350–425 calories and 30–35 grams protein.
