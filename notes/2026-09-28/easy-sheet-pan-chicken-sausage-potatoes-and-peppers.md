# Easy Sheet-Pan Chicken Sausage, Potatoes & Peppers

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-09-28]] · **Friday** · Serves **4** · About **45 minutes** (Sunday adds marinating).

Meat weights are raw unless specified; pasta and rice amounts are dry.

## Ingredients

### One-pan dinner

- 24 oz fully cooked chicken sausage — mild Italian style; choose about 140 calories and at least 13 g protein per 3 oz, without mustard
- 1 lb Yukon Gold or russet potatoes — ½-inch dice
- 3 medium bell peppers — bite-size pieces
- 1 medium yellow onions — thick wedges
- 3 tsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Line a large rimmed sheet pan. Slice fully cooked sausage into ¾-inch rounds.

2. Toss potatoes, peppers and onion with oil and all seasonings. Spread across the pan and roast 15 minutes.

3. Stir, add sausage and roast another 15–20 minutes, until potatoes are tender and sausage is hot throughout (follow package heating directions). Use two pans if vegetables overlap heavily; the double oven makes this easy.

4. Divide into four portions, each with one quarter of the sausage and vegetables. No separate side or sauce is needed.

> [!tip] Prep and equipment
> About 10 minutes active prep; use pre-cut peppers and onions to make it easier. Buy fully cooked sausage for this timing. Nutrition depends strongly on its label.
