# Chicken Tikka Masala with Basmati Rice & Cucumber-Tomato Salad

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-09-28]] · **Sunday** · Serves **4** · About **50 minutes** (Sunday adds marinating).

Meat weights are raw unless specified; pasta and rice amounts are dry.

## Ingredients

### Chicken and marinade

- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast — 1½-inch chunks
- ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- ½ whole lemon — juice
- 2 cloves garlic — grated
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp kosher salt

### Masala sauce

- 3 tsp olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onions — finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic — minced
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 1½ tsp garam masala
- 1½ tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 14½ oz crushed tomatoes
- ¼ cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) — optional, crushed
- ¼ tsp kosher salt
- ½ cup water, plus more if needed

### Rice and salad

- ¾ cup dry basmati rice
- 1 large English cucumber — diced
- 2 medium tomatoes — diced
- ¼ medium red onion — finely diced
- ½ whole lemon — juice
- ¼ cup chopped cilantro
- ¼ tsp kosher salt

## Method

1. At least 1 hour and up to 12 hours ahead, combine all marinade ingredients with chicken. Cover and refrigerate. Keep the spice level mild with sweet paprika; add heat at the table only if desired.

2. Heat the Classic II to 450–500°F with direct and indirect zones. Thread chicken onto metal skewers, leaving small gaps. Discard used marinade. Cook over direct heat, turning every few minutes, about 10–14 minutes; move to indirect heat if charring too quickly. Check several pieces reach 165°F.

3. Meanwhile heat oil in a deep skillet over medium heat. Soften onion for 8–10 minutes. Stir in garlic and ginger for 1 minute, then sauce spices for 30 seconds. Add tomatoes and ½ cup water; gently simmer 12–15 minutes, stirring. Blend carefully if a smoother sauce is wanted.

4. Cook rinsed basmati according to its package (typically about 1¼ cups water for ¾ cup rice; use the package ratio). Rest covered for 5 minutes and fluff. Expect about 2¼ cups cooked, roughly ½ cup per person with a little extra.

5. Lower sauce heat. Stir in cream and optional fenugreek, add cooked chicken and gently simmer 2–3 minutes. Thin with water if needed. Combine cucumber, tomatoes, onion, cilantro, lemon juice and salt in a separate bowl.

6. Divide chicken, sauce, rice and salad into four portions. Offer extra rice or naan separately for larger appetites; optional extras are outside the base grocery total.

> [!tip] Prep and equipment
> Active cooking is about 50 minutes, plus 1–12 hours marinating and grill startup. Joe Jr.: grill skewers in batches. Indoor option: broil on a foil-lined metal pan 6 inches from the element, turning once, about 10–15 minutes to 165°F; watch closely.
