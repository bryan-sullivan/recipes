# Baked Chicken Parmesan with Roasted Green Beans & Small Pasta

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-09-28]] · **Monday** · Serves **4** · About **40 minutes** (Sunday adds marinating).

Meat weights are raw unless specified; pasta and rice amounts are dry.

## Ingredients

### Chicken Parmesan

- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast — four equal cutlets, pounded to ½ inch
- ½ cup plain breadcrumbs
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 3 oz part-skim mozzarella — shredded
- 1½ cups marinara sauce
- 2 tsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ¼ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

### Sides

- 1½ lb green beans — trimmed
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic — minced
- ¼ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 4 oz dry pasta — 1 oz dry per person; roughly ½ cup cooked

## Method

1. Heat one oven to 450°F for chicken and the other to 425°F for beans. Line two rimmed pans. Put a pot of water on the electric stove for pasta.

2. Mix breadcrumbs, Parmesan, Italian seasoning and the chicken’s salt and pepper. Brush cutlets with 2 tsp oil, press into crumbs and lay on one pan. Bake 12–16 minutes; begin checking early because cutlet thickness matters.

3. Toss beans with 1 tsp oil, garlic and their salt and pepper. Roast in the second oven for 15–20 minutes, stirring once. With one oven, roast beans at 450°F and check at 12 minutes.

4. Spoon ¼ cup marinara onto each cutlet; divide mozzarella over them. Bake another 4–7 minutes, until the thickest part of each cutlet reaches 165°F and cheese melts.

5. Boil pasta according to its package and drain. Warm the remaining ½ cup marinara. Serve one cutlet, one quarter of the beans and one quarter of the pasta per person, with the warmed sauce.

> [!tip] Prep and equipment
> The small pasta serving is 1 oz dry per person. Extra pasta for larger appetites is optional and is not included in the base grocery total.
