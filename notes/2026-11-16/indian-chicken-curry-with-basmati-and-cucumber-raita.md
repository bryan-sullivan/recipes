# Indian Chicken Curry with Basmati Rice and Cucumber Raita

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-11-16]]

A lighter, fragrant chicken curry with onion-tomato masala, aromatic spices, basmati rice and cool cucumber raita; excellent leftovers.

**Equipment:** Large lidded stovetop pot, rice cooker or second pot, grater · **Time:** 55 minutes on cooking day · **Servings:** 8 (4 per night)

## Ingredients
- 3 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into 1½-inch pieces
- 2 tbsp neutral oil or olive oil
- 2 medium yellow onions, very finely chopped
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp freshly grated ginger
- 4 medium Roma tomatoes, grated or finely chopped (about 1 cup)
- ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt, whisked, room temperature, for curry
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 2 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp mild Kashmiri red chili powder or sweet paprika (adjust heat)
- 2 bay leaves (optional)
- 1 cinnamon stick, 2-inch (optional)
- 4 green cardamom pods (optional)
- 4 whole cloves (optional)
- 1½ tsp kosher salt, plus to taste
- 1 to 1½ cups hot water for sauce, as needed
- ¼ cup fresh chopped cilantro, for curry garnish
- 2 cups dry basmati rice (about 6 cups cooked)
- 2 large cucumbers, grated or finely diced for raita
- 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt, for raita
- ½ tsp ground cumin for raita
- ¼ cup chopped cilantro for raita
- 1 lemon, for optional finishing squeeze

## Directions
1. Wash basmati rice until rinse water runs mostly clear. Cook 2 cups dry rice with the amount of water recommended by the package, in a rice cooker or covered pot, then rest 5 minutes and fluff. Do this while the curry cooks.

2. In a wide heavy pot over medium, heat 2 tbsp oil. If using whole spices, toast bay leaves, cinnamon, cardamom pods and cloves for 20–30 seconds. Add finely chopped yellow onions and cook about 8–10 minutes, stirring, until well golden; this develops the curry's flavor.

3. Add garlic and ginger; stir 1 minute. Stir in chopped tomatoes, salt and turmeric; cook about 5–7 minutes until the tomatoes soften, their water reduces and the mixture becomes jammy.

4. Turn heat to low. Whisk ½ cup yogurt smooth in a bowl with two tablespoons of the warm onion-tomato mixture, then stir it into the pot so it does not split. Stir in coriander, garam masala and mild chili powder/paprika; cook gently for another 2 minutes.

5. Add chicken and stir until coated. Cook uncovered for 3–4 minutes until surfaces turn pale. Cover and allow chicken to take up spices for 3–4 minutes on low, then add 1 cup HOT water. Simmer covered 10–15 minutes (depending on cube size), adding a little hot water only if too thick, until the center of the largest chicken piece reaches 165°F and sauce coats the pieces.

6. Remove whole cloves, cardamom, cinnamon and bay leaves if practical. Adjust salt and spice, and sprinkle with chopped cilantro. Optional: finish with a small squeeze of lemon.

7. While curry simmers, stir together grated/diced cucumber, 1 cup Greek yogurt, ½ tsp cumin and ¼ cup chopped cilantro; season lightly with salt. Refrigerate raita until serving. Serve four portions of curry over moderate basmati rice with cucumber raita; save another four portions promptly chilled.

8. On Saturday, reheat curry and refrigerated rice thoroughly to 165°F; add a splash of water to loosen the sauce. Keep cucumber raita refrigerated and serve cold. Refrigerate cooked rice within 2 hours rather than leaving it at room temperature.

## Storage
Refrigerate within two hours. Reheat hot dishes to 165°F. Keep cold pico or raita refrigerated and separate from reheated food.
