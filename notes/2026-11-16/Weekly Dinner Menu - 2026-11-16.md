## Advance preparation
- Monday: use large lidded oven-safe pot, reserve and promptly chill Tuesday portions.
- Wednesday: use microwave brown rice and store pico separately.
- Friday: make curry and rice for two nights; keep cucumber raita cold.
- Reheat hot leftovers to 165°F; refrigerate rice within two hours.
