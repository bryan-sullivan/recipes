# High-Protein Skillet Lasagna with Broccoli

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-11-16]]

A comforting, cheesy 90/10 beef lasagna skillet with cottage cheese, served with simple steamed broccoli; makes two family dinners.

**Equipment:** Large lidded Dutch oven or oven-safe deep skillet, stovetop, broiler, steamer/microwave · **Time:** 55 minutes on cooking day · **Servings:** 8 (4 per night)

## Ingredients
- 1½ lb 90/10 ground beef, raw (family protein boost; original doubled amount is 1 lb)
- 1½ cups part-skim cottage cheese
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley, plus garnish
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus to taste
- 1 tbsp olive oil, for skillet
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 5 cups canned crushed tomatoes (buy two 28-oz cans; save excess)
- 5 cups low-sodium chicken bone broth
- ½ cup fresh chopped basil
- 16 oz dry short pasta (campanelle, bowties, or broken lasagna noodles)
- 1½ cups part-skim shredded mozzarella
- 2 lb broccoli florets, trimmed
- 2 tsp olive oil, optional for broccoli
- Black pepper to taste

## Directions
1. Set an oven rack roughly two levels below the broiler and prepare a large oven-safe Dutch oven (at least 6 qt) or two deep oven-safe skillets; an eight-serving batch is too large for most 12-inch skillets. Mix cottage cheese, Parmesan, and parsley in a bowl.

2. Heat the large pot over medium-high. Brown the 90/10 beef with about ¾ tsp salt, breaking it into small pieces, 6–8 minutes. Drain excess fat if present. Stir in 1 tbsp olive oil, diced onion, and garlic; sauté about 4–5 minutes until fragrant.

3. Add crushed tomatoes, 5 cups chicken bone broth, and basil. Bring to a simmer. Stir in dry pasta and make sure it is covered by liquid. Cover, reduce to medium-low, and simmer about 18–22 minutes, stirring every few minutes, until pasta is just tender. If needed, add a small splash of hot broth or water; do not allow the pasta to stick.

4. While the pasta simmers, steam broccoli in a covered pot with a little water for about 5–7 minutes or microwave in covered batches until bright green and tender-crisp. Season with salt, black pepper, and optional 2 tsp olive oil.

5. Turn off the lasagna heat. Spoon cottage-cheese mixture over the pasta without mixing it all in and scatter mozzarella over the surface. Broil 2–4 minutes just until bubbly and lightly browned, watching closely. If the pot is not broiler-safe, keep covered on low heat 3–5 minutes until cheese melts instead.

6. Divide into eight portions; serve four tonight with half the broccoli. Cool and refrigerate the other four portions and remaining broccoli in shallow covered containers within 2 hours. On Tuesday, reheat portions to 165°F, adding a spoon of water if pasta has absorbed sauce.

## Storage
Refrigerate within two hours. Reheat hot dishes to 165°F. Keep cold pico or raita refrigerated and separate from reheated food.
