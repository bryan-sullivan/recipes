# Easy Korean Beef Rice Bowls with Cucumber & Sesame Vegetables

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-13]]

The genuinely easy Wednesday dinner: one beef skillet, microwave brown rice and no-cook crisp vegetables.

Serves 4 · Electric stovetop + microwave · About 25 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb 90/10 lean ground beef
- 2 microwaveable brown rice pouches (about 8.8 oz each)
- 1 English cucumber, sliced thinly
- 8 oz pre-shredded carrots
- ½ medium yellow onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tsp light brown sugar
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil, divided
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 4 scallions, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds (optional)
- Gochujang (optional, for serving)
- Pinch red pepper flakes (optional)

## Method

1. Whisk soy sauce, brown sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil and 2 tbsp water. Heat a nonstick skillet on medium-high; brown beef for 6–8 minutes, draining excess fat if needed.

2. Stir in onion, garlic and ginger; cook 2 minutes. Pour in soy mixture and simmer 3–5 minutes until glossy and beef is fully cooked (160°F).

3. Microwave the two rice pouches according to their package directions. Toss cucumber and bagged carrots with rice vinegar, remaining 1 tsp sesame oil and half the scallions.

4. Portion rice into four bowls. Add beef and crisp sesame vegetables. Top with remaining scallions, sesame seeds and optional gochujang.

> [!tip]
> Keep Wednesday easy: use bagged carrots, microwave rice, and pre-minced garlic/ginger if desired. Gochujang stays on the side.
