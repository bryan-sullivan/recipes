# Blackstone Steak & Sweet Potato Bowls with Chimichurri and Roasted Brussels Sprouts

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-11-03]]

Sirloin steak bites, warm roasted sweet potatoes, baby arugula and bright parsley chimichurri, with crispy Brussels sprouts.

Serves 4 · Blackstone 36-inch griddle + oven · 45 min (plus Kamado startup where applicable). Meat weights raw; pasta and couscous dry.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb lean sirloin steak, trimmed and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1½ lb sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into ½-inch cubes
- 1½ lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- 4 oz baby arugula
- ¾ cup packed fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, grated or finely minced
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 lemon (1 tbsp juice, plus wedges)
- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, divided (chimichurri 1½ tbsp; sweet potatoes ½ tbsp; sprouts ½ tbsp; arugula ½ tbsp)
- 1 tsp avocado or olive oil, for the Blackstone steak
- ½ tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp kosher salt, divided, plus to taste
- ½ tsp black pepper, plus to taste

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss sweet potato cubes with ½ tbsp olive oil, ¼ tsp salt and a little black pepper. Roast on a sheet pan about 25 minutes, turning once, until browned and tender.

2. On a second sheet pan, toss halved Brussels sprouts with ½ tbsp olive oil and ¼ tsp salt. Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning once, until edges become deep golden.

3. Make chimichurri by stirring parsley, garlic, red wine vinegar, 1½ tbsp olive oil, remaining salt and black pepper in a bowl. Taste and adjust vinegar or water for a spoonable dressing.

4. Season steak cubes with chili powder, cumin, salt, pepper and 1 tsp avocado oil. Heat the Blackstone griddle to medium-high; place steak in a single layer and sear for 2–3 minutes without moving, then turn and finish 2–4 more minutes, depending on size and desired doneness. For food-safety guidance, whole-cut beef is recommended at 145°F followed by a 3-minute rest.

5. Toss arugula with ½ tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp lemon juice. Divide among four bowls, top with steak and sweet potatoes, drizzle chimichurri over steak, and serve Brussels sprouts beside. Add lemon wedges if desired.

> [!tip]
> Roast both vegetables while the Blackstone heats. The sweet potato and chimichurri can be prepped on Friday. Steak cubes cook quickly; don't crowd the griddle.

## Nutrition

High-protein, calorie-conscious family adaptation; calculate WeightWatchers points in the WW app using the listed ingredients. The original fall steak salad provides about 395 calories and 33.5 g protein per serving; adding Brussels sprouts and changing quantities alters nutrition.
