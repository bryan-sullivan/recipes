# Chocolate Chip Oatmeal Cookies

Browned-butter oatmeal cookies with ground rolled oats, semisweet chocolate chips and grated milk chocolate. **Original family recipe (not calorie-reduced).**

**Yield:** Approximately 34 cookies  
**Oven:** 375°F (190°C)  
**Bake time:** About 12 minutes per sheet  
**Portion:** Rounded 2-tablespoon scoops

## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter (16 tbsp total): 11 tbsp browned butter and 5 tbsp regular butter
- ¾ cup white sugar (180 g, as written in source)
- 1¼ cups packed light brown sugar (260 g)
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 cups all-purpose flour (10 oz)
- 2½ cups rolled oats, ground finely
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1½ cups semisweet chocolate chips
- 4 oz milk chocolate, finely grated or pulsed into small pieces

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Line cookie sheets with parchment paper. Measure the rolled oats into a food processor or blender and pulse until finely ground; set aside.

2. Brown 11 tablespoons of the butter and let it cool enough to blend with the remaining 5 tablespoons of regular butter. Cream the butter mixture with the white and brown sugars in a large bowl.

3. Beat in the two whole eggs, one at a time, then add the additional egg yolk and vanilla extract.

4. In a separate bowl, combine flour, finely ground oats, grated milk chocolate, salt, baking powder and baking soda. Fold the dry mixture into the butter-sugar mixture by hand until just combined; fold in the semisweet chocolate chips. (The source mentions grated chocolate twice; add its measured 4 oz only once.)

5. Drop dough in rounded 2-tablespoon scoops onto the prepared baking sheets, leaving room to spread. Lightly flatten each scoop into a thick disc.

6. Bake at 375°F for about 12 minutes, as specified in the original recipe. Let cookies cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes, then transfer to wire racks to cool completely.

> [!tip]
> **If doubling, add one extra whole egg for moisture.** To prepare the grated milk chocolate, the source freezes it and pulses the chocolate in a food processor with a little of the ground oat powder to prevent caking.

> [!note] Original measurement and directions
> The source lists **¾ cup white sugar (180 g)**. These measures may not be a conventional equivalent; both are preserved exactly as supplied. The source's directions repeat the grated chocolate; use the listed **4 oz once**, not twice.
