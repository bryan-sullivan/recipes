# One-Pot Chicken & Orzo with Lemon-Garlic Green Beans

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-11-03]]

Golden, crisp-skinned chicken thighs over a savory tomato-orzo pilaf, with bright garlicky green beans.

Serves 4 · Electric stovetop + oven · 65 min (plus Kamado startup where applicable). Meat weights raw; pasta and couscous dry.

## Ingredients

- 4 large bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2½–3 lb total)
- 1 cup dry orzo pasta (about 7 oz)
- ½ medium yellow onion, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced, for orzo
- ½ cup tomato paste
- 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth, plus extra ⅓ cup if needed
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano, optional
- ¼ cup sliced fresh basil
- ½ tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, for chicken
- 1¼ lb green beans, trimmed
- 2 garlic cloves, finely minced, for green beans
- 2 tsp olive oil, for green beans
- 1 lemon (juice and wedges)
- 1 tsp kosher salt, divided, plus to taste
- ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Dry chicken skin thoroughly, season both sides with about ¾ tsp salt and pepper, and place skin-side down in a large cold or just-warm oven-safe skillet with ½ tbsp olive oil. Bring to medium heat and slowly brown for 12–15 minutes, until much of the fat renders and the skin becomes crisp. Move thighs to a plate.

2. Pour off excess rendered fat, leaving roughly 2 teaspoons in the skillet. Add diced onion and sauté 3–4 minutes until translucent, then toast the dry orzo for 2–3 minutes. Add 3 minced garlic cloves, stir 30 seconds, and cook tomato paste for 1–2 minutes until darkened.

3. Pour in 3 cups broth, scrape up browned bits, and bring to a gentle simmer. Set the thighs on top with the skin above the liquid. Transfer uncovered to the oven for about 25–30 minutes; stir orzo carefully if needed without wetting chicken skin. If pasta dries out before tender, add hot broth in ⅓-cup splashes.

4. Check the thickest part of each thigh near the bone with a thermometer; chicken must reach at least 165°F. Cook a few minutes longer if necessary. Remove skillet and let stand about 5 minutes, then sprinkle Parmesan and fresh basil over the orzo.

5. While the skillet is in the oven, toss trimmed green beans with 2 tsp olive oil and 2 minced garlic cloves. Roast on a separate baking sheet at 425°F for 12–16 minutes until tender-crisp, or sauté on the stovetop for 6–8 minutes. Finish with juice of ½ lemon and serve the other half in wedges.

> [!tip]
> Use the double ovens at 375°F for chicken and 425°F for green beans. Chicken thighs must reach 165°F, regardless of the orzo's doneness. Dry skin well and don't submerge it.
