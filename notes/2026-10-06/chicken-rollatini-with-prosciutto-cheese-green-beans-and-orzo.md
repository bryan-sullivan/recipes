# Chicken Rollatini with Prosciutto & Cheese, Roasted Green Beans & Lemon-Garlic Orzo

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-06]]

Serves 4 · Double oven + electric stovetop · About 45 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast — 8 thin 4 oz cutlets
- 2.8 oz prosciutto — 4 thin slices, halved
- 4 slices reduced-fat provolone — halve each
- ½ cup Italian seasoned breadcrumbs
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 2 whole lemon — one for coating, one for orzo
- 5 tsp olive oil — 3 for chicken; 1 beans; 1 orzo
- ¼ medium red onion — thinly sliced
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1½ lb green beans
- 2 cloves garlic
- 4 oz dry orzo
- ¼ tsp kosher salt

## Method

1. Heat chicken oven to 450°F and vegetable oven to 425°F. Mix crumbs with Parmesan; separately combine one lemon’s juice, 3 tsp oil and half the pepper.

2. Dip cutlets in lemon mixture and crumbs. Fill each with half a prosciutto slice, half a cheese slice and onion; roll seam-down into a lined baking dish. Bake about 25 minutes, checking the centers reach 165°F.

3. Toss beans with 1 tsp oil, salt and remaining pepper; roast 15–20 minutes. Boil orzo, drain, then toss with garlic softened in 1 tsp oil and the second lemon’s zest and juice to taste.

4. Serve two rolls with one quarter each of the beans and orzo.

> [!tip]
> Small pasta portion: 1 oz dry orzo per person. Angel hair can replace it ounce-for-ounce.
