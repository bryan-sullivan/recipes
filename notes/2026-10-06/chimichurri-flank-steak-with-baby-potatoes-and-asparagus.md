# Chimichurri Flank Steak with Crispy Baby Potatoes & Asparagus

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-06]]

Serves 4 · Blackstone or stovetop + double oven · About 40 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb flank steak
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ¾ tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp kosher salt — ½ steak; ¼ sauce; ¼ sides
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 0.333 cup chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 2 tbsp chopped red onion
- 4 cloves garlic
- 9 tsp olive oil — 6 sauce; 2 potatoes; 1 asparagus
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- ⅛ tsp red pepper flakes
- 1½ lb baby potatoes
- 1½ lb asparagus
- 1 tbsp water for sauce

## Method

1. Friday: mix parsley, onion, garlic, 6 tsp oil, vinegar, water, pepper flakes and ¼ tsp salt. Refrigerate in a covered container.

2. Saturday: halve potatoes; microwave covered with a splash of water 6–8 minutes until nearly tender. Drain and dry, toss with 2 tsp oil and half the side salt; roast cut-side down at 450°F for 15–20 minutes.

3. Season steak with garlic powder, cumin, oregano, ½ tsp salt and pepper. Sear on a hot Blackstone or skillet about 4–6 minutes per side; check 145°F, then rest at least 3 minutes. Slice thinly across the grain.

4. Toss asparagus with 1 tsp oil and remaining side salt. Roast at 425°F for 10–12 minutes while steak cooks. Serve four portions with chimichurri.

> [!tip]
> Use the Blackstone or stovetop after the firehouse event to avoid charcoal startup. About 35–40 minutes with sauce made Friday; thicker steak may need longer.
