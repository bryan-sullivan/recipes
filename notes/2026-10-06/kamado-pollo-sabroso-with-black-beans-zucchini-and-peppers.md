# Kamado Pollo Sabroso with Black Beans, Zucchini & Peppers

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-06]]

Serves 4 · Kamado Joe Classic II or Jr. + electric stovetop · About 40 minutes. Meat weights are raw; rice and pasta weights are dry.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp white vinegar
- 2 tsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 packet sazon with achiote
- 1 tsp adobo seasoning
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano — ½ tsp chicken; ½ tsp beans
- 2 15 oz cans black beans — drained and rinsed
- ½ medium yellow onions
- 2 cloves garlic
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- 2 medium zucchini
- 2 medium bell peppers
- 3 tsp olive oil — 1 beans; 2 vegetables
- 1 whole lime
- ¼ cup water for beans

## Method

1. Season thighs with vinegar, soy, sazon, adobo, garlic powder and half the oregano. Refrigerate 20 minutes or overnight. Allow additional time to light and stabilize the Kamado.

2. Grill at about 400°F, turning, roughly 5–7 minutes per side to 165°F. Move away from direct heat if browning too quickly. Rest 5 minutes.

3. Soften onion and garlic in 1 tsp oil. Add beans, cumin, remaining oregano and water; simmer 10 minutes.

4. Slice zucchini and peppers, toss with 2 tsp oil and grill 8–12 minutes, or roast at 425°F for 20 minutes. Divide everything four ways and finish with lime.

> [!tip]
> Boneless skinless thighs keep cooking quick. Joe Jr.: use batches; roast vegetables indoors. Oven backup: 425°F for about 20–25 minutes to 165°F.

## Nutrition

Approximately 455 calories and 43 g protein per serving including sides. Household estimates; brands and portions vary. Import to calculate WeightWatchers points.
