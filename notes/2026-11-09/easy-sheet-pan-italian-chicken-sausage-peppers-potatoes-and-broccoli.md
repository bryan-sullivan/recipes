# Easy Sheet-Pan Italian Chicken Sausage, Peppers, Potatoes & Broccoli

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-11-09]]

Easy Wednesday: fully cooked chicken sausage, colorful peppers, small potato pieces and broccoli roast on sheet pans with almost no cleanup.

**Equipment:** Electric oven · **Time:** approximately 35 minutes · **Servings:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb fully cooked Italian chicken sausage, sliced into ½-inch coins
- 1½ lb baby Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into ½-inch pieces
- 1 large red bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 large yellow or orange bell pepper, cut into chunks
- 1 medium red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 lb broccoli florets
- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp grated Parmesan, optional

## Method

1. Heat oven to 450°F. Line two rimmed sheet pans with parchment; use two pans to avoid steaming the vegetables.

2. Toss the small ½-inch potato pieces with 2 tsp olive oil, half the Italian seasoning and a pinch of salt. Spread on one pan and roast for 10 minutes.

3. Toss fully cooked sliced sausage, peppers, red onion and broccoli with remaining olive oil, seasoning, garlic powder and pepper on the second pan.

4. Add the second pan to the oven, and continue roasting both pans another 17–22 minutes, turning halfway, until potatoes are tender and sausage is hot throughout; monitor broccoli to avoid burning.

5. Mix the two trays and sprinkle with Parmesan if desired. No separate side or specialized grill equipment needed.

> [!tip]
> Choose fully cooked sausage for the 35-minute timing. Pre-cut vegetables the night before and use two baking sheets; potatoes must be cut small.
