# Thanksgiving Turkey Meatballs & Gravy with Egg Noodles and Roasted Carrots

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-11-09]]

Stuffing-scented ground turkey meatballs in savory herb gravy, with tender egg noodles and lemon-roasted carrots.

**Equipment:** Oven + electric stovetop · **Time:** approximately 75 minutes · **Servings:** About 6 meatball portions; sides for 4

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb 93% lean ground turkey (intentionally turkey for this Thanksgiving-inspired dish)
- 1 large egg
- 1½ cups seasoned dry stuffing mix, about 3½ oz
- ¾ cup low-sodium turkey or chicken broth to soften stuffing
- ½ small yellow onion, finely diced
- 2 medium celery stalks, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh sage
- 1¼ tsp salt-free poultry seasoning for meatballs
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 tbsp olive oil for aromatics
- 4 cups low-sodium turkey or chicken broth for gravy
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- ½ tsp poultry seasoning for gravy
- 1 sprig fresh sage and 1 sprig fresh thyme for gravy
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 8 oz dry egg noodles, cooked separately
- 1½ lb carrots, cut into ½-inch coins
- 2 tsp olive oil for carrots
- 1 lemon, juice for carrots
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 1 tsp kosher salt, divided, plus to taste
- ½ tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Heat oven to 425°F for carrots. Toss carrots with 2 tsp olive oil and a pinch of salt; spread on a sheet pan and roast about 20–25 minutes until browned and tender. Finish with lemon juice.

2. Whisk egg with ¾ cup broth and moisten the stuffing mix. Meanwhile sauté onion and celery in 1 tbsp olive oil 7–9 minutes; add garlic, minced sage and 1¼ tsp poultry seasoning for 1 minute. Cool vegetables before combining with stuffing.

3. Gently combine cooled stuffing mixture, ground turkey, Parmesan, salt and pepper; form approximately 30 small meatballs. Broil on a foil-lined sheet, 5–7 minutes, to brown; these are not fully cooked yet.

4. Bring 3½ cups gravy broth to a simmer with sage and thyme sprigs for 10–15 minutes. Mix remaining ½ cup cold broth with flour and ½ tsp poultry seasoning until smooth, then whisk into the hot broth. Simmer until thickening, add butter and discard herb stems.

5. Slide the browned meatballs into the gravy and simmer covered approximately 15–20 minutes, stirring gently, until the centers of several meatballs register 165°F. Prepare egg noodles according to package directions while meatballs simmer.

6. Serve meatballs and gravy over a moderate portion of egg noodles, with roasted carrots and parsley. The 1½ lb meatball batch yields about six original-size portions, so expect leftovers for a family of four.

> [!tip]
> Ground poultry must reach 165°F. The original Skinnytaste batch makes about 30 meatballs/six original-size servings; serve four and refrigerate extra. Use egg noodles rather than additional potatoes for this week's sides.
