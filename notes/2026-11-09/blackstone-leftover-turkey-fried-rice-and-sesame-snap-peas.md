# Blackstone Leftover Turkey Fried Rice with Sesame Snap Peas

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-11-09]]

A new use for Friday's roast turkey: hot Blackstone fried rice with carrots, peas, scrambled eggs, scallions and a side of sesame snap peas.

**Equipment:** Blackstone 36-inch griddle + stovetop · **Time:** approximately 35 minutes · **Servings:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb cooked turkey from Friday, refrigerated promptly, diced
- 1½ cups dry jasmine rice, cooked the night before to make about 4½ cups chilled rice
- 4 large eggs, beaten
- 2 medium carrots, finely diced
- 1 cup frozen green peas
- 4 scallions, whites and greens separated, thinly sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 tsp avocado or canola oil for griddle
- 1 lb sugar snap peas, trimmed, for side
- 1 tsp olive oil for snap peas
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar (optional)
- Black pepper, to taste

## Method

1. FRIDAY PREP: Cook 1½ cups dry jasmine rice according to package directions to yield roughly 4½ cups cooked. Spread hot rice in a shallow container and refrigerate promptly; keep at 40°F or below overnight. Do not leave rice out on the counter. Refrigerate the reserved cooked turkey promptly as well.

2. SATURDAY: Preheat Blackstone to medium-high and set out chilled rice, diced turkey, peas, carrots, scallions and eggs. Keep leftover turkey refrigerated until it goes on the hot griddle.

3. Oil part of the griddle and scramble beaten eggs until set; move to a warm clean tray. Stir-fry carrots and scallion whites in remaining oil 2–3 minutes, add garlic for the last 30 seconds, then fold in cold rice and cook 4–6 minutes, breaking up clumps.

4. Add turkey, peas and reduced-sodium soy sauce; toss and heat thoroughly until turkey and rice reach 165°F in several spots. Fold scrambled eggs back in; turn off heat and add sesame oil and scallion greens.

5. Meanwhile sauté sugar snap peas in 1 tsp olive oil for 3–5 minutes until bright green and tender-crisp. Add sesame seeds and optional rice vinegar. Serve fried rice immediately with the snap peas.

> [!tip]
> Cook rice Friday, chill it quickly, and use chilled cooked rice Saturday. Reheat leftover turkey to 165°F. Do not leave turkey or rice at room temperature for long periods.
