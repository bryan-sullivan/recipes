## Thursday turkey dry-fit

Fit a FULLY THAWED 12 lb turkey on the standard spit with a cold/unlit Kamado; truss, secure, center and test motor rotation. Do not use 10 lb basket accessory. If no clearance, use oven backup rather than force fit.

## Friday turkey dress rehearsal

Bank charcoal rearward, 325°F, clean smoke, no wood chunks, check breast/thigh/wing each 165°F; rest 20–30 min. Reserve 1½ lb turkey and quickly chill 4½ cups cooked rice for Saturday's fried rice. Record fuel, times, temperature and results.
