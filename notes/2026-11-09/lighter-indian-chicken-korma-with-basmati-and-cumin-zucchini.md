# Lighter Indian Chicken Korma with Basmati Rice & Cumin-Roasted Zucchini

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-11-09]]

A mild yogurt-cashew chicken korma with fragrant spices, portioned basmati rice and roasted cumin zucchini.

**Equipment:** Electric stovetop + oven · **Time:** approximately 55 minutes · **Servings:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 medium yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh grated ginger
- ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt, room temperature
- ¼ cup raw cashews, soaked in hot water 10 minutes then blended with ¼ cup water
- 2 medium tomatoes, diced
- 1 tbsp olive oil for curry
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ¼ tsp ground cardamom or seeds from 3 pods, optional
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- ½ tsp Kashmiri chili powder (optional; omit for mild)
- ½ cup low-sodium chicken broth or water, plus as needed
- 1 cup dry basmati rice
- 1½ lb zucchini, cut into half moons
- 2 tsp olive oil for zucchini
- ½ tsp ground cumin for zucchini
- 1 lemon, juice to finish
- 1 tsp kosher salt, divided

## Method

1. Cook basmati rice using package directions. Heat an oven to 425°F and toss zucchini with 2 tsp olive oil, ½ tsp cumin and a little salt; roast about 20 minutes, turning once.

2. Meanwhile sauté sliced onion in 1 tbsp olive oil in a heavy skillet for 8–10 minutes until golden. Add ginger and garlic for 1 minute, then coriander, cumin, turmeric, garam masala, cardamom and cinnamon stick for 30 seconds.

3. Add diced tomatoes and cook until thick, about 5 minutes. Stir in the blended cashew paste with ½ cup broth; simmer gently 3 minutes. Keep sauce mild unless your family wants the optional chili.

4. Lower heat so the sauce is below a boil. Whisk Greek yogurt smooth in a cup with a spoonful of warm sauce, then stir into the pot. Do not boil hard or it may split.

5. Add chicken pieces and simmer gently, stirring occasionally, about 12–16 minutes until the thickest pieces register 165°F. Add water or broth as needed for a creamy, spoonable texture. Remove cinnamon stick.

6. Season to taste and brighten with a small squeeze of lemon. Serve over moderate rice portions with roasted cumin zucchini.

> [!tip]
> This is a lighter family-style adaptation and intentionally not a verbatim recreation of Swasthi's recipe. Cashews are tree nuts; replace with extra Greek yogurt if needed, and temper the yogurt to avoid curdling.
