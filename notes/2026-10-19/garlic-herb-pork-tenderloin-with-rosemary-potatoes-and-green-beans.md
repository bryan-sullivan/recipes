# Garlic-Herb Pork Tenderloin with Rosemary Potatoes & Garlic Green Beans

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-19]]

Lean garlicky pork tenderloin with crisp rosemary potatoes and tender green beans.

Serves 4 · Double oven · 45 min. Meat weights raw; rice and pasta dry.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb pork tenderloin
- 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved
- 1½ lb green beans, trimmed
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh rosemary, divided
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp low-sodium chicken broth
- 1½ tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Heat oven to 425°F. Pat pork dry and season with thyme, half the rosemary, salt, pepper and 2 minced garlic cloves.

2. Toss potatoes with 2 tsp oil, remaining rosemary and a pinch of salt. Roast 30–35 minutes, turning once.

3. Sear pork in an oven-safe skillet with 1 tsp oil for 2 minutes per side, then roast at 425°F for 15–25 minutes until 145°F at the center. Rest at least 5 minutes.

4. Toss green beans with remaining oil and 2 minced garlic cloves; roast 12–15 minutes until tender-crisp.

5. Warm the last garlic clove in the skillet with broth and lemon juice for 1 minute; spoon over sliced pork. Serve with potatoes and beans.

> [!tip]
> Pork tenderloin is not pork loin. Use a thermometer; exact roasting time depends on thickness.
