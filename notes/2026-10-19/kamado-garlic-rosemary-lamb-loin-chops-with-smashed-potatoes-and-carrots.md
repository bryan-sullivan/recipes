# Kamado Garlic-Rosemary Lamb Loin Chops with Smashed Potatoes & Honey-Thyme Carrots

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-19]]

Meaty garlic-rosemary lamb loin chops, extra-crisp smashed potatoes and lightly sweet roasted carrots.

Serves 4 · Kamado Joe Classic II + oven · 50 min. Meat weights raw; rice and pasta dry.

## Ingredients

- 8 lamb loin chops, about 2½–3 lb total bone-in
- 1½ lb small Yukon Gold potatoes
- 1½ lb carrots, peeled and cut into batons
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh rosemary
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves, divided
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 2 tsp honey
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Marinate chops 2–4 hours in 1 tbsp olive oil, rosemary, minced garlic, lemon zest, a splash of lemon juice, salt and pepper; refrigerate. Remove from refrigerator 20 minutes before grilling.

2. Boil potatoes in salted water 15–20 minutes until fork-tender. Drain well, place on a lined tray, gently smash, brush with 2 tsp oil and roast at 450°F for 20–25 minutes until crisp.

3. Toss carrots with remaining oil, half the thyme and honey; roast at 425–450°F about 20–25 minutes until lightly caramelized.

4. Stabilize clean-burning Kamado Joe Classic II around 450°F. Grill chops over direct heat about 3–5 minutes per side, based on thickness, until 145°F internally; rest at least 3 minutes.

5. Finish potatoes with remaining thyme; serve chops alongside potatoes and carrots.

> [!tip]
> Choose loin (T-bone-shaped) chops; 2 per person. Trim external fat for a lighter meal.
