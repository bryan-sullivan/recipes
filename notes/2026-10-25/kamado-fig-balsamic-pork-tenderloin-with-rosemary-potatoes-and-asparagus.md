# Kamado Fig-Balsamic Pork Tenderloin with Rosemary Baby Potatoes & Asparagus

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-25]]

Charcoal-roasted lean pork tenderloin finished with a tangy fig-balsamic glaze, crispy potatoes and asparagus.

Serves 4 · Kamado Joe Classic II + double oven · 55 min. Meat weights raw, rice dry.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb pork tenderloin (usually 2 pieces), trimmed
- 2 tbsp fig preserves or fig butter
- 3 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh rosemary, divided
- 1½ lb baby Yukon Gold potatoes, halved
- 1½ lb asparagus, trimmed
- 1½ tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus to taste
- ½ tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Light Kamado Joe Classic II with lump charcoal and set for 375°F indirect cooking with deflectors. Wait until thick white smoke has dissipated.

2. Heat an oven to 425°F. Toss halved potatoes with 2 tsp olive oil, 1 tbsp rosemary, salt and pepper; roast 30–35 minutes, turning halfway.

3. Season pork with salt, pepper, half the garlic and remaining rosemary. Mix fig preserves with balsamic vinegar and 1 tbsp warm water; reserve as glaze.

4. Place pork on indirect Kamado grate and cook approximately 20–35 minutes, depending on thickness, brushing with glaze in final 10 minutes. Check several spots and remove at 145°F; rest at least 5–8 minutes.

5. Roast asparagus at 425°F with remaining olive oil and garlic for 10–12 minutes. Slice pork and serve with remaining warmed glaze, potatoes and asparagus.

> [!tip]
> Wait for thin, clean charcoal smoke before adding pork. Stabilize Classic II around 375°F indirect with heat deflectors. Pork safe temperature is 145°F with 3-minute rest; a longer 8-minute rest improves slicing.
