# Korean 90/10 Beef Bibimbap Bowls with Sesame Cucumber Salad

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-25]]

Korean-inspired rice bowls with seasoned lean beef, mushrooms, carrots, spinach and a fried egg; cool sesame cucumbers on the side.

Serves 4 · Electric stovetop · 35 min. Meat weights raw, rice dry.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb 90/10 lean ground beef
- 1 cup dry jasmine or brown rice
- 4 large eggs
- 8 oz cremini or shiitake mushrooms, sliced
- 3 medium carrots, shredded
- 5 oz baby spinach
- 1 English cucumber, thinly sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 2 tbsp gochujang, plus extra to serve
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar, divided
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil, divided
- 1 tsp honey
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp avocado or olive oil

## Method

1. Cook rice according to package directions, using 1 cup dry rice for four moderate portions.

2. Whisk gochujang, soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sesame oil and honey. In a separate bowl dress cucumber slices with remaining 1 tbsp vinegar, 1 tsp sesame oil, scallions and a pinch of salt.

3. Brown the beef in a large skillet 5–7 minutes, breaking it up; drain excess fat. Add mushrooms, garlic and ginger and sauté 4 minutes. Add carrots, then spinach, and cook until wilted; stir in sauce and heat through.

4. In a nonstick skillet fry 4 eggs using 1 tsp oil, until whites are fully set.

5. Divide rice among four bowls; top with beef-vegetable mixture, an egg and sesame seeds. Serve cucumber salad alongside.

> [!tip]
> Mix gochujang individually at the table for milder portions. For food safety, cook ground beef to 160°F and eggs until whites are set.
