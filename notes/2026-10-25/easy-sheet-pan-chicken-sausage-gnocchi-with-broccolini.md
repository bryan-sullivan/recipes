# Easy Sheet-Pan Chicken Sausage & Gnocchi with Broccolini and Tomatoes

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-25]]

One pan, almost no prep: precooked chicken sausage, soft gnocchi, caramelized broccolini and burst tomatoes.

Serves 4 · Oven only · 30 min. Meat weights raw, rice dry.

## Ingredients

- 1½ lb fully cooked Italian chicken sausage, sliced
- 16 oz shelf-stable potato gnocchi (no pre-boiling)
- 1½ lb broccolini or broccoli florets
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Heat oven to 425°F; line two rimmed baking sheets with parchment.

2. Combine sliced fully cooked chicken sausage, dry shelf-stable gnocchi, broccolini and cherry tomatoes on the pans. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle Italian seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, salt and pepper.

3. Toss well, spreading in a single layer; don't crowd the pans.

4. Roast 20–25 minutes, stirring halfway, until sausage is heated through, gnocchi is tender and tomatoes have burst. Optional: broil 1 minute for additional color.

5. Divide into four bowls and finish with grated Parmesan. No separate side required.

> [!tip]
> Wednesday is designed for your wife to prepare without the Kamado or Blackstone. Pre-cooked chicken sausage is essential to this 30-minute timing. Use two sheet pans if crowded.
