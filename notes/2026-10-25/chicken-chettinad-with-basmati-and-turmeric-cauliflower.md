# South Indian Chicken Chettinad with Basmati Rice & Turmeric-Roasted Cauliflower

[[Weekly Dinner Menu - 2026-10-25]]

Fragrant tomato-and-coconut Chettinad chicken with mild adjustable heat, fluffy basmati and oven-roasted cauliflower.

Serves 4 · Electric stovetop + oven · 50 min. Meat weights raw, rice dry.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into 1½-inch pieces
- 1 large yellow onion, finely diced
- 3 medium tomatoes, chopped
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 1-inch piece fresh ginger, grated
- 1 sprig fresh curry leaves (optional)
- ¼ cup unsweetened shredded coconut
- 1 tbsp coriander seeds (or 2 tsp ground coriander)
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- ¾ tsp cumin seeds
- ½ tsp black peppercorns (or ground pepper)
- 1 cinnamon stick
- ½ tsp turmeric, divided
- ½ tsp Kashmiri chili powder, optional
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 cup dry basmati rice
- 1 large cauliflower head (about 1½ lb), florets
- 1½ tbsp olive oil, divided
- ½ tsp kosher salt, plus to taste
- ¾ cup hot water

## Method

1. Toss chicken with lemon juice, ¼ tsp turmeric, a little salt and half the garlic and ginger. Rest while prepping vegetables. Preheat oven to 425°F.

2. Toast coriander, fennel, cumin and peppercorns in a dry skillet 2–3 minutes. Grind with coconut and ¼–½ cup water to a coarse paste; alternatively use ground spices and a splash of water.

3. In a Dutch oven warm 2 tsp olive oil, cook onion until golden 7–9 minutes. Add remaining garlic, ginger, cinnamon and curry leaves for 1 minute; stir in tomatoes and optional chili, cooking until softened.

4. Add spice-coconut paste and ¾ cup hot water. Simmer 4 minutes. Stir in chicken and simmer gently 10–14 minutes until center reaches 165°F; adjust water and salt to make a spoonable sauce.

5. Meanwhile cook basmati per package instructions; toss cauliflower in remaining olive oil, ¼ tsp turmeric and salt, then roast at 425°F for 22–28 minutes. Serve over modest rice portions with cauliflower.

> [!tip]
> This is a lighter family adaptation, not a traditional full-spice version. Use Kashmiri chili powder for warmth without aggressive heat; fresh curry leaves are optional but strongly recommended.
