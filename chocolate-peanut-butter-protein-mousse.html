Chocolate–Peanut Butter Protein Mousse

Serves: 8 · American

A thick, creamy, no-cook dessert that tastes like chocolate-peanut-butter pudding and takes only a few minutes to mix.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk, plus more as needed
  • ½ cup powdered peanut butter
  • One 1-ounce package sugar-free instant chocolate pudding mix
  • 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ⅛ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Zero-calorie sweetener to taste, optional

Optional toppings

  • Sliced strawberries or raspberries
  • Light whipped topping
  • A small amount of shaved dark chocolate
  • Chopped roasted peanuts

Instructions

Method

  1. Place the Greek yogurt in a large mixing bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the almond milk, powdered peanut butter, dry pudding mix, cocoa, vanilla and salt until smooth.
  3. Whisk the chocolate mixture into the yogurt until evenly combined and fluffy.
  4. Taste and add sweetener if needed. If the mousse is too thick, stir in almond milk 1 tablespoon at a time.
  5. Divide among eight small bowls or jars.
  6. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.

Notes

Nutrition

Approximately 120–130 calories and 15–16 grams protein per serving, before optional toppings. Actual values depend on the yogurt, pudding mix and powdered peanut butter used.

Texture options

  • Lighter mousse: Fold in 1 cup light whipped topping after mixing.
  • Frozen pops: Thin with an additional ¼ cup almond milk, spoon into pop molds and freeze until solid.
  • Chocolate dip: Use only ½ cup almond milk and serve with strawberries.

Make ahead and storage

  • Make up to 2 days in advance for the best texture.
  • Refrigerate covered for up to 4 days.
  • Add fruit, whipped topping or crunchy garnishes immediately before serving.
