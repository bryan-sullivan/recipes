Bacon Egg and Cheese Rice Paper Spiral

A baked rice paper spiral filled with scrambled eggs, chopped bacon, mozzarella, and everything bagel seasoning.

Source: Josh Gale from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

1 spiral

Ingredients

Ingredients

  • Rice paper sheets
  • Egg wash
  • Scrambled eggs
  • Chopped bacon
  • Shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Everything bagel seasoning

Instructions

  1. Dip rice paper sheets in egg wash.
  2. Lay the egg-coated sheets on plastic wrap, overlapping them in a long row.
  3. Spoon scrambled eggs in a line down the middle.
  4. Sprinkle chopped bacon over the eggs.
  5. Add a layer of shredded mozzarella cheese.
  6. Roll up the rice paper sheets to form a long cylinder.
  7. Coil the cylinder into a spiral shape.
  8. Place the spiral onto a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  9. Sprinkle with everything bagel seasoning.
  10. Bake until golden brown.

WW Notes

Exact points will depend heavily on the number of rice paper sheets, eggs, bacon, and cheese. For a lighter version, use turkey bacon, egg whites or a mix of eggs and egg whites, and reduced-fat mozzarella.
