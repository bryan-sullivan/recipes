# Kamado Berry Cheesecake Cups

Creamy individual cheesecakes with fresh berries and a light graham-cracker crust. Use little or no smoking wood so the delicate filling does not become overly smoky.

## Nutrition

Approximately 180 calories and 17 grams protein per serving. Actual values depend on the cottage cheese, protein powder and sweetener used.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups low-fat cottage cheese
- 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
- 2 large eggs
- 2 scoops vanilla protein powder
- ¼ cup pure maple syrup or equivalent zero-calorie sweetener
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Zest and juice of ½ lemon
- ⅛ teaspoon kosher salt
- 1½ cups fresh or frozen berries
- 4 full graham-cracker sheets, finely crushed
- Nonstick cooking spray

## Kamado setup

1. Install the heat deflectors for indirect cooking.
2. Stabilize the Kamado Joe at **325°F** at the dome.
3. Skip smoking wood for a clean cheesecake flavor. If desired, use only a very small piece of mild apple or cherry wood.

## Method

1. Lightly spray eight 6-ounce heat-safe ramekins.
2. Divide the graham-cracker crumbs evenly among the ramekins and gently press them into the bottoms.
3. Blend the cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, eggs, protein powder, maple syrup, vanilla, lemon zest, lemon juice and salt until completely smooth. Scrape down the blender as needed.
4. Divide the filling among the ramekins. Top each with berries; frozen berries do not need to be thawed.
5. Arrange the ramekins on a rimmed pan or directly on the cooking grate over indirect heat.
6. Cook for **22–30 minutes**, until the edges are set and the centers still have a slight wobble. Do not cook until firm; they continue setting as they cool.
7. Cool at room temperature for about 30 minutes, then cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.

## Oven method

Bake the ramekins on a sheet pan at **325°F** for 22–30 minutes, using the same doneness cues.

## Make ahead and storage

- Make up to 2 days in advance for the best texture.
- Refrigerate covered for up to 4 days.
- Serve cold, or let the cups stand at room temperature for 10–15 minutes before eating.
