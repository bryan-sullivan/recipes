Cheese Crusted Flying Dutchman

A griddled Flying Dutchman-style burger with charred onion buns, cheese-crusted mozzarella, American cheese, and burger sauce.

Source: Nick Nesgoda from Instagram / Deglaze PDF export

Servings

4 servings

Ingredients

Main

  • 4 large yellow onion slices
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/4 pound burger balls
  • Burger seasoning
  • American cheese slices
  • 4 slices mozzarella cheese

Burger Sauce

  • 1 cup mayo
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp relish
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Slice the yellow onion into 4 large slices, keeping the shape of the slices together.
  2. Season the onion slices on both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Assemble the burger sauce by combining all sauce ingredients in a bowl and mixing thoroughly.
  4. On a hot griddle over medium-high heat, add the onion slices. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes per side until lightly charred.
  5. Add a splash of water, cover, and steam the onions for 3 to 4 minutes.
  6. Smash the 1/4 pound burger balls into thin patties with a burger press or spatula. Season with burger seasoning.
  7. Flip patties after 3 to 4 minutes, then add a slice of American cheese.
  8. Once onions are soft, place 4 slices of mozzarella directly on the griddle over medium heat.
  9. Place each onion on a slice of mozzarella. Remove after about 2 minutes, once the cheese is toasted and golden brown.
  10. Assemble with onion on the bottom, burger sauce, burger patties, more sauce, and an onion on top.

WW Notes

This is a higher-point recipe because of mayo, beef, American cheese, mozzarella, and sauce. For a lighter version, use 93/7 beef or ground turkey, light mayo, reduced-fat cheese, and a smaller amount of sauce.
