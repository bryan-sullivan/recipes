Mediterranean Sheet-Pan Salmon

Serves: 4 · Mediterranean

Salmon roasted with zucchini, pepper, onion, potatoes, olives, oregano and lemon.

Ingredients

Vegetables

  • 1 medium zucchini, cut into thick rounds
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 small red onion, cut into chunks
  • 8 ounces white or Yukon Gold potato, cut into thin half-rounds
  • 2 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried parsley
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano

Salmon

  • 4 skinless salmon fillets, 6 ounces each
  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • ¼ teaspoon sweet or smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¾ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ cup mixed pitted olives
  • Lemon wedges

Instructions

Cook

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a large rimmed sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Toss the vegetables with their oil and seasonings. Spread them out without crowding.
  3. Roast for 20 minutes.
  4. Coat and season the salmon.
  5. Move the partially cooked vegetables aside, add the salmon and scatter the olives over the pan.
  6. Roast for approximately 12–15 minutes, adjusting for fillet thickness. The USDA minimum for fish is 145°F.
  7. Serve with lemon wedges.

Notes

Nutrition

Approximately 380 calories and 36.5 grams protein per serving.
