Turkey Smashburgers with Vinegar Slaw

Serves: 4 · American

Thin, deeply browned turkey burgers with a mustard-free burger sauce, potato wedges and crisp slaw.

Ingredients

Burgers

  • 1½ pounds 93% lean ground turkey
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • 4 whole-wheat buns or sandwich thins
  • 4 slices reduced-fat cheese, optional
  • Lettuce, sliced onion and dill pickles

Mustard-free burger sauce

  • ½ cup nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon light mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon dill pickle brine
  • Black pepper

Vinegar slaw

  • 12–14 ounces shredded cabbage or slaw mix
  • 3 tablespoons red-wine vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • Black pepper

Potato wedges

  • 2 pounds Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder and paprika

Instructions

Prep

  1. Mix the slaw and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Stir together the burger sauce.
  3. Divide the turkey into eight loose 3-ounce balls. Do not compress them tightly.
  4. Toss the potatoes with oil and seasonings.

Cook

  1. Roast or air-fry the potato wedges at 425°F until browned and tender, approximately 30–40 minutes.
  2. Heat the Blackstone to medium-high and lightly oil it.
  3. Place the turkey portions on the griddle, cover with parchment and smash thin with a sturdy spatula or press.
  4. Season and cook until deeply browned. Flip once.
  5. Add cheese if using. Cook the turkey completely to 165°F.
  6. Stack two patties per bun and add sauce, lettuce, onion and pickles.

Notes

Estimated nutrition

Approximately 450–550 calories and 40–45 grams protein per serving, depending on bun, cheese and potato portions.
